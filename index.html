Your Trusted Partner for PECTAA 2025 & All BISE Punjab Boards

At Hira Science Academy, we understand that students across Punjab face different challenges while preparing for board exams. Whether you're appearing under BISE Lahore, BISE Gujranwala, BISE Multan, BISE Rawalpindi, BISE Faisalabad, BISE Sargodha, BISE DG Khan, BISE Bahawalpur, BISE Sahiwal, or the Federal Board – our notes are designed to meet the common core of the PECTAA 2025 syllabus while addressing the specific paper patterns of each board.

Our teaching methodology is rooted in the latest curriculum from the Punjab Education, Curriculum, Training and Assessment Authority (PECTAA). Every note, every solved example, and every MCQ set is crafted by our dual-specialist faculty: Muhammad Tayyab (MSc Mathematics) and Mirza Abid Shabbir Mughal (M.Phil Chemistry). Together, they bring over two decades of classroom experience to help students grasp concepts, not just memorize facts.

We break down complex topics into digestible sections. For Mathematics, we provide step-by-step solutions to all exercises, plus extra numerical problems. In Physics, we focus on conceptual clarity and problem-solving techniques. Chemistry notes include balanced equations, practical explanations, and board-pattern MCQs. Biology resources cover all chapters with detailed diagrams and glossary terms – all aligned with the 10 BISE Punjab boards' examination standards.

The PECTAA 2025 syllabus emphasizes deeper understanding over rote learning. That's why our notes include "exam smart" pointers, common mistake warnings, and pairing-scheme based revision. Every resource on this site is completely free for students across Punjab – from Lahore to Multan, Gujranwala to Bahawalpur, Rawalpindi to Sahiwal.
