# Our Approach

Your Trusted Partner for PECTAA & All BISE Punjab Boards

At Hira Science Academy, we provide educational resources, notes, solved examples, MCQs, and board-exam preparation support for students of Classes 9 and 10. Whether you're appearing under BISE Lahore, BISE Gujranwala, BISE Multan, BISE Rawalpindi, BISE Faisalabad, BISE Sargodha, BISE DG Khan, BISE Bahawalpur, BISE Sahiwal, or the Federal Board — our notes are designed to meet the common core of the PECTAA syllabus while addressing the specific paper patterns of each board.

Our teaching methodology is rooted in the latest curriculum from the Punjab Education, Curriculum, Training and Assessment Authority (PECTAA). Our notes, solved examples, and MCQ sets are prepared by our dual-specialist faculty: Muhammad Tayyab (MSc Mathematics) and Mirza Abid Shabbir Mughal (M.Phil Chemistry). Together, they bring classroom teaching experience to help students grasp concepts, not just memorize facts.

We break down complex topics into digestible sections. For Mathematics, we provide step-by-step solutions to all exercises, plus extra numerical problems. In Physics, we focus on conceptual clarity and problem-solving techniques. Chemistry notes include balanced equations, practical explanations, and board-pattern MCQs. Biology resources cover all chapters with detailed diagrams and glossary terms — all aligned with the BISE Punjab boards' and Federal Board's examination standards.

Beyond study notes, we also offer free online calculators and educational tools covering mathematics, algebra, geometry, GPA, physics formulas, and unit conversions. These tools pair perfectly with our Class 9 and Class 10 notes for homework checking and exam preparation.

🔍 Ready to achieve top marks in your BISE board exams? Start with our Class 9 or Class 10 notes below, or explore our free online tools.

# FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from students and parents about our Class 9 & 10 resources.

Which Punjab Boards does Hira Science Academy support?

We support all 9 BISE Punjab boards including Lahore, Gujranwala, Multan, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Sargodha, DG Khan, Bahawalpur, and Sahiwal, plus the Federal Board.

What is the PECTAA syllabus?

PECTAA stands for Punjab Education, Curriculum, Training and Assessment Authority. The updated syllabus emphasizes deeper understanding and concept-based learning over rote memorization.

Are the Class 10 notes updated for 2026?

Yes, the Class 10 notes are fully updated to the 2026 PECTAA syllabus. Class 9 notes cover the current syllabus as-is.

What subjects are available for Class 9?

We offer complete notes for Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Islamiat for Class 9.

What subjects are available for Class 10?

We offer complete notes for Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Pakistan Studies for Class 10, all updated to the 2026 syllabus.

How can I access the Student Portal?

You can access the Student Portal by clicking the 'Student Portal' button in the navigation bar or on the homepage. It provides resources for attendance, exam results, and academic progress.

Are the notes free to use?

Yes, all notes on Hira Science Academy are completely free for students across Punjab.

How are the notes different from other platforms?

Our notes are crafted by dual-specialist faculty (MSc Mathematics and M.Phil Chemistry) with classroom teaching experience. They focus on conceptual clarity, step-by-step solutions, and board-pattern MCQs — using an "error-first" approach that shows the common wrong method before the correct one.

Where can I download the 2027 pairing scheme?

The latest pairing scheme for Class 9 and Class 10 (all subjects) is available as a free PDF on our Pairing Scheme page, valid for the upcoming BISE Punjab board exams.
