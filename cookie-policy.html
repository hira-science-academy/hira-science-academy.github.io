How we use cookies to improve your experience on Hira Science Academy

Last updated: June 8, 2026

Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how Hira Science Academy ("we", "our", or "us") uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website https://hiraacademy.com.pk/. By using our website, you consent to the use of cookies as described in this policy.

We are committed to being transparent about our data practices and providing you with clear information about how and why we use cookies.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, phone, tablet) when you visit websites. They help us understand user behavior, improve site functionality, and deliver a better user experience.

📌 Cookies are safe — they cannot access your files or personal information on your device

  • Session Cookies: Temporary cookies that expire when you close your browser
  • Persistent Cookies: Cookies that remain on your device for a set period
  • First-party Cookies: Cookies set by our website directly
  • Third-party Cookies: Cookies set by external services we use

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Analytics: Understand how visitors interact with our website using Google Analytics
  • Performance: Monitor website performance and improve loading times
  • User Experience: Remember your preferences and improve navigation
  • Ad Targeting: Display relevant advertisements through Google AdSense

4. Third-Party Cookies

We allow trusted third-party services to use cookies on our website:

  • Google Analytics: For website traffic analysis and visitor behavior insights
  • Google AdSense: For displaying relevant advertisements
  • Social Media Platforms: For social sharing features (Facebook, Instagram)

These third parties may use cookies to collect information about your browsing activities. We do not control these cookies. For more information, please review the privacy policies of these services.

5. Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies in your browser settings. Here's how:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Site permissions → Cookies and site data

⚠️ Disabling cookies may affect your experience on our site — some features may not work properly

You can also opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The latest version will always be available on this page with the date of revision mentioned above.

We encourage you to review this policy regularly to stay informed about how we use cookies.

Frequently Asked Questions About Cookies

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by websites you visit. They help websites remember your preferences and improve your browsing experience.

Does Hira Science Academy use cookies?

Yes, we use cookies for analytics (Google Analytics), performance optimization, and ad targeting (Google AdSense).

Can I disable cookies?

Yes, you can disable or delete cookies in your browser settings. However, this may affect some features of our website.

Do you use third-party cookies?

Yes, we use third-party cookies from Google Analytics and Google AdSense to analyze traffic and display relevant ads.

How can I opt out of personalized ads?

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

How often is this policy updated?

We review and update this policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The latest revision date is shown at the top of this page.
