Govt. Christian High School Daska

A Proud Legacy of Excellence Since 1882

Established in 1882, Govt. Christian High School Daska is among the oldest educational institutions of the region. Originally founded as a Mission School during the British era, it aimed to provide modern education, English instruction, moral values, and disciplined academic training.

A historic milestone was achieved on 26 March 1915 when the foundation stone of Daska Mission High School was laid by E.R. Abbott Esq., Deputy Commissioner. This marked the formal recognition and strengthening of the institution.

After the independence of Pakistan in 1947, the school continued its mission of excellence and later became known as Government Christian High School Daska. The institution proudly holds EMIS Code: 34310007 and is located on Old Kachehri Road, Daska.

For more than 140 years, the school has produced countless successful professionals, leaders, and responsible citizens, maintaining a strong tradition of discipline, integrity, and academic achievement.

Historical Timeline

1882 – Establishment

The institution was founded in 1882 as a Mission School during British India. At a time when modern education was limited in the region, the school emerged as a center of structured academic learning, discipline, and moral instruction. It introduced organized classroom systems, English education, and a curriculum designed to uplift the intellectual standards of the local community.

Early 1900s – Expansion & Recognition

In the early twentieth century, the school experienced steady growth in enrollment and academic reputation. It became a respected institution in Daska and surrounding areas, attracting students from nearby towns and villages. The emphasis on discipline, character-building, and academic excellence strengthened its foundation.

26 March 1915 – Foundation Stone

A historic milestone was achieved when the foundation stone of Daska Mission High School was formally laid by E.R. Abbott Esq., Deputy Commissioner. This ceremony symbolized official recognition and administrative support, marking the transformation of the institution into a prominent high school of the region.

1947 – After Independence

Following the independence of Pakistan in 1947, the institution continued its noble mission under the new national framework. It maintained its academic traditions while adapting to the evolving educational system of Pakistan, eventually becoming known as Government Christian High School Daska.

Late 20th Century – Academic Strengthening

During the late twentieth century, the school strengthened its academic structure, faculty development, and examination performance. It gained recognition for producing outstanding results in board examinations and for nurturing responsible, well-disciplined citizens.

Present – Continuing Excellence

Today, Govt. Christian High School Daska stands as a proud symbol of heritage and excellence. With over 140 years of history, the institution continues to provide quality education up to the high school level. It remains committed to academic integrity, moral values, and preparing students to meet the challenges of the modern world with confidence and dignity.
