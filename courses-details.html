Frequently Asked Questions About Our Programs

Which programs do you offer?

We offer comprehensive coaching programs for Class 9, Class 10, and FSc (Pre-Medical & Pre-Engineering) students.

What is the fee structure?

Our program fee is PKR 10,000 per month for both Class 9 and Class 10 programs. FSc entry test preparation fees vary — please contact us for details.

What is the class schedule?

Classes are held Monday to Friday from 3:00 PM to 6:00 PM.

How large are the batches?

We maintain small batch sizes of up to 20 students to ensure personalized attention for every student.

Do you offer entry test preparation?

Yes, we offer specialized entry test preparation programs for medical (MDCAT) and engineering (ECAT) entrance exams.

How can I enroll?

You can enroll by clicking the "Enroll Now" button on this page or by contacting us through our Contact page.
