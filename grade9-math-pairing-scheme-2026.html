Examination Overview

Total Marks & Timing

  • Total Marks: 75
  • Objective Type: 15 marks
  • Subjective Type: 60 marks
  • Total Time: 2 hours 30 minutes
  • Objective Time: 20 minutes
  • Subjective Time: 2 hours 10 minutes

Paper Structure

  • Question 1: 15 MCQs (1 mark each)
  • Part-I (Q2-Q4): Short Questions (12 marks each)
  • Part-II (Q5-Q7): Long Questions (8 marks each)
  • Part-III (Q8-Q9): Long Questions (8 marks each)

Objective Type (MCQs)

Question 1: 15 Multiple Choice Questions (1 × 15 = 15 marks)

Questions will be selected from the entire textbook with the following distribution:

Chapters Number of MCQs
1 Real Numbers 1
2 Logarithms 1
3 Sets and Functions 2
4 Factorization 2
5 Linear Equations 1
6 Trigonometry 2
7 Coordinate Geometry 1
9 Similar Figures 1
10 Graphs of Functions 1
11 Loci and Construction 1
12 Information Handling 1
13 Probability 1

Part-I: Subjective Type (Short Questions)

Question 2: Attempt any 6 out of 9 questions (2 × 6 = 12 marks)

Questions will be selected from the following chapters:

Chapters with 2 questions each:

  • 1 Real Numbers
  • 2 Logarithms
  • 3 Sets and Functions
  • 4 Factorization

Chapter with 1 question:

  • 5 Linear Equations

Question 3: Attempt any 6 out of 9 questions (2 × 6 = 12 marks)

Questions will be selected from the following chapters:

Chapters with 3 questions each:

6 Trigonometry
7 Coordinate Geometry
9 Similar Figures

Question 4: Attempt any 6 out of 9 questions (2 × 6 = 12 marks)

Questions will be selected from the following chapters:

Chapters with 2 questions each:

  • 10 Graphs of Functions
  • 11 Loci and Construction
  • 13 Probability

Chapter with 3 questions:

  • 12 Information Handling
