Grade 9 Mathematics Pairing Scheme 2026

This official pairing scheme outlines the complete structure of the Annual Examination 2026 for Grade 9 Mathematics, including marks distribution, question types, and chapter-wise allocation.

Note: Total marks for the paper: 75 marks. Time allocation: 2:30 hours. Objective Type: 20 minutes, Subjective Type: 2:10 hours.

Grade 9 Math Pairing Scheme 2026

Paper Structure Overview

Total Marks: 75

Objective: 15 marks + Subjective: 60 marks

Total Time: 2:30 hours

Objective: 20 min + Subjective: 2:10 hours

Objective Type (MCQs) - 15 Marks

Question Description Marks
Q-1 15 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) from entire textbook 1 × 15 = 15
Distribution: One MCQ each from chapters 1, 2, 5, 7, 9, 10, 11, 12, and 13. Two MCQs each from chapters 3, 4 & 6.

Subjective Type - 60 Marks

Part-I: Short Questions (12 + 12 + 12 = 36 Marks)
Q-2: Attempt any 6 out of 9 questions (2 × 6 = 12 marks)

Two short questions from each of chapters 1, 2, 3 and 4, and one from chapter 5.

Q-3: Attempt any 6 out of 9 questions (2 × 6 = 12 marks)

Three short questions from each of chapters 6, 7 and 9.

Q-4: Attempt any 6 out of 9 questions (2 × 6 = 12 marks)

Two short questions from each of chapters 10, 11, and 13, and three from chapter 12.

Part-II: Long Questions (Attempt 2 out of 3 questions) - 16 Marks
Question Chapter Parts Marks
Q-5 Chapter 1 & 2 (a) From Chapter 1, (b) From Chapter 2 8 (4+4)
Q-6 Chapter 3 & 4 (a) From Chapter 3, (b) From Chapter 4 8 (4+4)
Q-7 Chapter 5 & 6 (a) From Chapter 5, (b) From Chapter 6 8 (4+4)
Students attempt any 2 questions out of Q-5, Q-6, Q-7 (2 × 8 = 16 marks)
Part-III: Long Questions (Attempt 1 out of 2 questions) - 8 Marks
Question Chapter Parts Marks
Q-8 Mixed Chapters (a) From Chapter 7 or 10, (b) From Chapter 9 8 (4+4)
Q-9 Mixed Chapters (a) From Chapter 12, (b) From Chapter 11 or 13 8 (4+4)
Students attempt any 1 question out of Q-8, Q-9 (1 × 8 = 8 marks)

Preparation Strategy

Focus on High-Weightage Chapters

Chapters 3, 4, 6, 7, 9, and 12 carry more marks. Allocate study time accordingly.

Practice Selective Attempts

In subjective parts, you can attempt only required questions. Choose your strongest areas.

Time Management

Allocate only 20 minutes for MCQs. Use remaining time strategically for subjective questions.
