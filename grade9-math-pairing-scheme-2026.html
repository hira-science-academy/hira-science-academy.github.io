Preparation Tips & Strategies

Smart Preparation Strategy

📚 Focus on High-Weightage Chapters

Prioritize chapters with higher marks distribution:

  • Chapter 6: Trigonometry (12 marks)
  • Chapter 3: Sets and Functions (10 marks)
  • Chapter 4: Factorization (10 marks)
  • Chapter 7: Coordinate Geometry (11 marks)
  • Chapter 9: Similar Figures (11 marks)
  • Chapter 12: Information Handling (11 marks)

⏱️ Time Management

  • MCQs: Complete within 20 minutes
  • Short Questions: Allocate 1.5 hours
  • Long Questions: Allocate 40 minutes
  • Revision: Keep 10 minutes for review

🎯 Question Selection Strategy

  • Attempt all MCQs first
  • Choose easier short questions from each part
  • Select long questions from your strongest chapters
  • Attempt all parts of selected long questions

📝 Practice Tips

  • Practice previous year papers
  • Focus on textbook exercises
  • Time yourself during practice
  • Revise formulas regularly
  • Practice diagram-based questions

⚠️ Important Note

This pairing scheme is based on the SMART syllabus for the Annual Examination 2026. All questions will be asked from the textbook exercises. Make sure to practice all examples and exercises from your textbook thoroughly.
