Frequently asked questions about solved exercises

Are all textbook exercises solved on this page?

Yes, every exercise across all 12 chapters of Class 10 Mathematics is solved step by step, following the PECTAA 2026 syllabus.

Are review exercises included as well?

Yes, each chapter's review exercise is solved in full alongside its regular exercises.

Do the solutions show full working, or just final answers?

Every solution shows the complete step-by-step working, not just the final answer, so you can follow the method used.

Can I use these solved exercises to check my homework?

Yes, these solutions are designed for exactly that — attempt the exercise yourself first, then compare your working against the solved version.

Are these exercises aligned with the PECTAA 2026 syllabus?

Yes, the exercise numbering and content match the official PECTAA 2026 Class 10 Mathematics textbook, based on the Revised National Curriculum 2023.

Which BISE boards can use these solved exercises?

All 9 BISE boards in Punjab follow PECTAA 2026, so these solved exercises apply equally to every board.

Is this resource free to use?

Yes, all solved exercises are completely free to access online, with no sign-up required.

How should I use these solved exercises for exam prep?

Attempt each exercise first, check your working against the solution, and revisit any exercise where your method differed before the board exam.
