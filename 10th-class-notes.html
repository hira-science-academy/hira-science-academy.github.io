How to Prepare for the Class 10 Board Exam

A Simple Study Routine

  1. Read the textbook topic first, then use the notes to check your understanding.
  2. Learn definitions and formulas daily. They feed MCQs and short questions.
  3. Solve exercises yourself before opening the solved version.
  4. Practise MCQs after each chapter and list the topics you missed.
  5. Check the pairing scheme and model paper from your board for the paper pattern.
  6. Attempt past papers under time limits in the last weeks.

Mistakes That Cost Marks

  • Skipping steps: examiners award marks for method.
  • Missing units: write units with every Physics and Chemistry numerical answer.
  • Sign errors: recheck negative signs in Mathematics.
  • Unlabelled diagrams: practise labelled diagrams where the question asks for them.
  • Not reading the question twice: underline what is asked before you start.
  • Relying on unofficial syllabus PDFs: confirm the syllabus on PECTAA or your board's website.

Frequently Asked Questions About Class 10 Notes

Are these notes for the Punjab Board?

Yes. The notes follow the Punjab textbooks used by the nine BISE Punjab boards: Lahore, Gujranwala, Faisalabad, Rawalpindi, Multan, Sargodha, Bahawalpur, DG Khan and Sahiwal. The Federal Board (FBISE) is a separate board with its own papers, so if you study under FBISE, check your own textbook and scheme of studies.

Which Class 10 subjects are available?

Notes are available for Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry and Pakistan Studies. Biology notes are still being prepared. English, Urdu and Computer Science notes are not available on Hira Science Academy.

Which syllabus and session do these notes follow?

This page covers the 2026-27 Class 10 session, with annual exams in 2027. The Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry and Pakistan Studies pages follow the revised PECTAA textbooks based on the National Curriculum 2023. Hira Science Academy is not a government body, so confirm the syllabus on the PECTAA website or your board's notifications.

Are the notes free?

Yes. Notes and textbook pages can be opened for free without an account. Some interactive MCQ tests may ask you to register before you start.

Where can I download Class 10 notes as PDF?

Class 10 Mathematics and Class 10 Physics each have a dedicated notes PDF page, and Class 10 Pakistan Studies has PDF files for short questions and MCQs in each chapter. For Chemistry, open the subject page to see what is available.

Are the notes chapter-wise, and do they include solved exercises?

Yes. Mathematics has all 12 units with exercise-wise solutions. Physics has a chapter page for each of its 12 chapters with short questions, long questions, numerical problems and MCQs. Chemistry has 13 chapters with MCQs, short answer, constructed response and descriptive questions. Pakistan Studies has 8 chapters with short questions and MCQs.

Are MCQs available for Class 10?

Yes. Class 10 Mathematics and Class 10 Physics each have an MCQ page and a practice test, Chemistry has chapter-wise MCQs, and Pakistan Studies has MCQ files for each chapter.

Where can I find the official Class 10 syllabus and pairing scheme?

The official syllabus, pairing schemes and model papers are issued by PECTAA and your Punjab board, so confirm them on the PECTAA website or your board's notifications page. The pairing scheme guide on Hira Science Academy is a study summary, not an official document.

Who prepares these notes?

The Chemistry notes are published by Hira Science Academy, which was founded by Muhammad Tayyab and Mirza Abid Shabbir Mughal (M.Phil Chemistry).
