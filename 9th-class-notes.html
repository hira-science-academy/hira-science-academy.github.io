How to Prepare for the Class 9 Board Exam

A Simple Study Routine

  1. Read the textbook topic first, then use the notes to check your understanding.
  2. Learn definitions and formulas daily. They are the base for MCQs and short questions.
  3. Solve exercises yourself before looking at the solved version, at least 5 problems per exercise.
  4. Practise MCQs after each chapter and note the topics you got wrong.
  5. Check the pairing scheme and model paper from your board to see the paper pattern.
  6. Attempt past papers under time limits in the last weeks before the exam.

Mistakes That Cost Marks

  • Skipping steps: examiners award marks for method, so show your working.
  • Missing units: write units with every Physics and Chemistry numerical answer.
  • Sign errors: recheck negative signs in Mathematics.
  • Unlabelled diagrams: practise labelled diagrams for Biology and Physics.
  • Not reading the question twice: underline what is asked before you start.
  • Using unofficial "smart syllabus" PDFs: confirm the syllabus on PECTAA or your board's website.

Frequently Asked Questions About Class 9 Notes

Are these notes for the Punjab Board?

Yes. The notes follow the Punjab textbooks used by the nine BISE Punjab boards: Lahore, Gujranwala, Faisalabad, Rawalpindi, Multan, Sargodha, Bahawalpur, DG Khan and Sahiwal. The Federal Board (FBISE) is a separate board with its own papers, so if you study under FBISE, check your own textbook and scheme of studies.

Which Class 9 subjects are available?

Notes are available for Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Islamiat. English, Urdu and Computer Science notes are not yet available on Hira Science Academy.

Are the notes free?

Yes. Notes and textbook pages can be opened for free without an account. Some interactive MCQ tests may ask you to register before you start.

Where can I download Class 9 notes as PDF?

Class 9 Mathematics and Class 9 Physics each have a dedicated notes PDF page, linked in the subject table on this page. For Chemistry, Biology and Islamiat, open the subject page to see which downloads are currently available.

Are the notes chapter-wise, and do they include solved exercises?

Class 9 Mathematics is organised chapter by chapter, with exercise-wise solved pages, definitions and MCQs for each chapter. For the other subjects, open the subject page to see what is available chapter by chapter.

Are MCQs available for Class 9?

Yes. Class 9 Mathematics has chapter-wise MCQs and a 100-question practice test, and Class 9 Physics has an interactive MCQ test. For other subjects, check the subject page.

Where can I find the official syllabus, smart syllabus and pairing scheme?

The official syllabus, smart syllabus and pairing schemes are issued by PECTAA and your Punjab board, so confirm them on the PECTAA website or your board's notifications page. Hira Science Academy provides study summaries such as the Class 9 Mathematics smart syllabus page and the pairing scheme pages, but these are not official documents.

Which academic session does this page cover?

This page is prepared for the 2026-27 Class 9 session in Punjab, with annual exams in 2027. Exam dates can change, so check your board's date sheet.

Are English and Urdu medium resources available?

The notes are written in English, and the Mathematics notes add Urdu terms where needed. This page does not offer separate Urdu-medium notes.

Who prepares these notes, and how do I know they are current?

The notes are prepared by Muhammad Tayyab (MSc Mathematics, Subject Specialist at Govt Christian High School Daska) and Mirza Abid Shabbir Mughal (M.Phil Chemistry). The last reviewed date near the top of this page shows when the page was last checked against current syllabus information.
