SMART Syllabus / Accelerated Learning Program (ALP) — Grade 9 Physics (Session 2025-26)

Note: All types of questions for BISE Examination paper will be prepared from the entire content of the textbook other than those topics and questions which have been excluded / deleted in the Smart Syllabus (ALP) for Physics-9 (Session 2025-26).

Note: This syllabus applies only to the Annual Examination 2026. All excluded content listed below will not appear in board exams.
