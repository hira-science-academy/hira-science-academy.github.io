Class 10 Physics Notes (New 2026 PECTAA Edition)

Download the complete 10th Class Physics Notes (2026 Edition) for all Punjab Boards (BISE Punjab), prepared according to the Punjab Education Curriculum Training and Assessment Authority (PECTAA) syllabus based on the National Curriculum 2023. These chapter-wise notes are carefully written by Muhammad Tayyab, Subject Specialist Physics at Govt. Christian High School Daska to help students understand every concept clearly and perform well in board examinations.

This free study resource includes Chapter Notes, Exercise Short Questions, Additional Short Questions, Constructed Response Questions (CRQs), Long Questions, Solved Numerical Problems, Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs), and Exam Preparation Material. Every chapter is explained in simple language following the latest PECTAA 2026 Physics textbook.

The notes cover all 12 chapters, including Thermal Physics, Transfer of Thermal Energy, Waves, Sound, Light, Electrostatics, Electricity, Electromagnetism, Electromagnetic Induction, Electronics, Atomic & Nuclear Physics, and Space & Environment. Whether you are preparing for the annual board examination, school tests, or revision, this page is your complete hub for 10th Class Physics Notes, the Physics Key Book, downloadable PDFs, MCQs, formulas, definitions, important questions, a guess paper, and past papers — all in one place.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the PECTAA syllabus for Class 10 Physics?

The PECTAA syllabus for Class 10 Physics (2026) is based on the National Curriculum 2023. It comprises 12 chapters covering Thermal Physics, Waves, Sound, Light, Electrostatics, Electricity, Electromagnetism, Electronics, Atomic & Nuclear Physics, and Space & Environment.

Who prepared these Class 10 Physics notes?

These notes are prepared by Muhammad Tayyab, Subject Specialist Physics at Govt Christian High School Daska, and designed to align with the latest PECTAA syllabus.

Do these notes include solved numerical problems?

Yes, each chapter includes a dedicated section for numerical problems with step-by-step solutions, and the full set is also organised on the Solved Exercises page.

Is there a separate Physics Key Book for Class 10?

Yes. The 10th Class Physics Key Book brings together chapter-wise solved exercises, numericals, definitions and MCQs in one place.

Can I download the Physics notes as a PDF?

Yes. The 10th Class Physics Notes PDF page offers chapter-wise notes you can read online or save for offline revision.

Which boards are these notes applicable for?

These notes are applicable for all BISE Punjab boards including Lahore, Gujranwala, Faisalabad, Multan, Rawalpindi, Bahawalpur, Sargodha, Dera Ghazi Khan, Sahiwal, and the Federal Board.

Where can I find a Physics guess paper for the 2026 exams?

The 10th Class Physics Guess Paper page highlights the most important chapters, numericals and questions based on recent board exam trends.
