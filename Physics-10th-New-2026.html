Class 10 Physics (New 2026) – PECTAA Edition

Access the complete Class 10 Physics Notes (2026 Edition) developed by Punjab Education Curriculum Training and Assessment Authority (PECTAA) based on the National Curriculum 2023. Prepared by Muhammad Tayyab, Subject Specialist Physics at Govt Christian High School Daska, these notes cover all 12 chapters with Short Questions, Exercise Short Questions, Constructed Response Questions, Long Questions, Numerical Problems, and MCQs.

Topics include Thermal Physics, Transfer of Thermal Energy, Waves, Sound, Light, Electrostatics, Electricity, Electromagnetism, Electromagnetic Induction, Electronics, Atomic & Nuclear Physics, and Space & Environment.
