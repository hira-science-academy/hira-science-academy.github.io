Class 9 Math MCQs According to Smart Syllabus

These Class 9 Mathematics MCQs are organized according to the revised PECTAA Smart Syllabus 2026. The questions cover the examinable topics of 9th class Mathematics, so students can practise what matters for their Punjab Board BISE examinations.

Practise chapter by chapter, or attempt the complete 9th Class Maths online test with all 100 MCQs. Every question has four options, shows instant feedback with the correct answer, and your result includes a score, performance analysis and review of wrong answers.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many Class 9 Mathematics MCQs are available on this page?▼

This page has 100 Class 9 Mathematics MCQs with answers, arranged in 12 chapters according to the PECTAA Smart Syllabus 2026.

Are these MCQs based on the PECTAA Smart Syllabus?▼

Yes. The question bank is organized according to the revised PECTAA Smart Syllabus for Class 9 Mathematics and covers the examinable topics included in it.

Can I practice 9th class math MCQs chapter-wise?▼

Yes. After registration you can choose any single chapter from the dropdown menu, or select the complete test to attempt all MCQs together.

Is registration compulsory before starting the test?▼

Yes. Students must enter their name, a valid email address, school name and contact number (roll number is optional) before the MCQ tests unlock.

Do I get instant feedback and the correct answer?▼

Yes. As soon as you choose an option, the page shows whether it is correct or wrong and highlights the right answer. Explanations are shown where available.

Is there a timer, and can I pause the test?▼

Yes. A timer tracks your time, and you can pause and resume the test. You can also mark questions for review and jump to any question using the question-number grid.

Can I review my wrong answers and download a certificate?▼

Yes. After submitting, you see your score, percentage, performance analysis and a review of wrong or skipped questions. You can also download a PDF certificate with your result.

Is this online test suitable for Punjab Board exam preparation?▼

These MCQs are designed to help Class 9 students prepare for Punjab Board BISE examinations. Use them alongside your textbook, notes and the pairing scheme.
