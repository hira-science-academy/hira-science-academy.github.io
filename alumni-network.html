Frequently Asked Questions About Alumni Network

Who can join the alumni network?

All former students of Hira Science Academy are welcome to join our alumni network, regardless of when they studied or which field they are in.

How can I share my success story?

You can share your story by contacting us at tayyabgchs@gmail.com with your testimonial, photo, and achievements.

Can alumni mentor current students?

Yes, we encourage alumni to mentor current students through career guidance, study tips, and sharing their experiences.

Are there any alumni events?

We are planning alumni meetups and events. Stay tuned for announcements on our website and social media channels.

How can I stay connected with other alumni?

You can stay connected through our alumni network page, social media channels, and upcoming alumni events. We will also be creating an alumni directory in the future.

Will my story be shared publicly?

We will only share your story with your permission. You can choose to remain anonymous or have your name and achievements featured.
