Class 10 Pakistan Studies Notes (New 2026 PECTAA Edition)

Download the complete 10th Class Pakistan Studies Notes (2026 Edition) for all Punjab Boards (BISE Punjab), prepared according to the Punjab Education Curriculum Training and Assessment Authority (PECTAA) syllabus based on the National Curriculum 2023. These chapter-wise notes are carefully written by Hira Science Academy to help students understand every concept clearly and perform well in board examinations.

This free study resource includes Chapter Notes, Exercise Short Questions, Additional Short Questions, Constructed Response Questions (CRQs), Long Questions, Solved Numerical Problems, Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs), and Exam Preparation Material. The notes cover all 8 chapters, including Pakistan's Ideological Foundation, Pakistan Movement, History of Pakistan (1971 onwards), Pakistan and World Affairs, Land and Environment, Population and Culture, Economic Development, and Women's Empowerment. Whether you are preparing for the annual board examination, school tests, or revision, this page is your complete hub for 10th Class Pakistan Studies Notes, the Key Book, downloadable PDFs, MCQs, definitions, important questions, a guess paper, and past papers — all in one place.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the PECTAA syllabus for Class 10 Pakistan Studies?

The PECTAA syllabus for Class 10 Pakistan Studies (2026) is based on the National Curriculum 2023. It comprises 8 chapters covering Pakistan's ideological foundation, Pakistan Movement, history (1971 onwards), foreign relations, geography, population and culture, economic development, and women's empowerment.

Who prepared these Class 10 Pakistan Studies notes?

These notes are prepared by Hira Science Academy, and designed to align with the latest PECTAA syllabus.

Do these notes include solved exercises?

Yes, each chapter includes a dedicated section for solved exercises, short questions, and MCQs.

Is there a separate Pakistan Studies Key Book for Class 10?

Yes. The 10th Class Pakistan Studies Key Book brings together chapter-wise solved exercises, definitions and MCQs in one place.

Can I download the Pakistan Studies notes as a PDF?

Yes. The 10th Class Pakistan Studies Notes PDF page offers chapter-wise notes you can read online or save for offline revision.

These notes are applicable for all BISE Punjab boards including Lahore, Gujranwala, Faisalabad, Multan, Rawalpindi, Bahawalpur, Sargodha, Dera Ghazi Khan, Sahiwal, and the Federal Board.
