📘 How to Prepare for 10th General Science Board Exam 2027

The Class 10 General Science Pairing Scheme 2027 is designed to help students streamline their preparation for the Punjab Board examinations. Under the new PECTAA syllabus, the paper focuses on Chapters 7 to 11. By analyzing this scheme, you can adopt a targeted approach and focus only on the high-yield topics. The total paper is worth 75 marks, split into a 15-mark objective section and a 60-mark subjective section.

Start with the MCQs distribution. The paper has 15 MCQs, and the distribution is perfectly balanced: exactly 3 MCQs come from each of the five chapters (7, 8, 9, 10, and 11). Mastering these chapters is crucial to securing a strong start.

For short questions, the structure has changed. Part-I consists of three questions (Q.2, Q.3, Q.4), each containing 9 short questions. You must attempt 6 out of 9 from each group, earning 2 marks per question (12 marks per section, 36 marks total). Q.2 covers Chapters 7 and 11, Q.3 covers Chapter 8 exclusively, and Q.4 covers Chapters 9 and 10. This gives you strategic flexibility to skip the toughest questions.

Long questions (Part-II) are the most scoring section. There are five long questions (Q.5 to Q.9), with one question from each chapter. You must attempt any 3 out of 5, and each carries 8 marks for a total of 24 marks. Since every chapter has a long question, no chapter can be ignored for the subjective part. Download the official PDF, review the chapter-wise breakdown, and structure your study plan accordingly to secure maximum marks.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions – 10th General Science Pairing Scheme

What is the paper pattern for Class 10 General Science 2027 under the new syllabus? ▼
The 2027 Class 10 General Science paper is 75 marks. It consists of an Objective part (15 MCQs = 15 marks) and a Subjective part (60 marks). The subjective part has three short question sections (Q.2, Q.3, Q.4) where you attempt 6 of 9 questions each (12 marks per section), and a long question section where you attempt 3 of 5 questions (8 marks each = 24 marks). Timing is 2 hours 30 minutes.
How many MCQs come from each chapter in 10th General Science 2027? ▼
There are 15 MCQs in total. The distribution is 3 MCQs from each chapter (Chapters 7, 8, 9, 10, and 11).
Is this pairing scheme valid for all Punjab Boards? ▼
Yes, this scheme is based on the new 2027 PECTAA curriculum followed by all 9 BISE Punjab boards: Lahore, Gujranwala, Multan, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Sargodha, DG Khan, Bahawalpur, and Sahiwal.
