10th Class Physics Important Questions (2026 PECTAA Edition)

This page collects the 10th Class Physics Important Questions most likely to appear in your 2026 board exam, organised chapter by chapter for the PECTAA (National Curriculum 2023) syllabus. It is prepared by Muhammad Tayyab, Subject Specialist Physics at Govt. Christian High School Daska, based on patterns seen across recent BISE Punjab board papers.

Rather than covering every possible question, this list is filtered for relevance: definitions and laws that examiners test repeatedly, short questions that appear across multiple boards, long questions with a consistent structure, and numericals built on the most commonly used formulas. It is ideal for focused revision in the final weeks before your exam.

For complete concept coverage, start with the 10th Class Physics Notes. Once you have studied a chapter fully, come back here to prioritise the questions worth memorising first.

🎯 Board Preparation With Important Questions

Board exams reward focused, well-organised revision. Rather than re-reading an entire chapter the night before an exam, use this important-questions list to prioritise the concepts that carry the most marks and appear most consistently across boards and past sessions.

📝 Short Questions

Definition-based and concept-check questions that appear in the objective and short-answer sections of the paper.

📖 Long Questions

Descriptive questions and derivations that carry the highest marks per question in the board paper.

📐 Numericals

With limited time before an exam, prioritisation matters more than coverage. Use this order to get the most marks per hour of revision.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as an "important question" in Physics?

An important question is one that appears frequently across recent BISE Punjab board papers, or that covers a concept examiners consistently test.

Are both short and long questions included?

Yes. This page organises short conceptual questions, long descriptive questions, and important numericals separately for each chapter.

How are "most repeated" questions identified?

By reviewing recent years' BISE Punjab Physics papers and noting which questions recur most often across boards and sessions.

Should I only study the important questions before an exam?

They are best for focused, time-limited revision. For full preparation, combine them with the complete Physics Notes and Solved Exercises.

Do important questions replace a guess paper?

No. Important questions are a broader revision list, while the Guess Paper is a focused prediction based on recent trends. The two work well together.
