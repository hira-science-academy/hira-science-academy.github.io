📋 Overall Paper Pattern & Distribution

The Physics 12th Class paper for 2027 will consist of 85 marks, with a total time of 3 hours. The paper is divided into three parts: Objective (Part-I), Short Answer Questions (Part-II), and Detailed Questions (Part-III).

Part / Question(s) Description Marks
Part-I: Q-1Objective: 17 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) from Chapters 13 to 21.17
Part-II: Q-2 to Q-4Subjective: Short answer questions. Attempt 8 of 12 in Q-2, 8 of 12 in Q-3, and 6 of 9 in Q-4.44
Part-III: Q-5 to Q-9Subjective: Five detailed questions. Attempt any three. Each question has a long/theoretical part and a numerical part.24
Total Marks85

Part-I: Objective (MCQs)

Q.1 will contain 17 MCQs from the entire syllabus. The chapter-wise distribution is as follows:

Question Chapters Included Distribution
Q.1Chapters 13 to 212 MCQs each from Ch 13 to 20, and 1 MCQ from Ch 21

Part-II: Subjective — Short Answer Questions

This section contains three questions (Q.2, Q.3, Q.4) with a total of 12, 12, and 9 parts respectively. Students must attempt 8, 8, and 6 parts respectively. Each part carries 2 marks.

Short Questions Chapters Included Total Parts Distribution Attempt / Marks
Q.2Chapters 13, 14, 15124 + 4 + 48 parts = 16 Marks
Q.3Chapters 16, 17, 18124 + 4 + 48 parts = 16 Marks
Q.4Chapters 19, 20, 2193 + 3 + 36 parts = 12 Marks

Part-III: Subjective — Detailed Questions / Numericals

This section contains five detailed questions (Q.5 to Q.9). Students must attempt any three. Each question carries 8 marks and is divided into two parts: (a) a long/theoretical question (5 marks) and (b) a numerical (3 marks).

Question Chapter(s) Included Part Pattern Marks

Q.7Chapter 17Long question + Numerical5 + 3
Q.8Chapter 18 + Chapter 19Long question + Numerical5 + 3
