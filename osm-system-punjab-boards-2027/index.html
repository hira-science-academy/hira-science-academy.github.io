On-Screen Marking (OSM) is no longer only a plan in Punjab. As of 8 November 2026, official pages of BISE DG Khan, BISE Multan, BISE Lahore and BISE Sargodha show OSM being prepared or piloted for Computer Science in the HSSC (Intermediate) Second Annual Examination 2026, which began on 3 November. BISE Lahore is also reported to include Chemistry, Biology and Statistics.

OSM is not an online exam. Students still write with a pen on a printed paper answer copy (an “e-sheet”). After the exam the copy is scanned and an examiner marks the scanned pages on a computer screen. The change is in how papers are checked, not in how students attempt them.

What is not confirmed: OSM for Matric (9th/10th) papers, OSM for all subjects, or a notice that covers all nine boards. Three boards (Faisalabad, Bahawalpur, Sahiwal) have no specific official announcement that we could verify. The “2027” in many headlines refers to a wider expansion mentioned by the Punjab Education Minister; a confirmed 2027 subject list has not been published.

  • BISE Multan: notification on OSM for HSSC Part-I Computer Science, an OSM tender notice, and a Computer Science specimen answer sheet (posted 28 Oct 2026).
  • BISE Lahore: a complete answer book and bubble sheet pattern for Computer Science (OSM) on its notifications page.
  • BISE Sargodha: notice that the PBCC (meeting of 13-06-2026, item 04) decided to pilot OSM for Computer Science (HSSC Part-1) in the 2nd Annual Exam 2026.

📰 Reported by media, not found on a board page

  • BISE Lahore letter No. 512-RCO/BISE LHR (7 Oct 2026): four Intermediate subjects (Chemistry, Biology, Computer Science, Statistics) and training workshops 12–31 Oct 2026. We could not find the letter itself in the board's public notification list.
  • BISE Rawalpindi (press release, 28 Oct 2026): digital marking system with e-sheets and QR-code attendance.
  • BISE Gujranwala: an illustrated e-sheet filling guide published for students (also reported by ProPakistani on 5 Nov 2026); we have not seen it on the board's website.
  • Education Minister: statement that on-screen assessment will replace manual checking from 2027.

⚠️ Not confirmed

  • OSM for Matric (9th/10th) papers.
  • OSM for all subjects or all nine boards in one notice.
  • AI-based marking by any Punjab board.
  • Changes to rechecking or re-evaluation rules for OSM papers.
  • Whether scanned copies will be shown to students.

Scope also differs between sources: the PBCC decision refers to Computer Science Part-1, while BISE DG Khan names Part 1 and Part 2. Check your own board's notice.

📖 What is the OSM System in Punjab Boards 2027?

OSM stands for On-Screen Marking.

In simple terms, OSM is a digital evaluation system where answer scripts are scanned and examiners mark them on computer screens instead of physically checking paper copies.

📝 Traditional Process

Student → Paper answer copy → Bundles sent to a marking centre → Examiner marks the paper copy

💻 OSM Process

Student → Paper answer copy with barcode/QR → Board scans it → Examiner marks the scan on screen

How digital marking works

  1. The board prints answer copies with a barcode/QR code on each page and fixed answer areas.
  2. The student attempts the paper by hand inside the printed spaces.
  3. The copy is collected, scanned and linked to the candidate by its codes.
  4. The examiner logs in, views the scanned pages and enters marks against each question.
  5. Marks are totalled and checked by the board's system and staff.

Online exam vs. OSM exam

PointOnline examOSM exam
How the student answersOn a computer or deviceWith a pen on a printed answer copy
Where the exam is heldOnline or in a computer labNormal examination centre
What changesThe way the paper is attemptedThe way the paper is checked
Who gives marksOften an automatic systemA human examiner on a screen

Important: OSM does not mean a computer checks everything automatically. The examiner views the scanned answer copy on screen and assigns marks.

📝 Punjab Board E-Sheet Filling Rules: Summary

These points come from the candidate instructions (“SOPs for Candidates”) published on the BISE DG Khan website under the heading BISE Punjab – Instructions for Students, dated 7 November 2026. Other boards may print slightly different rules.

✓

1. Check the copy before you write

All pages present, in order and untorn; barcode and QR code clear on every page. If not, tell the invigilator and get a new copy.

✓

2. Write details only in the title-page boxes

Write your roll number and other required details only in the boxes on the title page. Do not write your name or roll number anywhere else.

✓

3. Protect the barcode and QR code

Do not write, draw, tick, underline or scratch on them, and do not cover them with ink, whitener, tape or stickers.

✓

4. Use the permitted pen

The DG Khan SOP says blue or black ink pen only; no red or green ink, highlighters, markers or smudging gel pens. The BISE Gujranwala student guide says blue or black ink pen and rules out markers, highlighters, gel pens and pencils. Follow your own copy.

✓

5. Fill each MCQ bubble fully

Fill exactly one bubble completely and darkly. Ticks, crosses, circles, dots or half-filled bubbles are not accepted, and an MCQ answer cannot be changed once filled.

✓

6. Stay inside the margins and answer spaces

Writing outside the margins or on the top and bottom edges will not be scanned or marked. Answer each question in its own designated area.

✓

7. No whitener, no extra sheet

Cancel a wrong written answer with one straight line. No extra or supplementary sheet will be attached, so plan your answers to fit.

✓

8. Rough work only where marked

Use only the space marked for rough work. Do not press hard, because ink showing through can make the other side hard to read after scanning.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

1Is OSM confirmed for Punjab boards? +

Partly. As of 8 November 2026, official pages of BISE DG Khan, BISE Multan, BISE Lahore and BISE Sargodha show On-Screen Marking being prepared or piloted for Computer Science in the HSSC Second Annual Examination 2026. A single notice covering all nine boards or all subjects has not been verified.

2Does OSM apply to all subjects and all nine Punjab boards? +

No. Confirmed material mainly covers Computer Science at Intermediate level. BISE Lahore is reported to include Chemistry, Biology and Statistics as well. For BISE Faisalabad, Bahawalpur and Sahiwal, no specific official implementation announcement was verified.

3Is an OSM exam an online exam? +

No. Students still sit a paper exam and write with a pen on a printed answer copy. After the exam the copy is scanned and the examiner marks the scanned pages on a computer screen.

4Will a computer check my answers? +

No verified notice says so. Under OSM, human examiners assign marks on screen. Plans to use AI for checking have been mentioned in the media, but no official Punjab board notice confirming AI marking was found.

5Does OSM apply to Matric (9th and 10th) papers? +

No official OSM notice for Matric (SSC) papers in Punjab was verified. The notices found concern Intermediate (HSSC) papers. Check your own board's website if you appear in Matric.

6Which pen should I use on an OSM answer copy? +

The official candidate instructions published by BISE DG Khan say to use only a blue or black ink pen and not to use red or green ink, highlighters, markers or gel pens that smudge. BISE Gujranwala's student guide also says blue or black ink pen and rules out markers, highlighters, gel pens and pencils. Follow the instructions printed on your own answer copy.

7Can I ask for an extra sheet or write outside the margins? +

The official instructions say no extra or supplementary sheet will be attached and that anything written outside the margins will not be scanned or marked. Plan your answers to fit the space provided.

8Where can I download a sample e-sheet? +

Use the green download button in the Sample Sheet section of this page. For official samples, BISE Lahore lists a Computer Science (OSM) answer book and bubble sheet pattern on its notifications page, and BISE DG Khan and BISE Multan have posted specimen answer sheets.

9Will OSM change rechecking or my marks? +

No verified notice describes a change to rechecking rules. Marks are still given by examiners using the marking scheme. Clear handwriting and keeping each answer in its own space matter more because the examiner reads a scan.

10Is 2027 the start date of OSM in Punjab? +

Not exactly. Computer Science OSM is already scheduled for the 2026 Second Annual exams. Media reports quote the Punjab Education Minister on wider on-screen assessment from 2027, but a confirmed subject list for 2027 has not been verified.

1OSM سسٹم کیا ہے؟ +

3OSM نمونہ شیٹ PDF کیسے ڈاؤن لوڈ کریں؟ +

آپ OSM نمونہ شیٹ PDF اس بلاگ پوسٹ سے براہ راست ڈاؤن لوڈ کر سکتے ہیں۔ اوپر نمونہ شیٹ والے حصے میں سبز رنگ کا "📥 OSM نمونہ شیٹ ڈاؤن لوڈ کریں" بٹن دیکھیں۔

4کیا کمپیوٹر میرے جوابات چیک کرے گا؟ +

نہیں۔ OSM میں نمبر آج بھی انسانی ممتحن ہی لگاتے ہیں۔ کمپیوٹر صرف اسکین شدہ کاپی اسکرین پر دکھاتا ہے۔
