About Hira Science Academy

Hira Science Academy is an educational platform based in Daska, Punjab, providing free notes, chapter explanations, solved examples, MCQs, and board-exam preparation resources for students of Class 9 and Class 10.

🎯 Subject Specialist Faculty 📚 Free Educational Resources 🧭 Board Exam Preparation

What Hira Science Academy Is

Hira Science Academy is an educational platform that provides free study resources for Class 9 and Class 10 students — notes, chapter explanations, solved examples, MCQs, and practice material — organized around the Punjab Boards' curricula.

The academy is based in Daska, Punjab, and its content is created and maintained by Muhammad Tayyab (MSc Mathematics), with Chemistry and Biology contributions from Mirza Abid Shabbir Mughal (M.Phil Chemistry). Together they teach Class 9 and 10 students and turn that classroom material into the notes and resources published on this site.

"This site started as a way to organize the notes and explanations I was already preparing for my own students, so more students could use them to revise and practice."

— Muhammad Tayyab

Why Hira Science Academy Was Created

Many Class 9 and 10 students in and around Daska struggle to find organized, easy-to-follow notes for board exam preparation — especially material that walks through solved examples step by step rather than just listing answers. Hira Science Academy was created to put that kind of material online, for free, so students don't have to rely only on classroom time or paid tuition to access clear explanations.

The goal is simple: organize difficult topics clearly, break problems into understandable steps, and give students enough practice material (MCQs, solved exercises, past-paper style questions) to prepare confidently for their exams.

Frequently Asked Questions About Hira Science Academy

Where is Hira Science Academy located?

Hira Science Academy is based in Daska, Punjab, near Masjid Usman Ghani, Model Town. We are easily accessible to students from Daska and surrounding areas.

Which classes do you teach?

We focus on Class 9 and 10 students preparing for Punjab Board examinations, covering Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Islamiat, and Pakistan Studies.

Who creates the content on this site?

The site is created and maintained by Muhammad Tayyab (MSc Mathematics), with Chemistry and Biology contributions from Mirza Abid Shabbir Mughal (M.Phil Chemistry). Both teach these subjects to Class 9 and 10 students.

Is Hira Science Academy affiliated with a BISE board or the government?

No. Hira Science Academy is an independent educational platform. It is not an official BISE or government body — our notes are organized around the Punjab Boards' published curricula, but they are our own material, not official board publications.

Do you offer online resources?

Yes. We provide free notes, solved exercises, MCQs, and practice material online, organized by class and subject.

How can I contact Hira Science Academy?

You can contact us through our Contact page, via WhatsApp at +923007242631, or visit our campus in Daska.

Questions, or Ready to Get Started?
