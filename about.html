About Hira Science Academy

Founded with a vision to transform science education, Hira Science Academy has been empowering students with quality education, expert guidance, and comprehensive learning resources since its establishment.

Our Story

Hira Science Academy was established with a simple yet powerful mission: to provide quality science education that goes beyond textbooks and examinations. We believe that every student has the potential to excel when given the right guidance and resources.

Located in the heart of Daska, our academy has become a trusted educational institution known for its dedicated faculty, comprehensive study materials, and proven academic results. We specialize in preparing Class 9 and 10 students for Punjab Board examinations while building strong conceptual foundations.

"Our approach combines traditional teaching values with modern educational techniques to create an optimal learning environment for every student."

95%+
Success Rate in Board Exams

Our Mission

To provide high-quality, accessible science education that empowers students to achieve academic excellence, develop critical thinking skills, and build strong foundations for future success. We strive to make learning engaging, practical, and relevant to real-world applications.

Our Vision

To become the leading science academy in Punjab, recognized for producing academically excellent students who are well-prepared for higher education and professional careers. We envision a future where every student has access to quality education that unlocks their full potential.
