About Hira Science Academy

Empowering students of Class 9 & 10 to achieve excellence in Science and Mathematics.

Our Mission

At Hira Science Academy, our mission is to make science and mathematics education simple, accessible, and result-oriented. We provide comprehensive, syllabus-aligned resources that help students develop clear concepts and perform confidently in board exams.

Our Vision

We envision a digital learning environment where every student can access high-quality educational material free of cost — helping them learn independently, revise effectively, and achieve their academic goals.

What We Offer

Comprehensive Notes

Chapter-wise notes for Physics and Math (Classes 9 & 10), following the Punjab Textbook Board syllabus, explained in simple language.

Solved Numericals & MCQs

Fully solved numerical problems and multiple-choice questions designed for quick revision and board exam preparation.

Downloadable PDFs

All resources are available in downloadable PDF format for offline learning convenience.

Continuous Updates

We regularly improve our notes and add new resources to keep the content accurate, updated, and student-friendly.
