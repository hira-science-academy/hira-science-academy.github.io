📘 Chapter 1: Real Numbers

Learn and understand the complete Chapter 1 – Real Numbers from the Class 9 Mathematics textbook with clear explanations, definitions, and examples. This chapter covers rational and irrational numbers, their decimal representations, surds, radicals, and properties of real numbers according to the Punjab PECTAA 2025 syllabus. The study of real numbers forms the foundation of mathematics, dealing with different types of numbers, their properties, and how they relate to each other. Understanding the classification and properties of real numbers is essential for solving mathematical problems and building more advanced mathematical concepts.
