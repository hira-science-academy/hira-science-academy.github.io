Chapter 1: Physical Quantities and Measurements

Introduction to physics, branches, physical quantities (base and derived), international system of units (SI), scientific notation, measuring instruments (vernier calipers, screw gauge), significant figures, and errors in measurements. Includes numerical problems on precision and accuracy.

Chapter 2: Kinematics

Rest and motion, types of motion (translatory, rotatory, vibratory), scalars and vectors, distance and displacement, speed and velocity, acceleration, equations of motion under uniform acceleration, motion due to gravity, graphical analysis of motion.

Chapter 3: Dynamics

Newton's laws of motion, force, inertia, momentum, impulse, law of conservation of momentum, friction (types, advantages, disadvantages), rolling friction, uniform circular motion, centripetal and centrifugal forces.

Chapter 4: Turning Effect of Forces

Torque (moment of force), couple, equilibrium (first and second conditions), center of mass and center of gravity, stability (stable, unstable, neutral equilibrium), and practical applications like levers and bridges.

Chapter 5: Work and Energy

Work done, energy (kinetic, potential), forms of energy, interconversion of energy, law of conservation of energy, power, efficiency, and renewable/non-renewable energy sources. Solved numericals on work, power, and energy calculations.

Chapter 6: Mechanical Properties of Matter

Elasticity, Hooke's law, stress and strain, Young's modulus, pressure in fluids, Pascal's law, Archimedes principle, density, specific gravity, and applications like hydraulic press and submarines.

Chapter 7: Thermal Properties of Matter

Temperature scales, thermal expansion (linear, area, volume), specific heat capacity, latent heat of fusion and vaporization, heat transfer (conduction, convection, radiation), and calorimetry numericals.

Chapter 8: Magnetism

Magnetic poles, magnetic field lines, electromagnets, permanent magnets, magnetic properties of materials (ferromagnetic, paramagnetic, diamagnetic), Earth's magnetic field, and applications in daily life.

Chapter 9: Nature of Science

Scientific method, role of physics in technology and society, limitations of science, science vs pseudoscience, contributions of Muslim scientists, and ethical dimensions of scientific research.
