10th Class Physics Formulas (2026 PECTAA Edition)

This page is a complete 10th Class Physics Formula Sheet covering every chapter of the PECTAA (National Curriculum 2023) syllabus, compiled by Muhammad Tayyab, Subject Specialist Physics at Govt. Christian High School Daska. Each formula is listed with its symbol meanings and SI unit, so you can use this as a fast reference while solving numericals.

Getting the right formula is only half the job — using the correct units and constants is what turns a correct method into a correct final answer. This sheet keeps both together, chapter by chapter, from Thermal Physics through Space and Environment.

Use it alongside the Solved Exercises page to see these formulas applied step by step, or the Key Book for full worked numericals.

📏 SI Units, Symbols & Constants

Numerical marks are frequently lost over unit mistakes rather than formula mistakes. Keep these reference points in mind while working through any chapter's numericals.

📏 SI Units

Always convert given values to SI units (metres, kilograms, seconds, kelvin) before substituting into a formula.

🔤 Symbols

Each chapter page lists the exact symbol used for every quantity, matching the notation used in the PECTAA textbook.

🔢 Physical Constants

Speed of light, Coulomb's constant, and speed of sound in air are listed with their standard values for quick lookup.

🎯 Revision Tip

Write each chapter's formulas on a single card and test yourself from memory before checking this page.

💡 Numerical Tips Using This Formula Sheet

A formula sheet is only useful if you know how to apply it correctly under exam pressure. Keep this sequence in mind for every numerical.

1️⃣ List the Given Values First

Write down every given quantity with its unit before touching a formula.

2️⃣ Match the Correct Formula

Use the chapter-wise list above to find the formula that fits the quantities you were given.

3️⃣ Convert Units Before Substituting

Convert to SI units first, then substitute, to avoid an incorrect final answer.

4️⃣ Check the Answer's Reasonableness

A quick sanity check on the size of your final answer often catches an arithmetic slip before you submit.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What SI unit is used for heat energy in Class 10 Physics?

Heat energy is measured in joules (J) in the SI system, used consistently across Thermal Physics numericals in the 2026 PECTAA syllabus.

Which formulas are most important for Class 10 Physics numericals?

The most frequently tested formulas cover specific heat capacity, wave speed, the mirror and lens equations, Ohm's Law, electrical power, and Coulomb's Law.

Does this formula sheet include physical constants?

Yes, each chapter section lists the relevant physical constants alongside their standard values and units.

How should I use the formula sheet while solving numericals?

Identify the given quantities and units, select the matching formula, convert units where necessary, then substitute and solve, keeping units attached at every step.

Is this formula sheet enough on its own for exam preparation?

It's a quick-reference companion. Pair it with the Physics Notes for concept explanations and Solved Exercises for worked examples.
