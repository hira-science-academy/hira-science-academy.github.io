Frequently Asked Questions About the New 2026 Syllabus

What is the new 2026 PECTAA syllabus for Class 10 Math?

It is the updated Class 10 Mathematics curriculum introduced by the Punjab Education Curriculum, Training, and Assessment Authority, based on the Revised National Curriculum 2023, with a stronger focus on real-world applications.

How many chapters are in the new syllabus?

There are 12 chapters (units), from Complex Numbers through Probability, each with its own solved exercises and a review exercise.

Is this different from the old Class 10 Math syllabus?

Yes, the New 2026 Edition follows the Revised National Curriculum 2023, which introduces topics like Complex Numbers and Vectors and reorganizes several existing chapters.

Which BISE boards do these notes cover?

These notes are suitable for all 10 BISE boards in Punjab, including Lahore, Gujranwala, Multan, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Sargodha, DG Khan, Bahawalpur, and Sahiwal.

Are the notes free to use?

Yes, all chapter notes, solved exercises, and the MCQs practice test are completely free to access online.

Do these notes include real-world applications?

Yes, each chapter highlights practical applications in engineering, finance, and scientific research, in line with the Revised National Curriculum 2023.

Is there an MCQs practice test?

Yes, an interactive MCQs test covering the full syllabus is linked from the practice banner above.

Is there a dedicated page for each chapter?

Yes, each chapter is being developed into its own set of resources, including a key book, solved exercises, MCQs, definitions, formulas, and past papers.
