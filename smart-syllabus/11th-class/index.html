✅ Announced Current status (12 November 2026)

📋 What the 2027 1st Year Smart Syllabus Covers

The official Annex-A for 1st Year (11th Class) (Session 2026-27) reduces selected topics and exercise questions across all compulsory and elective subjects. This single hub page links to the notified details for every subject. Key exclusions are listed subject-wise in the official PDF above, along with the pairing scheme and model papers. The subjects covered include:

  • English (Compulsory): Specific units, poems, exercises, and grammar topics excluded.
  • Urdu (Compulsory): Specific lessons, poems, exercises, and grammar topics excluded.
  • Islamiat (Compulsory): Specific topics and exercise questions excluded.
  • Mathematics: Specific chapters, topics, examples, exercises, and review questions excluded.
  • Physics: Specific topics, examples, exercises, and review questions excluded.
  • Chemistry: Specific topics, examples, exercises, and review questions excluded.
  • Biology: Specific topics, examples, exercises, and review questions excluded.
  • Computer Science & Entrepreneurship: Specific topics, examples, exercises, and review questions excluded.

A full subject-wise list of excluded MCQs, short answer questions, comprehensive questions, and exercise questions is included in the official PDF above. The pairing scheme and model papers for each subject are also part of the same document.

A typical 1st Year (11th Class) student studies 5–6 subjects depending on their group. Compulsory subjects are English, Urdu, Islamiat, and Tarjuma-tul-Quran (or Ethics for non-Muslims). Elective subjects depend on the group: Pre-Medical (Physics, Chemistry, Biology), Pre-Engineering (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics), ICS (Computer Science, Mathematics/Physics, etc.), Commerce, Arts, and others. The 2027 Smart Syllabus reductions, pairing schemes, and model papers for all these subjects are covered in the official PDF above.
