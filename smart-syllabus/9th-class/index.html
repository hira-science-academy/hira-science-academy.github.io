Current status (27 November 2026) — ANNOUNCED ✅

The 2027 subject-wise Smart Syllabus for Class 9 has been officially announced by PECTAA. Notification No. PECTAA/C&C Unit/2026/1238, dated 06.11.2026, notifies the Smart Syllabus (Accelerated Learning Programme — ALP / Deleted Topics and Exercise Questions), Pairing Schemes, and Model Papers for the Academic Session 2026-27 and BISEs Annual Examination-2027 for Grade-9 across Punjab.

All ten subject pages below are now marked ✅ Announced. Students should follow the officially notified deleted topics and pairing schemes for the 2027 examination — do not rely on unverified copies.

🆕 Latest Notification — 25 November 2026

  • Notification No.: PECTAA/C&C Unit/2026/1273, dated 25.11.2026 (Punjab Education, Curriculum, Training and Assessment Authority, Lahore).
  • Supersedes: the earlier Notification No. PECTAA/C&C Unit/2026/1255 dated 14.11.2026. The revised Smart Syllabus for Computer Science & Entrepreneurship and General Science on this site now follows the 25.11.2026 notification.
  • Covers: Revised Smart Syllabus (Accelerated Learning Programme – ALP / Deleted Topics and Exercise Questions), Pairing Schemes and Model Papers for Computer Science & Entrepreneurship, General Science and Masihi Taleem (Grade-9) (Annex-A) for the Academic Session 2026-27 and BISEs Annual Examination-2027, for implementation across Punjab.
  • Issued under: Section 9(1)(b) of the Punjab Education, Curriculum, Training and Assessment Authority Act-2025.
  • Exception: no Smart Syllabus has been notified for Tarjama-tul-Quran and Ethics (Grade-9), as no topics or exercise questions have been deleted in these subjects.

Key points from the notification

  • Issued under Section 9(1)(b) of the Punjab Education, Curriculum, Training and Assessment Authority Act 2025.
  • Approved by the Academics Committee of PECTAA in its 13th meeting held on 05.11.2026.
  • Notifies the Smart Syllabus (ALP / Deleted Topics and Exercise Questions), Pairing Schemes, and Model Papers for Grade-9 (Annex-A).
  • Applicable to the Academic Session 2026-27 and BISEs Annual Examination-2027 across Punjab.
  • Director (IT), PECTAA, instructed to upload the notification and Smart Syllabus on the PECTAA website.

Class 9: 2026 vs 2027 — What Changed?

Point 2026 (past) 2027 (upcoming)

Notification date7 November 20256 November 2026
ApplicabilityApplied to 2026 board exams.Applies to BISEs Annual Examination 2027 (Grade-9, Annex-A).
What students should do— (past exams)Study according to the officially notified 2027 Smart Syllabus, deleted topics, and pairing scheme.

Important: 2026 Is Not Automatically 2027

The 2025 PECTAA notification specifically identified the revised Grade 9 Smart Syllabus as being for the Annual Examination 2026. A new examination year requires its own official notification — and that notification has now been issued on 6 November 2026 for the Annual Examination 2027. Students should follow the 2027 notification, not the previous year's list.

What Should Class 9 Students Study Right Now?

The 2027 notification is now official. Students should follow the officially notified Smart Syllabus, deleted topics, and pairing scheme for the 2027 examination.

  • Follow the officially notified 9th class Smart Syllabus (ALP) and deleted topics list for 2027.
  • Use the notified Pairing Scheme and Model Papers released by PECTAA for Grade-9, Annual Examination 2027.
  • Study from the current officially prescribed textbook and curriculum as the base.
  • Keep solving textbook exercises and important conceptual questions, excluding officially deleted exercise questions.
  • Verify subject details on PECTAA and your BISE website; do not rely on unofficial copies.
  • Bookmark this page — the status table and subject pages reflect the official 2027 notification.

How to Verify the Official 9th Class Notification

  1. Open the official PECTAA Curriculum & Compliance page.
  2. Look for the document identifying Grade 9 and the Annual Examination 2027 (Notification No. PECTAA/C&C Unit/2026/1238, dated 06.11.2026).
  3. Check the notification date and issuing authority.
  4. Cross-check the announcement with your relevant BISE Punjab board website.
  5. Do not rely only on a PDF filename, YouTube title, or social-media screenshot.

Frequently Asked Questions — 9th Class 2027

Has the Smart Syllabus for 9th Class 2027 been announced?

Yes. PECTAA issued Notification No. PECTAA/C&C Unit/2026/1238 on 6 November 2026, notifying the Grade-9 Smart Syllabus (ALP / Deleted Topics and Exercise Questions), Pairing Schemes, and Model Papers for Academic Session 2026-27 and BISEs Annual Examination-2027.

Will the 2026 Smart Syllabus apply to the 2027 exam?

The 2027 exam has its own officially notified Smart Syllabus (dated 6 November 2026). Students should follow the 2027 notification rather than assuming the 2026 list carries over unchanged.

Which subjects are covered by the 2027 notification?

The Grade-9 notification covers all ten subjects tracked on this page: Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, General Science, English, Islamiat, Computer Science, Tarjuma-tul-Quran, and Urdu.

Should I skip topics that are officially deleted for 2027?

Follow the officially notified 2027 deleted topics list. Do not skip topics based on unofficial or unverified sources.

Where can I download the official PDF?

Only from PECTAA's official Curriculum & Compliance page (and adopted by your BISE board). Each subject page above links to the official source. We do not host unofficial copies.
