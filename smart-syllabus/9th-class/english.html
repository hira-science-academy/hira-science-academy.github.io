✅ What Should Students Study Right Now?

Until PECTAA publishes a 2027 notification, the safest approach is to prepare from the current officially prescribed textbook and curriculum. Reducing preparation based on an unverified 2027 PDF is risky.

  • Follow the current 9th class English textbook and officially prescribed curriculum.
  • Keep practising textbook exercises, comprehension passages, grammar, and writing skills.
  • Use the 2026 Grade 9 English pairing scheme and model papers as a study aid — but do not assume the same deleted topics will apply to 2027.
  • Do not permanently skip a chapter, poem, essay, or exercise only because an unofficial website says it may be deleted.
  • Check PECTAA and your BISE board website when the 2027 notification is announced.
  • Bookmark this page — it will update as soon as a verified official notification is issued.

🔍 How to Verify Any 2027 Notification

  1. Open the official PECTAA Curriculum & Compliance page.
  2. Look for a document specifically identifying Grade 9, English, and Annual Examination 2027.
  3. Check the notification date and issuing authority.
  4. Cross-check with your relevant BISE Punjab board website.
  5. Do not rely only on a PDF filename, YouTube title, or social-media screenshot.
