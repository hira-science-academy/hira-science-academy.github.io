✅ Announced — All Subjects Current status (15 November 2026)

The 2027 10th Class Tech Smart Syllabus has been officially announced by PECTAA for all subjects. The notification includes the Smart Syllabus (ALP / Deleted Topics and Exercise Questions), Pairing Schemes, and Model Papers for Grade-10 Tech (Academic Session 2026-27 and BISEs Annual Examination-2027).

This covers General Science (Tech), Physics (Tech), Chemistry (Tech), Biology-Tech, Computer Science & Entrepreneurship (Tech), Agriculture Sciences, Health Sciences, Information and Communication Technologies (ICT), and Fashion Designing. No Smart Syllabus has been notified for Tarjama-tul-Quran and Ethics, as no topics or exercise questions have been deleted in those subjects — students study the full prescribed syllabus there.

📋 Subject-wise Smart Syllabus Overview — 10th Class Tech 2027

The official notification reduces selected topics and exercise questions across all 10th Class Tech subjects. The summary below is taken directly from the notification; the exact page numbers and question numbers are in the embedded PDF.

General Science (Tech)

Chapter / Topic Deleted Content

  • Follow the officially notified 10th Class Tech Smart Syllabus (ALP) and deleted topics list for 2027, subject by subject.

🔍 How to Verify the Official Notification

  1. Open the official PECTAA Curriculum & Compliance page.
  2. Look for the document identifying 10th Class Tech, all subjects, and Annual Examination 2027.
  3. Check the notification date and issuing authority (Director, Curriculum & Compliance).
  4. Cross-check with your relevant BISE Punjab board website.
  5. Do not rely only on a PDF filename, YouTube title, or social-media screenshot.
