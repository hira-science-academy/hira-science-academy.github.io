Current status (12 November 2026) — 9th Class & 1st Year ANNOUNCED ✅ · 10th Class PHYSICS ANNOUNCED ✅ · 2nd Year ANNOUNCING SOON ⏳

The 2027 subject-wise Smart Syllabus for Class 9 and 1st Year (11th Class) has been officially announced by PECTAA. Notification No. PECTAA/C&C Unit/2026/1238, dated 06.11.2026, notifies the Smart Syllabus (ALP / Deleted Topics and Exercise Questions), Pairing Schemes, and Model Papers for Grade-9 and 1st Year.

2nd Year (12th Class): The official 2027 notification is expected in the coming days. Bookmark the 2nd Year hub page — it will be updated with the official PDF, deleted topics and pairing scheme the moment PECTAA announces.

Class 10: The 2026 exams followed the old (full) syllabus. For 2027, a new 10th class syllabus has been introduced. The Accelerated Learning Program (ALP) smart syllabus for 10th Class Physics (Session 2026-27) has been announced — reduced topics, excluded exercise questions, excluded practicals and the PDF are on the 10th Class Physics page. For the other 10th class subjects, no additional smart-syllabus reduction has been verified yet.

Why This Dashboard Exists

Every year, when a Smart Syllabus notification is expected, students, parents, and teachers are flooded with unverified PDF files, YouTube thumbnails, and coaching-centre predictions. Many of those turn out to be outdated, mislabelled, or simply fabricated.

This page is a single, honest source: it shows exactly what has been officially notified (with dates and links) and what has not. When PECTAA publishes a new notification, each subject or hub page here is updated with the official document, deleted topics, excluded exercise questions, pairing scheme, and model paper.

Until then, this page and every subject/hub page will clearly say "pending official notification" or "announcing soon" — because that is the honest, verified position.

What should students do right now?

  • Class 9 students: follow the officially notified 2027 Smart Syllabus and deleted topics list.
  • 1st Year (11th) students: follow the officially notified 2027 Smart Syllabus and deleted topics list — the hub page embeds the official PDF.
  • 2nd Year (12th) students: continue studying the full prescribed textbook until the official 2027 notification is issued. Do not skip topics based on unverified PDFs. Bookmark the 2nd Year hub page for the announcement.
  • Class 10 students: follow the new 10th class syllabus introduced for 2027. For Physics, follow the announced ALP 2026-27 smart syllabus (reduced topics, excluded exercises and practicals — see the Physics page). For the other subjects, study in full and do not skip chapters based on unverified PDFs.
  • Keep solving textbook exercises, excluding officially deleted exercise questions (9th, 11th and 10th Physics) or following the new syllabus (other 10th subjects).
  • Use official pairing schemes and model papers once they are released for the applicable examination.
  • Bookmark this page and check it periodically for any further updates.

Frequently Asked Questions

Has the Smart Syllabus for 2027 been announced?

Yes, for Class 9 and 1st Year (11th Class). PECTAA issued the official 2027 notification on 6 November 2026. For 2nd Year (12th Class), the notification is expected in the coming days. For Class 10, the 2026 exams followed the old syllabus, a new 10th class syllabus is introduced for 2027, and the Accelerated Learning Program (ALP) smart syllabus for Physics (Session 2026-27) has been announced.

Is the 2026 Smart Syllabus applicable for the 2027 exams?

No automatic carry-over should be assumed. Students should follow the newly issued 2027 notification for Class 9 and 1st Year, and the new syllabus for Class 10 (with the ALP reductions for Physics). For 2nd Year, wait for the official 2027 notification.

When will the 2nd Year (12th Class) 2027 Smart Syllabus be announced?

PECTAA has not given an exact date, but the 2nd Year notification is expected within the coming days, following the 1st Year notification issued on 6 November 2026. The 2nd Year hub page will be updated as soon as the official notification is published.

What about 10th class? Is there a Smart Syllabus for 2027?

For 10th class, the 2026 exams followed the old (full) syllabus. For 2027, a new 10th class syllabus has been introduced. The Accelerated Learning Program (ALP) smart syllabus for 10th Class Physics (Session 2026-27) has been announced — see the Physics page for reduced topics, excluded exercise questions, excluded practicals and the PDF. For the other 10th class subjects, no separate smart-syllabus reduction has been verified yet.

Are there separate subject pages for 1st Year and 2nd Year?

Not yet. The 1st Year (11th) and 2nd Year (12th) pages are single hub pages covering all subjects together. Separate subject pages may be added later. The hub pages embed the official PDF, deleted topics and pairing scheme.

Where can I download the official PDF?

The official PDF is only published by PECTAA (and adopted by BISE boards). Each subject/hub page links to the official PECTAA source. We do not host unofficial copies.

How often is this page updated?

Each subject/hub page is updated within 24 hours of any verified official notification. The status table above reflects the latest known state at the "Last updated" date shown at the top of this page.
