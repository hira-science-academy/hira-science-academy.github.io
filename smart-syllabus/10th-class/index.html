Current status (23 November 2026)

Class 10 is different from Class 9. In the 2026 board exams, 10th class students followed the old (full) syllabus — no smart syllabus was issued for 10th class in 2026. For the 2027 exams, a new 10th class syllabus has been introduced, and students should prepare from that new syllabus.

The Accelerated Learning Program (ALP) smart syllabus has now been announced for every subject where topics were deleted ✅ Announced — including Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Chemistry (Tech), Biology, General Science, English, Pakistan Studies, Urdu, Computer Science, and Tarjuma-tul-Quran. Open any subject page for the reduced topics, excluded questions and the official PDF. Pairing schemes and model papers are also available for all 11 subjects — see the columns below.

Important: 10th Class Is Not Like 9th Class

In 9th class, a revised Smart Syllabus was notified by PECTAA for the Annual Examination 2026 and again for 2027 (Notification dated 06.11.2026). In 10th class, the 2026 exams were held on the old (full) syllabus — no 10th class smart syllabus was issued for 2026. For the 2027 exams, a new 10th class syllabus has been introduced, and the ALP smart syllabus has been announced for every subject with deleted topics (Tarjuma-tul-Quran and Ethics: none, as nothing was deleted). Students should prepare from the new syllabus using the smart syllabus reduction, pairing schemes, and model papers. Class 9 students can follow the 9th class notes, while Class 10 students will find chapter-wise material in the 10th class notes.

What Should Class 10 Students Study Right Now?

Because the 10th class has moved to a new syllabus for 2027 and the ALP smart syllabus is now announced for all subjects, use all official resources together.

  • Prepare from the new 10th class syllabus issued for the 2027 exams.
  • Use the smart syllabus reduction for each subject to skip deleted topics and questions.
  • Solve all textbook exercises and important conceptual questions from the new syllabus.
  • Revise chapter by chapter from the free 10th class notes, and see the 9th class notes if you need to revisit last year's basics.
  • Use the pairing schemes and model papers linked above to prioritise high-weightage chapters and practise under timed conditions.
  • Do not permanently skip a chapter or topic only because an unofficial website says it may be deleted — always verify from the official ALP notification on the subject page.
  • Check PECTAA and your BISE website for any further notification, and read how OSM (on-screen marking) works in the Punjab boards so you know how your answer sheet will be checked.
  • Use the free online calculators (GPA, percentage and more) to track your preparation and results.
  • Bookmark this page and check the status table above — it stays updated as official notifications are verified.

What Does PECTAA Officially Say?

PECTAA's Curriculum & Compliance page is the most reliable official reference. Its current Smart Syllabus section lists the revised Grade 9 material associated with the Annual Examination 2027 (Notification No. PECTAA/C&C Unit/2026/1238, dated 06.11.2026), as well as a Grade 11 Smart Syllabus for the 2026 examination. For Grade 10, the revised Notification No. PECTAA/C&C Unit/2026/1266 dated 20.11.2026 supersedes the earlier Notification No. 1257 dated 14.11.2026. The ALP smart syllabus for 10th class 2027 has now been announced across all subjects with deleted topics — check individual subject pages for detailed official references.

How to Check the Official 10th Class Notification

  1. Open the official PECTAA Curriculum & Compliance page.
  2. Look for any document specifically identifying Grade 10 and the Annual Examination 2027.
  3. Check the notification date and issuing authority.
  4. Cross-check the announcement with your relevant BISE Punjab board website.
  5. Do not rely only on a PDF filename, YouTube title, or social-media screenshot.
