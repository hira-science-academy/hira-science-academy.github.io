⏳ Pending Current status (11 November 2026)

No 2027 smart-syllabus (reduced-topic) reduction has been notified for 10th Class English beyond the new 10th class syllabus. In 2026, 10th class followed the old (full) syllabus — no 10th class smart syllabus was issued for that year. For 2027, a new 10th class syllabus is in place. No separate smart-syllabus reduction for 2027 has been verified on PECTAA's public Curriculum & Compliance page as of this update.

This page will be updated within 24 hours of any verified official notification, with the complete list of deleted topics, excluded exercise questions, and the official PDF. In the meantime, the English pairing scheme and model papers are available.

📘 Official PECTAA Reference

PECTAA's Curriculum & Compliance page is the most reliable official source. Its current Smart Syllabus section lists revised Grade 9 material for the Annual Examination 2027 (Notification No. PECTAA/C&C Unit/2026/1238, dated 06.11.2026), as well as a Grade 11 Smart Syllabus for the 2026 examination. It does not list a 10th class 2027 smart-syllabus (reduced-topic) notification.

✅ What Should 10th Class Students Study Right Now?

Because 10th class has moved to a new syllabus for 2027, your first priority is to follow that new syllabus in full. Do not reduce preparation based on an unverified 2027 PDF.

  • Prepare from the new 10th class English syllabus issued for 2027 exams.
  • Keep practising reading comprehension, grammar, translation, essay/letter writing, and question answers as prescribed in the new syllabus.
  • Use the new 10th class pairing scheme and model papers as a study aid — but do not assume any unofficial deleted-topic list applies to 2027.
  • Do not permanently skip a chapter, poem, or exercise only because an unofficial website says it may be deleted.
  • Check PECTAA and your BISE board website when any 2027 notification is announced.
  • Bookmark this page — it will update as soon as a verified official notification is issued.

🔍 How to Verify Any 2027 Notification

  1. Open the official PECTAA Curriculum & Compliance page.
  2. Look for any document specifically identifying Grade 10, English, and Annual Examination 2027.
  3. Check the notification date and issuing authority.
  4. Cross-check with your relevant BISE Punjab board website.
  5. Do not rely only on a PDF filename, YouTube title, or social-media screenshot.
