Class 9 Physics Book – Punjab Curriculum (PCTB / PECTAA 2025)

Access the complete Class 9 Physics notes (PECTAA 2025) featuring fully solved numerical problems, conceptual examples, definitions, review exercises, and topic-wise MCQs. These notes follow the latest syllabus prescribed by the Punjab Education Curriculum Training and Assessment Authority (PECTAA), formerly known as the Punjab Curriculum and Textbook Board (PCTB).

Each chapter explains fundamental concepts such as motion, forces, work and energy, pressure, heat, and electricity with clear diagrams and solved examples. Designed specifically for English Medium learners to develop a strong understanding of Physics principles and ensure success in board (BISE) examinations. Perfect for students, teachers, and exam candidates who prefer well-organized and authentic study materials.
