Class 9 Physics Book – Punjab Curriculum (PCTB 2025)

Explore the complete Class 9 Physics notes (PECTA 2025) featuring fully solved numerical problems, conceptual examples, definitions, review exercises, and topic-wise MCQs. These notes strictly follow the Punjab Curriculum and Textbook Board (PCTB) syllabus and are ideal for students, teachers, and exam preparation throughout the academic year.

Each chapter explains fundamental concepts such as motion, forces, work and energy, pressure, heat, and electricity with clear diagrams and solved examples. Designed to support both English and Urdu Medium learners for a strong understanding of Physics principles and board exam success.
