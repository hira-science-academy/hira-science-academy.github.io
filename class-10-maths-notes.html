Frequently asked questions

What syllabus do these Class 10 Math notes follow?

They follow the PECTAA syllabus for Class 10 Mathematics, based on the Revised National Curriculum 2023, with a strong focus on real-world applications. All content is aligned with the Punjab Education Curriculum Training and Assessment Authority guidelines.

How many chapters are in the Class 10 Math syllabus?

There are 12 chapters (units), from Complex Numbers through Probability. Each chapter includes its own solved exercises, a review exercise, and a dedicated MCQs page for exam practice.

Is this different from the old Class 10 Math syllabus?

Yes, these notes follow the Revised National Curriculum 2023, which introduces new topics like Complex Numbers and Vectors in Plane and reorganizes several existing chapters to emphasize practical, application-based learning.

Which BISE boards do these notes cover?

These notes are suitable for all 9 BISE boards in Punjab, including Lahore, Gujranwala, Multan, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Sargodha, DG Khan, Bahawalpur, and Sahiwal. All boards follow the same PECTAA syllabus.

Are the Class 10 Maths notes free to use?

Yes, all chapter notes, solved exercises, and the MCQs practice test are completely free to access online. No login or registration is required.

Do these notes include real-world applications?

Yes, each chapter highlights practical applications in engineering, finance, and scientific research — a core focus of the Revised National Curriculum 2023. Examples include electrical circuit analysis, computer graphics, projectile motion, and financial modeling.

Is there an MCQs practice test available?

Yes, an interactive MCQs test covering the full syllabus is linked from the practice banner above. Additionally, each of the 12 chapters has its own dedicated MCQs page with 20 interactive multiple-choice questions, instant feedback, and score tracking.

Is there a dedicated page for each chapter?

Yes, each of the 12 chapters has its own dedicated hub page containing exercise cards, MCQs, definitions, formulas, and past paper topics — all in one organized place for focused study.
