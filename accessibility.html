Accessibility Statement

Hira Science Academy is committed to providing accessible and inclusive educational resources for all students, including those with disabilities.

Last updated: June 8, 2026

Our Commitment to Accessibility

Hira Science Academy is dedicated to ensuring that our website and educational materials are accessible to all users, including individuals with disabilities. We are actively working to improve the user experience for everyone by following best accessibility practices and web standards.

We believe that education is a fundamental right that should be accessible to everyone, regardless of their abilities. Our commitment to accessibility reflects our core values of inclusion, diversity, and equal opportunity.

1. Our Accessibility Standards

We aim to comply with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.1) at level AA, which define how to make digital content more accessible for people with disabilities and more user-friendly for all visitors.

📱

Perceivable

⌨️

2. Key Accessibility Features

We have implemented the following features to make our website accessible to all users:

📱

Responsive Design

Our website is fully responsive and works on all devices, including smartphones, tablets, and desktops.

🎨

High Contrast & Readable Fonts

We use clear fonts, sufficient color contrast, and proper text sizing for easy readability.

🖼️

Descriptive Alt Text

All important images include descriptive alternative text for screen reader compatibility.

⌨️

Keyboard Navigation

All interactive elements can be accessed using a keyboard for users with motor disabilities.

📝

Semantic HTML

We use proper heading structures and semantic elements for screen reader compatibility.

🔍

Zoom & Resize Support

Content remains readable and functional when zoomed up to 200% without loss of functionality.

3. Supported Assistive Technologies

Our website is designed to work with the following assistive technologies:

  • Screen Readers: JAWS, NVDA, VoiceOver, and TalkBack
  • Screen Magnifiers: ZoomText and built-in magnifiers
  • Speech Recognition: Dragon NaturallySpeaking and built-in voice controls
  • Alternative Input Devices: Switch controls, eye-tracking devices, and adaptive keyboards

We continuously test our website with these technologies to ensure compatibility and optimal user experience.

4. Limitations and Ongoing Improvements

While we make every effort to ensure full accessibility, some materials may not yet meet all accessibility standards. This includes:

  • Legacy Materials: Older PDF files or scanned documents that may not be fully accessible
  • Third-Party Content: Embedded content from external platforms that may have accessibility limitations
  • User-Generated Content: Content uploaded by users that may not follow accessibility guidelines

We are actively working to convert these resources into accessible formats and prioritize accessibility in all new content creation. Our development team regularly conducts accessibility audits and implements improvements.

5. Our Accessibility Roadmap

We are committed to continuous improvement in accessibility. Our roadmap includes:

  • Quarterly Accessibility Audits: Regular testing and evaluation of our website
  • Staff Training: Training for content creators on accessibility best practices
  • User Testing: Regular testing with users who have disabilities
  • PDF Conversion: Converting all legacy PDFs to accessible formats
  • Video Captioning: Adding captions and transcripts to educational videos

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of our website. If you encounter any accessibility barriers or have suggestions for improvement, please let us know:

7. Non-Discrimination Policy

Hira Science Academy does not discriminate on the basis of disability in admission, access to programs, or employment. We are committed to providing equal opportunities to all students and individuals.

This policy is in accordance with applicable laws and regulations, including:

  • Constitution of Pakistan - Article 25 (Equality of citizens)
  • UN Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities
  • ICT Accessibility Standards

Frequently Asked Questions About Accessibility

What is WCAG compliance?

WCAG (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines) is a set of international standards for making web content accessible to people with disabilities. We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA standards.

What assistive technologies does your website support?

Our website supports major screen readers (JAWS, NVDA, VoiceOver), screen magnifiers, speech recognition software, and alternative input devices.

Are your educational materials accessible?

We are actively converting all our educational materials to accessible formats. While some older resources may have limitations, all new content follows accessibility guidelines.

How do I report an accessibility issue?

You can report accessibility issues via email at tayyabgchs@gmail.com or by phone at +92 333 8114798.

Do you offer accommodations for students with disabilities?

Yes, we are committed to providing reasonable accommodations for students with disabilities. Please contact us to discuss your specific needs.

How often do you update your accessibility features?

We conduct quarterly accessibility audits and continuously implement improvements. Our team stays updated on the latest accessibility guidelines and technologies.
