Single Math Curriculum in Punjab

Fair Standard or Unfair Burden for Arts Students?

Published: September 3, 2026

Analysis of the 2026 unified Mathematics textbook for Grade 9 Arts/Humanities students

📑 Jump to: 📊 Board Results ⚖️ Removed vs Added 🧑‍🏫 Action Steps 📢 Policy Appeal ❓ FAQ
Home > Blog > Single Math Curriculum

A quiet but devastating educational shift is currently unfolding across Punjab. The Government of Punjab and the Punjab Curriculum and Textbook Board (PCTB) mandated that Grade 9 Arts and Humanities group students must study the exact same Mathematics textbook as Science group students.

For decades, the educational path in Pakistan was structured with a clear understanding of students' diverse future needs. Science students studied formal, university-prep mathematics (algebra, geometry, trigonometry), while Arts and Commerce students studied General Mathematics—a highly practical course centered on financial literacy, business calculations, and real-world economics.

"In moving from the previous General Mathematics textbook to the new unified 2026 Grade 9 Mathematics textbook, the curriculum has systematically stripped away all practical, real-world finance and business math that Arts and Commerce students rely on for daily survival."

⚖️ 2. What Has Been Removed vs What Has Replaced It

❌ Removed (Practical Financial Literacy)

  • Zakat, Ushr, Inheritance – calculations of 2.5% Zakat, 10% Ushr, distribution of inherited property.
  • Commercial Banking & Currency – PLS savings, profit/markup, foreign exchange, cheques, demand drafts.
  • Leasing, Insurance, Installments – premium payments, depreciation, down payments, amortization.
  • Consumer Mathematics & Utility Bills – gas, electricity, telephone bills, direct/indirect taxes.
  • Arithmetic & Geometric Sequences – \( a_n = a+(n-1)d \), \( a_n = ar^{n-1} \), means.
  • Business Percentage, Ratio & Proportion – compound proportions, inverse ratios.

زکوٰۃ، عشر، بینکنگ، انشورنس، بجلی اور گیس کے بل، تناسب اور شرحِ سود کے ابواب ختم کر دیے گئے۔

📐 Added (Abstract University-Prep Math)

  • Formal Logic & Deductive Proofs – truth tables: \( [(p \to q) \land p] \to q \), converse/inverse/contrapositive.
  • Trigonometry & Identities – \( (\sec \theta - \tan \theta)^2 = \frac{1-\sin \theta}{1+\sin \theta} \), \( \sin^6\theta + \cos^6\theta = 1 - 3\sin^2\theta \cos^2\theta \).
  • Coordinate Geometry – distance formula \( d = \sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2+(y_2-y_1)^2} \), slope, normal form \( x\cos\alpha + y\sin\alpha = p \).
  • Linear Inequalities & Programming – graphing feasible regions, optimization \( f(x,y)=2x+5y \) subject to constraints.
  • Geometrical Similar Figures & Tessellations – volume scale factors \( V_1/V_2 = (l_1/l_2)^3 \), angles at vertices.
  • Graphs of Functions – reciprocal \( y=\frac{a}{x} \), exponential \( y=ka^x \).

منطق، مثلثات، کوآرڈینیٹ جیومیٹری، لکیری پروگرامنگ اور گراف کے نئے ابواط شامل کیے گئے۔

🧑‍🏫 3. Key Action Steps for Math Educators (2026–2027)

For high school teachers now managing mixed Science/Arts classrooms under this unified curriculum, the following strategies can help bridge the gap and support struggling students:

🔄 Bridging the Gap

  • Integrate practical financial examples (banking, tax calculations) alongside abstract topics to show real-world relevance.
  • Use visual aids and step-by-step scaffolding for logic/trigonometry to reduce anxiety.
  • Offer after-school support sessions specifically for Arts students struggling with abstract concepts.

📊 Assessment Adaptation

  • Weight practical word problems more heavily in internal school assessments.
  • Collaborate with Arts department teachers to contextualize mathematical applications.
  • Provide tiered assignments (core + extension) to accommodate different learning paces.

اساتذہ کو چاہیے کہ وہ آرٹس کے طلباء کی مدد کے لیے عملی مثالیں دیں، بصری اوزار استعمال کریں، اور اضافی کلاسز کا اہتمام کریں۔

📢 4. Urgent Appeal to the Provincial Minister of Education

The 2026 board results from BISE Gujranwala provide concrete evidence that this policy is failing students. When a core subject sees nearly 40% of its total examinees fail, the issue lies not with student effort, but with curriculum design and policy execution.

Key Policy Demands:

  • Restore General Mathematics for the Arts Group – reintroduce a distinct, updated General Mathematics syllabus tailored to non-science students, re-incorporating essential financial literacy, banking, tax calculations, and applied arithmetic.
  • Provide Immediate Examination Relief – adjust the paper scheme and marking thresholds for upcoming examination cycles to ensure Arts students are not penalized unfairly.
  • Re-align Mathematics Education with Real-World Career Goals – ensure Science students receive abstract preparation for engineering/technology, while Arts students receive practical financial and quantitative skills.

ہم پنجاب کے وزیر تعلیم اور پی سی ٹی بی کے چیئرمین سے اپیل کرتے ہیں کہ اس فیصلے کا جائزہ لیں۔ آرٹس کے طلباء کو ان کے مستقبل کے مطابق عملی ریاضی کی تعلیم دی جانی چاہیے۔

📌 Frequently Asked Questions

Is the new math book the same for all students?

Yes, the 2026 unified Mathematics textbook is mandatory for both Science and Arts groups in Grade 9 across Punjab.

Why was the General Math book removed?

The PCTB and government replaced it with a single STEM-focused curriculum, citing "equality" in education standards.

What is the impact on Arts students?

Arts students now face abstract topics like logic, trigonometry, and coordinate geometry, leading to high failure rates.

What can parents do?

Parents can contact the Minister of Education, PCTB, and local MNA/MPA to advocate for the restoration of General Mathematics.
