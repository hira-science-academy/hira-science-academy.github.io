PM Youth Chromebook Scheme 2026 – Eligibility, Online Apply & Documents

The federal government has announced 500,000 refurbished Chromebooks for high-performing students. Here is what is confirmed, what is reported, and how to apply through the official IBCC portal.

Current Status

The scheme was launched on 25 October 2026. The official IBCC portal is the only application route. Eligibility rules are still being clarified across media reports; always check the portal for the final list of accepted institutions.

Home > Blog > PM Youth Chromebook Scheme 2026

The PM Youth Chromebook Scheme 2026 is a federal initiative to provide digital learning tools to students who have performed well in their Grade 9 (SSC-I) examinations. The programme is managed by the Ministry of Federal Education and Professional Training with the Prime Minister’s Youth Programme.

This guide explains the reported eligibility rules, the official IBCC application process, required documents, and the key warnings every applicant should know. Because official rules can change quickly, always confirm details on the official IBCC Chromebook portal before submitting your form.

What is the PM Youth Chromebook Scheme?

It is a government programme that aims to distribute 500,000 refurbished Chromebooks over two years to high-achieving students, primarily from Grade 9. The goal is to support digital learning, access to e-libraries, and online educational resources at home.

Key Facts at a Glance

  • Total Devices: 500,000 (refurbished)
  • Launch Date: 25 October 2026
  • Target Grades: Grade 9 (SSC-I) in most provinces; Grade 9 & 10 in ICT, AJK, GB
  • Minimum Marks (Reported): 50% in latest SSC-I exams
  • Included Items (Reported): Chromebook, carrying bag, and MiFi internet device

Who is eligible?

Eligibility rules have been reported differently across media. The table below reflects the most commonly reported criteria. You must verify your institution type on the portal.

⚠️ Important Discrepancy

Some official launch reports state that students from registered madaris and private schools supported by provincial foundations are eligible. However, other reports (and the portal instructions at the time of writing) suggest the scheme is only for government/public school students. This page cannot resolve that conflict. If you study in a private school or madrasa, check the portal directly or contact your board.

By Province / Region

  • Punjab, Sindh, KP, Balochistan: Grade 9 (SSC-I) students.
  • Islamabad, AJK, Gilgit-Baltistan: Both Grade 9 (SSC-I) and Grade 10 (SSC-II) students.
  • Minimum Marks: A reported 50% in the latest SSC-I exam is required to be considered.

By Institution Type (Reported)

  • Government / Public Schools.
  • Private schools supported by provincial education foundations (reported, but contradicted by some sources).
  • Registered madaris (reported, but contradicted by some sources).

College and university students are not part of this scheme. They fall under the separate PM Laptop Scheme.

How to apply online

All applications must go through the official IBCC portal. There is no other legitimate application form.

Visit Official IBCC Chromebook Portal →

1. Open the official IBCC Chromebook portal.

Go to pmychromebooks.ibcc.edu.pk. Do not use links from social media or WhatsApp.

2. Select your education board.

Choose the BISE or board that conducted your SSC-I exams.

3. Enter your identifier and date of birth.

The portal may ask for CNIC, B-Form, Roll Number, or Registration Number.

4. Verify the displayed record.

Check your name, school, marks, and board details. If the record is wrong, contact your board before proceeding.

5. Upload documents.

Prepare files in the required format and size. The reported limit is 300 KB per file. Use our Picture Resizer for BISE Forms to compress photos and scans quickly.

6. Review and submit.

You cannot edit the form after submission. Check every field carefully.

7. Save your confirmation slip.

Download or print the reference number and slip for your records.

Troubleshooting: “Record not found”

If the portal cannot find your record, try an alternative identifier (such as roll number instead of CNIC) only if the portal allows it. If the problem continues, contact your respective education board. Do not create a duplicate application.

Documents required

The following checklist is based on media reports of the portal’s requirements. Confirm the exact list on the portal before uploading.

  • ☐ Recent colour photograph (plain background) — JPG or PNG, max 300 KB
  • ☐ Grade 9 (SSC-I) or Grade 10 (SSC-II) marks sheet — JPG, PNG, or PDF, max 300 KB
  • ☐ Attested student B-Form or CNIC — JPG, PNG, or PDF, max 300 KB
  • ☐ Attested father’s or guardian’s CNIC — JPG, PNG, or PDF, max 300 KB

Preparation tip: Scan your documents clearly and compress them to under 300 KB each before starting the form. Blurry or oversized files are a common reason for delays. You can use our free Picture Resizer for BISE Forms to resize images without losing quality.

Selection and verification (Reported)

  • Verification: The IBCC will verify your exam results against board records.
  • Merit: Selection is reported to be based on merit within your province/region, board, and stream.
  • School Confirmation: Your school may be asked to confirm your enrolment and results before delivery.
  • Delivery: Devices are expected to be dispatched via courier with a tracking system.
  • Complaints: Grievance committees are reportedly being set up to handle selection and delivery issues.

Important: Applying does not guarantee selection. Meeting the minimum marks does not guarantee a device if the quota for your area is filled by applicants with higher marks.

Sindh registration update

According to media reports, registration for students in Sindh is linked to the announcement of the Class 9 Annual Examination 2026 results by the relevant Sindh boards. Students in Sindh should monitor the official IBCC portal for the exact date when registration opens for their board.

Do not rely on a single date from social media. The portal will display the active application window for your board.

Important warnings

  • Use only the official portal. The only legitimate application URL is pmychromebooks.ibcc.edu.pk.
  • Never pay an agent. No one can guarantee selection in exchange for money.
  • Protect your identity. Do not share your CNIC, B-Form, or portal login with unverified websites or social media accounts.
  • Enter correct information. False information can lead to cancellation of your allocation.
  • Save your reference number. You may need it for tracking or complaints.
  • No application fee has been announced. Do not pay anyone to “process” your form.

Why the scheme matters

A Chromebook with a MiFi device can give a student access to online classes, digital libraries, past papers, and educational videos from home. For students in rural or low-income households who do not have a personal computer, this can make a meaningful difference in how they study and prepare for board exams.

The government has positioned the scheme as a way to support merit and reduce the digital divide. However, the long-term impact will depend on whether devices are delivered on time, whether the MiFi connectivity is sustained, and whether schools and boards can verify records efficiently.

Note: Some reports mention a Google Chromebook assembly initiative in Haripur. That project is separate from the distribution of these devices and is not a confirmed part of the application or selection process.

Frequently asked questions

What is the PM Youth Chromebook Scheme 2026?

It is a federal programme to distribute 500,000 refurbished Chromebooks (with a bag and MiFi device) to high-performing school students, mainly in Grade 9, to support digital learning.

Is the Chromebook Scheme free?

Yes, the devices are being provided free of cost to selected students. No application fee has been announced. Beware of anyone asking for money to “guarantee” selection.

Who is eligible for the PM Chromebook Scheme?

Reported criteria: Grade 9 (SSC-I) students in Punjab, Sindh, KP, and Balochistan; Grade 9 and 10 in ICT, AJK, and GB. A minimum of 50% marks in the latest SSC-I exam is reported. Students must be in government/public schools (or possibly foundation-supported private schools and madaris, depending on the final portal rules).

Can private-school students apply?

Reports conflict. Some say only government/public school students are eligible. Others say private schools supported by provincial education foundations are included. Check the official IBCC portal for the definitive answer before applying.

Can registered madaris students apply?

This is also disputed. The PM’s launch speech included madaris, but some portal instructions reportedly exclude them. Do not assume you are eligible; verify on the portal.

Can college or university students apply?

No. This scheme targets school students (Grade 9/10). College and university students fall under the separate PM Laptop Scheme.

What is the official Chromebook application portal?

The only official portal is pmychromebooks.ibcc.edu.pk, managed by the Inter Boards Coordination Commission (IBCC).

How can I apply online?

Visit the official portal, select your board, enter your identifier and date of birth, verify your record, upload documents (under 300 KB each), review, and submit. Save the confirmation slip.

What documents are required?

Reported requirements: recent photo (JPG/PNG), SSC marks sheet (JPG/PNG/PDF), attested B-Form/CNIC, and attested father/guardian CNIC. Each file must be under 300 KB.

What is the minimum marks requirement?

Media reports consistently mention a 50% minimum in the latest SSC-I (Grade 9) exams to be considered. Confirm this on the portal.

What is the last date to apply?

There is no single nationwide deadline reported yet. Deadlines may vary by board and region. Check the official IBCC portal for your specific board’s schedule. Some Sindh boards are waiting for Grade 9 results.

Why can’t I find my board record?

The portal may not have updated your record yet. Try an alternative identifier (roll number vs CNIC) if the portal allows. If the issue persists, contact your respective education board for help. Do not create a duplicate application.

How are students selected?

Selection is reported to be merit-based within your province/region, board, and stream, after IBCC verifies your exam results. A quota does not guarantee a device for every applicant.

How will selected students receive their Chromebooks?

Reports indicate devices will be dispatched via courier with a tracking system, after school verification of enrolment and results.

Is the PM Chromebook Scheme the same as the PM Laptop Scheme?

No. The Chromebook Scheme targets school students (Grade 9/10) with Chromebooks and MiFi devices. The older Laptop Scheme targeted college and university students with laptops.

When will Sindh registration open?

Media reports state that Sindh registration is linked to the announcement of Grade 9 annual exam results by the Sindh boards. Monitor the official portal for the exact opening date.

How can I report a problem with my application?

First, contact your education board for record or verification issues. For selection or delivery complaints, grievance committees are reportedly being established. Check the portal for official contact details.
