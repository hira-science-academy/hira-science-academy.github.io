Frequently asked questions

What is the OSM system in Punjab Boards 2027?+

OSM stands for On-Screen Marking. It is a digital evaluation system where answer scripts are scanned and examiners mark them on computer screens. It will be implemented across all 9 BISE Punjab boards starting 2027. Read the complete OSM guide.

What topics does the Hira Science Academy blog cover?+

Physics, Mathematics and Chemistry guides, exam strategies, study tips, Punjab Board syllabus updates, government schemes for students and career guidance for Class 9 and 10 students.

Who writes the articles?+

Articles are written by Muhammad Tayyab (MSc Mathematics) and Mirza Abid Shabbir Mughal (M.Phil Chemistry), both experienced Class 9 and 10 teachers.

Are the articles aligned with the Punjab Board syllabus?+

Yes. Articles follow the current Punjab Board syllabus and paper patterns, and syllabus-related posts are updated when official notifications change.

Why are Punjab's 9th class board results so low?+

Our analysis of the 2026 BISE results points to teacher shortages, no permanent headmasters since 2015, automatic promotion in middle school, disrupted academic calendars and a gap between public and private schools. Read the full analysis of the 9th class results.

Can I request a topic?+

Yes. Send your request through the Contact page or WhatsApp and the faculty will consider it for a future article.

Are the articles free?+

Yes. Every article on the blog is free to read.

Have a question about an article or a topic request?

Message the faculty on WhatsApp and we will reply as soon as we can.
