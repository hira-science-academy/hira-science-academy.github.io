Quick answer: You can save any Class 10 math chapter as a PDF in four steps: open the chapter page, press Ctrl+P, choose “Save as PDF”, and save. No app, sign-up or separate download link is needed, and the content is the same online or offline.

How to save any chapter as a PDF

  1. Pick a chapter or exercise.Choose one in the chapter list below, then open the page you want to keep.
  2. Open the print menu.Press Ctrl+P (⌘+P on a Mac). On a phone, open your browser menu and choose Print or Share.
  3. Choose “Save as PDF”.Select it as the destination instead of a physical printer.
  4. Save the file.Pick a folder or Downloads. The PDF now opens offline, on any device.

Each page saves as its own PDF, so repeat the steps for every chapter or exercise you want to keep. The notes are laid out to print cleanly, with solved exercises that don’t get cut off.
