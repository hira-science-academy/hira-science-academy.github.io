✅ Official 2027 Scheme Current status (13 November 2026)

The 10th Class Chemistry (Tech) Pairing Scheme for 2027 has been announced. The exam paper will consist of 40 marks in total, with a time limit of 2 hours. The paper is divided into Objective (10 marks), Subjective Part-I (16 marks), and Subjective Part-II (14 marks).

Make sure to also review the 10th Class Chemistry (Tech) Smart Syllabus 2027 to see which topics have been excluded under the ALP 2026-27.

🗂️ Paper Pattern & Marks Distribution

Based on the official instructions for paper setters, the Chemistry (Tech) paper for Class 10 consists of 40 marks with a 2-hour duration. Here is the exact breakdown:

Section / Question Description Marks
Objective
Q-1		 One multiple-choice question (MCQ) from each chapter 13 to 22. 1 × 10 = 10
(10 MCQs total)
Note: Check the ALP syllabus for excluded MCQs.
Subjective Part-I
Q-2		 4 short answer questions to be answered out of 06.
Chapters: 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 19 (1 question from each).		 2 × 4 = 08
Total 6 questions will be given, you choose 4.
Subjective Part-I
Q-3		 4 short answer questions to be answered out of 06.
Chapters: 20, 21, 22, 23, 24, 25 (1 question from each).		 2 × 4 = 08
Total 6 questions will be given, you choose 4.
Subjective Part-II
Q-4, Q-5, Q-6		 Contains 3 detailed questions bifurcated into two parts (a & b) carrying 4 & 3 marks each. Students have to attempt 2 questions. 2 × 7 = 14
Q-4: Chapter 13 (a) & Chapter 15 (b)
Q-5: Chapter 18 (a) & Chapter 19 (b)
Q-6: Chapter 21 (a) & Chapter 24 (b)
Note: This pairing scheme is the same as given in the Chemistry Tech 10 Textbook at Page number 134.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions — 10th Class Chemistry (Tech) 2027

The total marks for the Chemistry (Tech) paper are 40 marks. The time allowed is 2 hours.

Question 1 (Objective) will consist of 10 MCQs. There will be one MCQ from each chapter, covering Chapters 13 to 22.

Subjective Part-I contains Short Answer Questions (SAQs). Q-2 covers Chapters 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, and 19. Q-3 covers Chapters 20, 21, 22, 23, 24, and 25. You have to attempt 4 out of 6 questions in each.

Subjective Part-II has three questions (Q-4, Q-5, Q-6), each split into parts (a) and (b) carrying 4 and 3 marks respectively. You must attempt 2 questions.
Q-4: Ch 13 (a) & Ch 15 (b)
Q-5: Ch 18 (a) & Ch 19 (b)
Q-6: Ch 21 (a) & Ch 24 (b)
