Privacy Policy

Your privacy and security are our priority at Hira Science Academy

Last updated: June 8, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Hira Science Academy — accessible at https://hiraacademy.com.pk. Your privacy is our priority. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle, store, and protect information when you interact with our educational platform.

We are committed to protecting your personal information and being transparent about our data practices. This policy applies to all visitors, users, and others who access our website.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers. Our website is designed purely to provide free educational content for students and teachers.

The only data we may collect is non-personal, anonymous data used for website analytics, including:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type and operating system
  • Referral source (how you found our website)
  • General geographic location (country/city level)

2. Cookies and Analytics

To improve your browsing experience, we use Google Analytics to understand traffic patterns. This may include non-personal data such as your IP address, browser type, and time spent on pages.

What are cookies? Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us analyze website traffic and improve your experience. We use the following types of cookies:

  • Analytical Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our website
  • Performance Cookies: Help us improve website speed and functionality
  • Ad Targeting Cookies: Used by Google AdSense to display relevant advertisements

You can disable cookies anytime from your browser settings. However, please note that disabling cookies may affect some features of our website.

3. Google AdSense

Our site displays ads through Google AdSense. Google may use cookies to show ads based on your previous visits to our or other websites.

How Google AdSense works:

  • Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites on the internet
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings
  • Third-party vendors and ad networks may also use cookies to serve ads

To control or opt out of personalized ads, visit Google Ads Settings.

4. Links to External Websites

Our content may include links to third-party websites. Please note that we are not responsible for the privacy policies, content, or practices of these external sites.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party websites you visit from links on our site. This policy applies solely to information collected by Hira Science Academy.

5. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided personal data on our website, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

Our educational content is designed for students of all ages, but we ensure that our data collection practices comply with applicable laws, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we collect from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Our security practices include:

  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Secure data transmission using HTTPS encryption
  • Limited access to data by authorized personnel only
  • Regular updates to our security protocols

While we strive to protect your personal information, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection laws, you may have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data
  • Right to Object: Object to the processing of your data
  • Right to Restriction: Request restriction of data processing
  • Right to Portability: Request transfer of your data to another service

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information provided in Section 9 below.

8. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be revised periodically to reflect website updates or legal requirements. The latest version will always be available on this page with the date of revision mentioned above.

We encourage you to review this policy regularly to stay informed about how we are protecting your information. Changes to this policy will be effective immediately upon posting.
