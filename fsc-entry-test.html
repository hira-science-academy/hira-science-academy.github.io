🩺 Pre-Medical Program

Our Pre-Medical program prepares students for careers in medicine, dentistry, pharmacy, and allied health sciences.

  • Biology: Complete F.Sc Biology with emphasis on entry test topics
  • Chemistry: Organic, inorganic & physical chemistry for MDCAT
  • Physics: Concept-based learning with numerical practice
  • English: Vocabulary, grammar & reading comprehension

🎯 Target: MDCAT & Other Medical Entry Tests

Frequently Asked Questions About FSc & Entry Test Prep

What FSc programs do you offer?

We offer both F.Sc Pre-Medical and F.Sc Pre-Engineering programs with complete coverage of all subjects.

Do you prepare students for entry tests?

Yes, we offer specialized MDCAT and ECAT preparation programs with mock exams, practice sessions, and targeted strategies.

What is the fee structure?

The F.Sc program fee is PKR 15,000 per month. The entry test preparation program fee is PKR 50,000.

Who are the faculty members?

Our faculty includes Muhammad Tayyab (MSc Mathematics) and Mirza Abid Shabbir (M.Phil Chemistry), both experienced educators.

How do I enroll?

You can enroll by clicking the "Enroll Now" button on this page or by contacting us through our Contact page.

Do you offer mock tests?

Yes, we conduct regular mock tests that simulate actual entry test conditions to help students build confidence and improve time management.
