Punjab Boards Exam Schedule 2027 – Official Matric Dates & Updates

Punjab Boards have announced the SSC 1st Annual Examination 2027 admission schedule. Check official 10th class exam commencement, admission deadlines, result date and updates for all nine Punjab Boards.

Official PBCC update

Dates stated in notification SO(Boards)Meetings/PBCC/2026, 10 November 2026

SSC Part-II exams commence16 March 2027

Class 10 result20 July 2027
Home > Blog > Punjab Board Exam Schedule

On 10 November 2026 the Punjab Boards Committee of Chairpersons (PBCC) issued notification SO(Boards)Meetings/PBCC/2026 approving the online admission schedule for the SSC 1st Annual Examination 2027, as proposed by BISE Lahore. The notification gives 16 March 2027 as the commencement of the SSC Part-II (1st Annual) Examination 2027 and 20 July 2027 as the result declaration date. The admission phases are explained in our guide to the Punjab Boards SSC 2027 admission schedule – Single, Double and Triple Fee.

The notification does not cover every examination. Where an official date has not been announced, this page says so, and anything marked expected is only a planning guide. We will update the page as BISE Lahore, Gujranwala, Rawalpindi, Sargodha, Faisalabad, Multan, Bahawalpur, Sahiwal and DG Khan publish further notices.

How to check the official date sheet

Visit your board’s official website.

Open Notifications, Date Sheet or Examinations.

Select your class: 9th, 10th, 11th or 12th.

Choose Annual Examination 2027 and download the PDF.

Check subject names, paper code, date, timing and group.

Board portals provide registration, roll number slips and examination services, so rely on them instead of unofficial pages.

Exam timing and paper pattern

Morning session

Paper starts at 9:00 AM. Report by 8:10 AM.

Evening session

Paper starts at 2:30 PM. Report by 2:00 PM.

Practicals

Science practicals follow the written papers

Friday Break Timings

  • Morning Session: Break from 1:10 PM to 2:00 PM.
  • Evening Session: Break from 2:30 PM to 3:00 PM.

Note: These timings follow earlier Punjab Boards examinations. The 2027 date sheet has not been announced, so confirm with your Board’s latest date sheet.

Science, Arts, Commerce, Computer Science, Pre-Medical and Pre-Engineering groups have separate schedules. Confirm your exact reporting time from the date sheet and roll number slip.

Preparation checklist

  • ☐ Make a subject-wise revision timetable with the Study Time Calculator.
  • ☐ Solve past papers of your own board and study the pairing scheme.
  • ☐ Start with numerical subjects: Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Accounting.
  • ☐ Practise English grammar and Urdu essays regularly.
  • ☐ Practise MCQs from board-approved textbooks. Revise with our Class 9 and Class 10 notes.
  • ☐ Keep a printed copy of the date sheet at home.
  • ☐ Ignore “leaked date sheets” and fake social media posts.

Why dates can change

Boards may revise schedules because of Ramadan, Eid, weather or administrative decisions. In 2026, all nine Punjab boards postponed the exams originally scheduled for 3 March and moved Matric exams to 27 March after Eidul Fitr. Intermediate exams began on 20 May. This is why you should wait for the official notification before fixing travel or study plans.

Punjab boards coordinate major exam calendars through the Punjab Boards Committee of Chairpersons (PBCC), so exam dates, results and fee deadlines are often announced together. Any later revision by the PBCC or your Board will replace the dates on this page.

Frequently asked questions

When will the 10th class exams start in Punjab Boards in 2027?

According to the PBCC notification, the SSC Part-II (1st Annual) Examination 2027 commences on 16 March 2027. The date sheet with individual paper dates has not been announced separately.

What is the official SSC Part-II examination date for 2027?

The notification SO(Boards)Meetings/PBCC/2026 dated 10 November 2026 gives 16 March 2027 as the commencement date of the SSC Part-II (1st Annual) Examination 2027.

What is the last date for SSC admission with single fee?

The single-fee admission period runs from 10 November 2026 to 11 December 2026.

What is the double-fee admission deadline?

The double-fee period runs from 12 December 2026 to 28 December 2026.

What is the triple-fee admission deadline?

The triple-fee period runs from 29 December 2026 to 11 January 2027.

When will the 10th class result be announced in 2027?

The notification gives 20 July 2027 as the declaration date of the SSC (10th Class) 1st Annual Examination 2027 result.

When will the 9th class exams start in 2027?

The supplied notification mentions only SSC Part-II, so no exact date for SSC Part-I (9th class) is specified. Wait for an official announcement from the Punjab Boards.

Has the Punjab Boards 2027 date sheet been announced?

No date sheet is included in the notification. It states the commencement date of SSC Part-II, but individual paper dates are awaiting a separate announcement.

When will the Intermediate exams start in 2027?

The PBCC notification does not cover Intermediate (HSSC) examinations, so no 2027 date is announced yet. Check your Board for HSSC notifications.

Where can students check the official PBCC notification?

The original notification is reproduced on this page. For the latest official copy, check the website of your Board and your institution’s notice board.
