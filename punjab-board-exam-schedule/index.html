Matric & Intermediate Exams 2027 in Punjab Boards

Expected dates, registration deadlines, date sheet and roll number slip updates for all nine BISE boards, in one page we update as notices arrive.

Where things stand today

No Punjab board has published an official 2027 date sheet yet. The dates below follow the usual yearly pattern and are marked Not announced until a board notifies them.

Students across Punjab are searching for the Matric and Intermediate annual exam schedule for 2027. This guide gives you the expected timeline, what to do before the date sheet arrives, and where to find official notices for your board.

We will update this post as soon as BISE Lahore, Gujranwala, Rawalpindi, Sargodha, Faisalabad, Multan, Bahawalpur, Sahiwal and DG Khan publish their official notifications.

Punjab boards normally hold Matric exams in March–April and Intermediate exams in May–June.

Registration and admission schedule

Regular students register through their school or college. Private candidates register online through their board.

Who registers and how

  • 9th class (2027 exams): registration schedule already announced by Punjab boards.
  • 11th class (2027 exams): follows its own board notice.
  • Regular students: forms and fees go through the institution.
  • Private candidates: online registration on the board portal.

9th class deadlines (2027 exams)

Regular students had a deadline of 8 June. Late registration was open until 23 June with an additional Rs. 600 fee.

Later 9th, 10th, 11th and 12th admission and fee deadlines normally follow the single, double and triple fee pattern. Check your board’s notice.

Keep these documents ready

B-Form or CNIC · recent photograph · previous result card (if applicable) · domicile (if required)

Photo too large for the form? Use our free Picture Resizer for BISE Forms.

How to check the official date sheet

Visit your board’s official website.

Open Notifications, Date Sheet or Examinations.

Select your class: 9th, 10th, 11th or 12th.

Choose Annual Examination 2027 and download the PDF.

Check subject names, paper code, date, timing and group.

Board portals provide registration, roll number slips and examination services, so rely on them instead of unofficial pages.

Exam timing and paper pattern

Morning session

Paper starts at 9:00 AM. Report by 8:10 AM.

Evening session

Paper starts at 2:30 PM. Report by 2:00 PM.

Practicals

Science practicals follow the written papers

Friday Break Timings

  • Morning Session: Break from 1:10 PM to 2:00 PM.
  • Evening Session: Break from 2:30 PM to 3:00 PM.

Note: Timings are as per official notification. Confirm with your specific board's latest date sheet.

Science, Arts, Commerce, Computer Science, Pre-Medical and Pre-Engineering groups have separate schedules. Confirm your exact reporting time from the date sheet and roll number slip.

Preparation checklist

  • ☐ Make a subject-wise revision timetable with the Study Time Calculator.
  • ☐ Solve past papers of your own board and study the pairing scheme.
  • ☐ Start with numerical subjects: Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Accounting.
  • ☐ Practise English grammar and Urdu essays regularly.
  • ☐ Practise MCQs from board-approved textbooks. Revise with our Class 9 and Class 10 notes.
  • ☐ Keep a printed copy of the date sheet at home.
  • ☐ Ignore “leaked date sheets” and fake social media posts.

Why dates can change

Boards may revise schedules because of Ramadan, Eid, weather or administrative decisions. In 2026, all nine Punjab boards postponed the exams originally scheduled for 3 March and moved Matric exams to 27 March after Eidul Fitr. Intermediate exams began on 20 May. This is why you should wait for the official notification before fixing travel or study plans.

Punjab boards coordinate major exam calendars through the Punjab Boards Committee of Chairmen, so Matric and Inter dates, results and fee deadlines are often announced together.

Frequently asked questions

When will Matric exams 2027 start in Punjab?

Matric exams normally start in March–April. No official 2027 date has been announced yet, so wait for your board’s notification.

When will 9th class exams 2027 start?

Expected in March–April 2027. The date sheet usually comes a few weeks before the first paper.

When will 10th class exams 2027 start?

Expected in March–April 2027, usually together with the 9th class papers. Official dates are not yet announced.

When will Intermediate exams 2027 start?

Intermediate exams normally begin in May–June. Boards will announce exact dates closer to the exams.

When will the 11th class date sheet 2027 be released?

Usually in April–May 2027, before the Intermediate exams. Check your board website for the notice.

When will the 12th class date sheet 2027 be released?

Usually in April–May 2027, often at the same time as the 11th class date sheet.

How can private students register for Matric or Inter 2027?

Private candidates register online through their board’s portal during the announced schedule. Keep your B-Form or CNIC, photograph and previous result card ready, and submit the fee before the deadline.

What is the last date for 9th class registration 2027?

For regular students the deadline was 8 June. Late registration was available until 23 June with an additional Rs. 600 fee.

Where can I download the official Punjab board date sheet?

From the official website of your own BISE board, under Notifications, Date Sheet or Examinations. Use the board links in this article.

When will Matric and Inter results 2027 be announced?

Matric results usually come in June–August and Intermediate results in August–September. Official dates depend on each board.
