Class 10 Math Past Papers – Pattern Analysis & Key Topics (PECTAA 2026)

Nothing prepares you for a board exam quite like practicing under real exam conditions. This page walks through past paper patterns for Class 10 Mathematics — how questions are typically structured and which topics have proven consistently important — to help you know what to expect before you sit the actual exam.

Since the New 2026 Edition introduces topics like Complex Numbers and Vectors under the Revised National Curriculum 2023, older past papers won't fully match every chapter's current format — use this page alongside our solved exercises to practice the exam style directly on the new syllabus.

Frequently Asked Questions About Past Papers

Are past paper style questions solved with full working?

Yes, past paper style questions are addressed with full step-by-step working, matching our solved exercises.

Is this based on PECTAA 2026?

Yes, topic coverage and question style reflect the current PECTAA 2026 syllabus, based on the Revised National Curriculum 2023.

Is the New 2026 syllabus different from before?

Yes, since it introduces topics like Complex Numbers and Vectors, older past papers won't fully match the current pattern for every chapter.

Should I practice under timed conditions?

Yes, solving practice questions under a realistic time limit is one of the best ways to build genuine exam readiness.

Which BISE boards does this apply to?

All 10 BISE boards in Punjab follow PECTAA 2026, so this analysis applies broadly across boards.

Is this resource free?

Yes, past paper guidance and pattern analysis are free to access online with no sign-up required.

Can past papers replace full syllabus revision?

No, past paper practice is best used to test and reinforce what you've already studied, not as your only preparation.

How should I use this with the guess paper?

Use the guess paper to prioritize chapters, then use past paper style practice to work under realistic exam conditions.
