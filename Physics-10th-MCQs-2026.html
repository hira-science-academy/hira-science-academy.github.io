Class 10 Physics MCQs – PECTAA 2026 Punjab Board

This page offers a complete set of Class 10 Physics multiple-choice questions (MCQs) for students studying under the Punjab Board / PECTAA 2026 curriculum. The questions cover all twelve chapters of the Class 10 Physics textbook used across the ten BISE boards of Punjab, and are organised so that a student can revise one chapter at a time or attempt the full paper in one sitting.

Each chapter is split into Exercise MCQs (the multiple-choice questions given at the end of the textbook chapter) and Additional MCQs (extra practice questions written to reinforce the same concepts and cover likely board-exam patterns). Students can choose to attempt Exercise only, Additional only, or All MCQs for any chapter, or take a combined test across the whole book.

Every question comes with instant feedback: once you submit an answer, the interactive test tracks your score, timing, and marked-for-review questions, and after submission you can go through a detailed review of any questions you answered incorrectly, complete with a short explanation for the correct option. This is meant to be used as a genuine self-testing and revision tool — students preparing for class tests, monthly tests, or the final Punjab Board Physics exam can return to any chapter as often as needed.

This resource was prepared by Muhammad Tayyab (Subject Specialist Physics, SSE) at Govt Christian High School Daska, as part of the free study material offered on Hira Science Academy for Class 9 and Class 10 students across Punjab.

Class 10 Physics MCQs – Chapter-wise Practice

All twelve chapters of the PECTAA 2026 Physics syllabus are covered below. Each chapter lists its Exercise and Additional MCQ counts exactly as offered in the interactive test below.

10. Thermal Physics

Exercise 5 + Additional 15 = 20 MCQs

Covers thermal expansion, specific heat capacity, latent heat, evaporation, and superconductors.

11. Transfer of Thermal Energy

Exercise 7 + Additional 15 = 22 MCQs

Covers conduction, convection, radiation, greenhouse effect, and applications like vacuum flasks.

12. Waves

Exercise 8 + Additional 15 = 23 MCQs

Covers wave types, wave equation, reflection, refraction, diffraction, and seismic waves.

13. Sound

Exercise 7 + Additional 15 = 22 MCQs

Covers production and propagation of sound, echo, audible range, and acoustic protection.

14. Reflection and Refraction of Light

Exercise 7 + Additional 15 = 22 MCQs

Covers mirrors, lenses, refractive index, total internal reflection, and optical fibres.

15. Electrostatics

Exercise 5 + Additional 15 = 20 MCQs

Covers electric charge, Coulomb's law, electric field, and electrostatic applications like photocopiers.

16. Electricity

Exercise 6 + Additional 15 = 21 MCQs

Covers current, Ohm's law, resistance, electrical power, and domestic circuits.

17. Magnetism and Electromagnetism

Exercise 6 + Additional 15 = 21 MCQs

Covers magnetic fields, D.C. motors, relays, loudspeakers, and Earth's magnetism.

18. Electromagnetic Induction & EM Waves

Exercise 5 + Additional 15 = 20 MCQs

Covers Faraday's law, Lenz's law, transformers, A.C. generators, and EM spectrum.

19. Basic Electronics

Exercise 5 + Additional 15 = 20 MCQs

Covers semiconductors, diodes, LEDs, logic gates, and analog vs digital signals.

20. Atomic and Nuclear Physics

Exercise 8 + Additional 15 = 23 MCQs

Covers atomic structure, radioactivity, fission, fusion, and \(E=mc^2\).

21. Space and Environment

Exercise 7 + Additional 15 = 22 MCQs

Covers solar fusion, planetary temperatures, cyclones, global warming, and background radiation.

How to Use This Page

  1. Register with your name, school, and contact number — this is a one-time step that lets you attempt any chapter test.
  2. Select a Physics chapter from the dropdown, or choose the full 250+ MCQ test.
  3. Choose Exercise, Additional, or All MCQs for that chapter, depending on what you want to revise.
  4. Start the test and answer each question — you can jump between questions and mark any for later review.
  5. Review instant feedback after submitting, including your final score and percentage.
  6. Check the results summary and open the wrong-answer review to see the correct option and explanation for each question you missed.
  7. Download a certificate showing your score if you would like a record of your attempt.

Why Practice Class 10 Physics MCQs?

Multiple-choice practice is one of the fastest ways to revise a large syllabus like Class 10 Physics. Working through MCQs chapter by chapter helps students:

  • Revise concepts and definitions quickly
  • Identify weak chapters that need more study time
  • Improve speed and accuracy when answering objective questions
  • Get familiar with the style of board-exam MCQs
  • Practise chapter-wise instead of the whole book at once
  • Review mistakes immediately, with explanations
  • Build confidence before class tests and the final exam

Exam Preparation Tips for Class 10 Physics

  1. Study the textbook concepts for a chapter before attempting its MCQs.
  2. Make sure you understand the formulas and units used in that chapter.
  3. Revise key definitions, since many MCQs are definition-based.
  4. Practise the chapter-wise MCQs on this page, starting with Exercise MCQs.
  5. Review every incorrect answer and read its explanation carefully.
  6. Repeat the Additional MCQs for any chapter where your score was low.
  7. Closer to your exam, attempt the All MCQs test under timed conditions to build speed.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are Class 10 Physics MCQs?

They are multiple-choice questions covering all twelve chapters of the Class 10 Physics course, offered here for chapter-wise or full-book practice with instant feedback.

Which syllabus do these MCQs follow?

These MCQs follow the PECTAA 2026 Physics syllabus used by all ten BISE boards in Punjab.

Can I practise Physics MCQs chapter-wise?

Yes, you can select any of the twelve chapters from the dropdown menu and practise that chapter on its own.

Can I choose Exercise or Additional MCQs?

Yes, each chapter has separate Exercise and Additional MCQs, and you can choose to attempt only Exercise, only Additional, or All MCQs.

How can I review wrong answers?

After submitting a test, use the "Review Wrong Answers" button on the results page to see the correct option and explanation for every question you missed.

Are these MCQs useful for Punjab Board preparation?

Yes, they are designed for students preparing for class tests, monthly tests, and the Class 10 Physics Punjab Board exam under the PECTAA 2026 syllabus.
