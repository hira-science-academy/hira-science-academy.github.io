Class 10 Physics Notes

Based on Punjab PECTAA 2025 syllabus – Free PDF Notes, Problems & MCQs

Class 10 Physics Book – Punjab Curriculum (PCTB / PECTAA 2025)

Download comprehensive Physics Class 10 (PECTA 2025) notes featuring fully solved numerical problems, conceptual explanations, important definitions, review questions, and topic-wise MCQs. These notes strictly follow the Punjab Textbook Board (PCTB) syllabus and help students master essential topics such as motion, waves, electricity, electromagnetism, electronics, and nuclear physics. Ideal for students, teachers, and exam preparation, these resources ensure clear understanding and strong conceptual foundations.

Each chapter explains fundamental concepts such as simple harmonic motion, sound waves, geometrical optics, electrostatics, current electricity, electromagnetism, basic electronics, ICT, and atomic & nuclear physics with clear diagrams and solved examples. Designed specifically for English Medium learners to develop a strong understanding of Physics principles and ensure success in board (BISE) examinations. Perfect for students, teachers, and exam candidates who prefer well-organized and authentic study materials.
