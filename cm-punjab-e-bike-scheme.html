CM Punjab E-Bike Scheme 2026: Applications Closed, What Happens Next

Phase 2 applications closed on 4 October 2026. This guide explains the verification stages, guarantor requirements, Bank of Punjab financing, and how to check your application status on bikes.punjab.gov.pk.

Short answer

The CM Punjab E-Bike Scheme 2026 Phase 2 application window closed on 4 October 2026. If you submitted your application before the deadline, you are now in the verification and selection period. The portal lists five stages: Application → Documentation → Verification → Approval & Financing → Bike Delivery. There is no public result page — you check your status only by logging in to bikes.punjab.gov.pk/login. No official balloting date had been announced for Phase 2 at the time this guide was prepared.

Current Status: What Happens After 4 October?

The application phase is over. According to the official portal and government sources, your application now moves through a structured process. The portal lists five stages that every application follows:

Stage 1: Submit Application

Your form is submitted. Note: Creating an account alone is not an application — you must have completed and submitted the form before the deadline.

Stage 2: Documentation

Your documents and your guarantor's documents are uploaded and added to your file. This includes your CNIC/B-Form, driving licence or learner's permit, student proof, and guarantor income documents.

Stage 3: Verification

Officials verify your details and eligibility. The Punjab Information Technology Board (PITB) cross-checks your CNIC against NADRA, your student enrolment with your institution, and your driving permit with the traffic police system. This process typically takes 1–3 weeks after the deadline.

Stage 4: Approval & Financing

You and your guarantor complete the financing formalities with the Bank of Punjab (BOP), which is the official banking partner. BOP will verify your guarantor's income and credit history.

Stage 5: Bike Delivery

After final approval, you receive your e-bike along with a free helmet and safety rods. The scheme offers black bikes for male students and pink bikes for female students.

How to Check Your Application Status

There is no public result page where you can simply type your CNIC to see if you were selected. The only official way to check your application status is to log in to your own account on the portal.

Step-by-Step Status Check

  1. Go to bikes.punjab.gov.pk/login
  2. Enter the CNIC you registered with
  3. Enter your password and the captcha numbers
  4. Click Login
  5. View your application dashboard — it will show your current stage

Important: Do not trust any website that claims to offer a "CNIC status check" or "track application" box without requiring your password. These are not official government portals. If you have lost your Application ID, check the SMS sent to your registered mobile number when you first submitted. You can also call the helpline: 042-99212260.

Will There Be Balloting? What We Know

The selection method for Phase 2 has not been officially announced as of the latest available information. Here is what is confirmed versus what is uncertain:

✅ Confirmed

  • In Phase 1 (2024), an e-ballot was held on 11 May 2024, and successful/waiting lists were posted on the portal.
  • The Punjab government's older scheme page (still describing Phase 1) states that e-balloting is done by domicile district when applications exceed quota.
  • Some sources indicate a ballot may be conducted after 4 October 2026, but no official date has been confirmed.

⚠️ Unconfirmed

  • The Phase 2 page on the official portal does not mention balloting in its Phase 2 section.
  • Claims of "first come, first served" processing appear in some news reports but could not be verified on the official portal or in government releases.

What this means for you: Keep checking your portal login and your registered mobile number for SMS updates. Do not pay anyone who promises "guaranteed selection" — selection is government-controlled and free.

Eligibility Rules: Phase 2 vs Phase 1 (Important Difference)

There is a documented conflict between the Phase 2 portal rules and older Phase 1 rules that some government press releases still repeat.

Phase 2 Portal Rules (as shown on bikes.punjab.gov.pk)

These are the rules you should follow if you applied in September–October 2026:

RequirementDetail

LicenceValid driving licence, learner's permit, or juvenile driving permit

GuarantorRequired; must have verifiable income of at least Rs 40,000/month

Phase 1 (Older) Rules Often Repeated Incorrectly

Some press releases and older guides still state: age 18+, regular student of HEC-recognised degree college/university, and Punjab domicile. If you are uncertain whether you qualify, the portal is the final authority — not older news articles.

The monthly installment of approximately Rs 3,000 is fixed for the entire 36-month period. There are no hidden bank charges or markup added to your principal.

Documents You Should Have Ready

Even though the application is closed, keep these documents accessible for verification and BOP financing steps:

  • Your CNIC / B-Form / CRC
  • Your driving licence, learner's permit, or juvenile permit
  • Student card or bonafide certificate from your institution
  • Your latest fee slip (proof of enrolment)
  • Guarantor's CNIC
  • Guarantor's bank statement and income proof
  • Mobile number registered in guarantor's name (or your own if you have documented income)

Make sure all names, CNIC numbers, and dates match across documents. Mismatches can delay or disqualify your application during verification.

Female Students: Reserved Quota & Pink Bikes

The scheme includes specific provisions for female students:

  • Equal male-to-female allocation across Punjab districts, with female applicants competing in their own dedicated pool.
  • Pink-colored e-bikes designed with ergonomic step-through frames for comfortable riding.
  • Free two-day riding training is offered to successful applicants, which is especially helpful for those with limited practical riding experience.
  • Female students who do not yet have a permanent licence can apply using a learner's permit, which can be obtained online at dlims.punjab.gov.pk.

Important Warnings: Protect Yourself

The scheme is 100% free to apply. Do not fall for these common scams:

WarningWhat to Know
"Guaranteed selection for a fee"No agent can guarantee selection. The process is government-controlled.
Fake status-check websitesOnly bikes.punjab.gov.pk is official. Websites asking for your CNIC without password login are not government sites.
Sharing your OTP or passwordNever share these with anyone, even someone claiming to be from the scheme.
Paying for "fast-track approval"Verification is automatic and free. No payment speeds it up.
Unofficial result lists on social mediaOfficial lists (if published) will appear only on the government portal.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: I missed the 4 October deadline. Can I still apply?

No. Phase 2 applications are closed. The government has indicated interest in expanding the scheme later to other groups (government employees, delivery riders), but no new student application phase has been announced. Check the portal regularly for future phases.

Q: When will the balloting result be announced?

No official date has been announced for Phase 2 results or balloting. Keep checking your portal login and SMS. Do not rely on social media claims of specific dates.

Q: Is the scheme really interest-free?

Yes. The Punjab government pays the full markup/interest. You repay only the principal amount of Rs 109,000 over 36 months.

Q: What if my application is rejected?

If your application fails verification, you may be notified through the portal or SMS. Applications that are verified but not selected in the current round may be carried forward to future rounds, depending on the final policy. Check your portal status for specific messages.

Q: Can I apply with a learner's permit instead of a full licence?

Yes. A valid learner's permit or juvenile driving permit is accepted under Phase 2 portal rules.

Q: Do I need to pay anything at the Bank of Punjab?

No down payment is required. Your first installment is due approximately 30 days after you collect your bike. The government covers registration, token tax, and insurance.

Final Words

The CM Punjab E-Bike Scheme 2026 is a significant opportunity for students who need affordable transport. With a Rs 90,000 subsidy, zero markup, no down payment, and a monthly installment of about Rs 3,000, it removes the biggest financial barriers to owning an electric bike.

Since applications are now closed, your focus should shift to monitoring your application status and keeping your documents ready for the verification and financing stages. Log in to bikes.punjab.gov.pk regularly, keep your registered mobile number active, and ignore unofficial "guaranteed selection" offers.

For the latest official updates, visit bikes.punjab.gov.pk and the Punjab government's scheme page. Hira Science Academy will continue to share verified information as it becomes available.
