📘 How to Prepare for 9th General Science Board Exam 2027

The Class 9 General Science Pairing Scheme 2027 follows the PECTAA Smart Syllabus / Accelerated Learning Program (ALP) for Academic Session 2026-27 and the BISE Exam 2027. The paper carries 75 marks (15 objective + 60 subjective) and covers Chapters 1 to 6 in 2:30 hours.

Start with the MCQs. The 15 MCQs are drawn from the entire textbook: 2 each from Chapters 1, 2, 4 and 6, 3 from Chapter 3, and 4 from Chapter 5. Chapter 5 carries the most objective marks, so give it extra revision. You get only 20 minutes for this part, so practise quick, accurate answers.

In Part-I (short questions), Q.2 covers Chapters 1 and 2 (4 + 5 questions), Q.3 covers Chapters 3 and 4 (4 + 5 questions), and Q.4 covers Chapters 5 and 6 (4 + 5 questions). In each group you attempt any 6 of 9 at 2 marks each, so every group is worth 12 marks and you can skip the three hardest questions.

Part-II (long questions) has five 8-mark questions: Q.5 from Chapter 2, Q.6 from Chapter 3, Q.7 from Chapter 4, Q.8 from Chapter 5 and Q.9 from Chapter 6. You attempt any three, so be thoroughly prepared in at least three of these chapters. Note that Chapter 1 has no long question, but it still appears in MCQs and short questions.

This pairing scheme is applicable to all 9 BISE Punjab boards (Lahore, Gujranwala, Multan, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Sargodha, DG Khan, Bahawalpur, and Sahiwal). Review the official PDF and the chapter-wise breakdown above, then plan your study time around the marks each chapter carries.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions – 9th General Science Pairing Scheme

What is the paper pattern for Class 9 General Science 2027 under the new syllabus? ▼
The 2027 Class 9 General Science paper is 75 marks: 15 MCQs (1 mark each = 15), three short-question groups (Q.2, Q.3 and Q.4, attempt any 6 of 9 in each, 2 marks each = 36), and 5 long questions (Q.5 to Q.9, attempt any 3, 8 marks each = 24). Time is 2:30 hours: 20 minutes objective + 2:10 hours subjective.
How many MCQs come from each chapter in 9th General Science 2027? ▼
There are 15 MCQs: 2 each from Chapters 1, 2, 4 and 6, 3 from Chapter 3, and 4 from Chapter 5.
Which chapters carry the long questions in 9th General Science 2027? ▼
Q.5 is from Chapter 2, Q.6 from Chapter 3, Q.7 from Chapter 4, Q.8 from Chapter 5 and Q.9 from Chapter 6. No long question comes from Chapter 1. You attempt any 3 of the 5, 8 marks each.
Is this pairing scheme valid for all Punjab Boards? ▼
Yes, this scheme is based on the PECTAA Smart Syllabus / ALP for BISE Exam 2027, followed by all 9 BISE Punjab boards: Lahore, Gujranwala, Multan, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Sargodha, DG Khan, Bahawalpur, and Sahiwal.
