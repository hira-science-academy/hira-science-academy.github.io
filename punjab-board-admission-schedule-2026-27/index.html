Punjab Boards Matric Admission Schedule 2027 – Single, Double & Triple Fee, All Nine Boards

Home > Blog > Punjab Boards Admission Schedule 2027

On 10 November 2026 the Punjab Boards Committee of Chairpersons (PBCC) issued notification SO(Boards)Meetings/PBCC/2026 approving the online admission schedule for the SSC 1st Annual Examination 2027, as proposed by BISE Lahore. Admission runs in three phases: single fee until 11 December 2026, double fee until 28 December 2026 and triple fee until 11 January 2027. The same notification gives 16 March 2027 as the commencement of the SSC Part-II (1st Annual) Examination and 20 July 2027 as the result date.

This guide explains what the PBCC notification says, what it does not say, and where each Board’s own notice and fee schedule can be found. The official admission and fee notifications of all nine Boards are linked below, and the Matric admission fee structure for all nine Boards explains what is and is not verified. For paper dates and results, see the Punjab Boards 2027 examination schedule and 10th class result date.

How to use these notices

  1. Open your own Board's PDF (Official PDF button) and note the dates, fee details and instructions it gives.
  2. If the Board website is slow or blocked, use the backup copy or the preview above. Backup copies are saved copies of the originals, not replacements.
  3. Compare the Board's dates with the PBCC schedule on this page. If they differ, follow your Board's notice and your school's instructions.
  4. If the Board issues a revised notice, the Board's website is authoritative.

The three admission phases

Phase 1: Single Fee

10 November 2026 to 11 December 2026

Students should complete their online admission formalities during the single-fee period.

Phase 2: Double Fee

12 December 2026 to 28 December 2026

This is the double-fee admission period according to the PBCC schedule.

Phase 3: Triple Fee

29 December 2026 to 11 January 2027

This is the final admission period specified in the notification.

The notification identifies the fee categories but does not state the amounts. Confirm them from the applicable official Board fee schedule. No further phase or extension date is given in the notification, and we have not added one.

What does this notification cover?

The notification provides the online admission schedule for the SSC 1st Annual Examination 2027. The text we have does not set out separate eligibility rules or separate deadlines for regular and private candidates, so none are given here.

For candidate-specific admission procedures, consult your respective Board and your institution.

What should students do?

The notification itself gives no student-level procedure. The steps below are general practical advice, not an official Board procedure.

  1. Contact your school administration early.
  2. Confirm your class and registration details.
  3. Check your name, father's name, date of birth and group.
  4. Ask which documents the school needs.
  5. Follow the school's instructions for online admission.
  6. Confirm the applicable charges before paying.
  7. Complete everything within the prescribed phase dates.
  8. Get confirmation that your admission was submitted.

How single, double and triple fee work

  • Lahore, Gujranwala, Rawalpindi and Sahiwal print one set of fee rates (Lahore’s notice is titled single examination fee and other dues). None of the four states a multiplier or separate double/triple amounts, so we do not calculate them. Check the Board notice or challan for the exact double and triple amounts.
  • Multan prints single, double and triple amounts. Comparing the figures, the double and triple columns rise by the examination-fee part only (for example Class 9 regular Science: Rs 2,900, 3,600, 4,300, a step of Rs 700). This is our reading of the printed numbers, not a rule stated by the Board.
  • Late windows after the triple-fee date: Sahiwal accepts triple fee plus Rs 500 per day until 28 February 2027. Rawalpindi accepts forms with triple fee plus a fine of Rs 200 per day from 12 January to 28 February 2027. Multan accepts triple fee plus Rs 500 per day from 12 January to 6 March 2027 (until ten days before the exam). Lahore says no admission form will be accepted after 11 January 2027, and Gujranwala says none after the schedule.

Important instructions for students and parents

  • Do not wait until the last day.
  • Verify your personal information.
  • Confirm that your school has submitted your admission.
  • Keep any receipt or confirmation.
  • Ask the school about applicable charges.
  • Do not trust unofficial admission links or payment requests.
  • Follow the latest instructions from your own Board and institution.

What happens after admission?

Once admission is submitted, check that it is confirmed and that your registration or examination record is accurate, and follow later Board instructions. The notification gives 16 March 2027 as the SSC Part-II commencement date and 20 July 2027 as the result date (see the exam schedule page). It gives no date sheet, roll number slip date, practical date or SSC Part-I (Class 9) exam date, so none is given here. Keep preparing with your syllabus in the meantime.

Registration and examination admission: the difference

Registration or enrolment records a student with the Board, while examination admission enters the student for an exam. Where applicable they are separate administrative processes, but not every student must complete two. What you need depends on your existing registration status and your Board's instructions, so ask your school. Class 9 registration should not be confused with the SSC examination admission covered here.

Frequently asked questions

What is the last date for Matric admission with a single fee in 2027?

The single-fee period runs from 10 November 2026 to 11 December 2026. The Lahore, Gujranwala, Rawalpindi, Sahiwal and Multan notices and the BISE Faisalabad portal all show this date.

What are the double-fee and triple-fee admission dates?

Double fee: 12 December 2026 to 28 December 2026. Triple fee: 29 December 2026 to 11 January 2027.

What is the Class 9 admission fee in each Punjab Board?

Single-fee total for a regular Science candidate: Lahore Rs 2,200 and Gujranwala Rs 2,200 (calculated), Rawalpindi Rs 1,880 (calculated), Sahiwal Rs 1,850 (calculated), Multan Rs 2,900 (printed). Private fresh candidates: Lahore Rs 2,480, Gujranwala Rs 4,050, Rawalpindi Rs 4,030, Sahiwal Rs 2,900, Multan Rs 4,150. Sargodha, Faisalabad, Bahawalpur and DG Khan amounts are awaiting verification.

What is the Class 10 admission fee in each Punjab Board?

Single-fee total for a regular Science candidate: Lahore Rs 3,480, Gujranwala Rs 3,500, Rawalpindi Rs 2,980, Sahiwal Rs 2,850 (all calculated), Multan Rs 4,300 (printed). Private fresh: Lahore Rs 3,480, Gujranwala Rs 3,850 (excluding registration), Rawalpindi Rs 3,980, Sahiwal Rs 2,900 (excluding registration), Multan Rs 4,350. The other four Boards are awaiting verification.

Are private candidates charged differently?

Yes in most Boards. Lahore charges private and regular candidates the same except a Rs 280 Class 9 sports fee. Gujranwala private Science exam fee is Rs 950 per class plus registration and postal charges. Rawalpindi and Sahiwal private candidates pay registration (Rs 1,150 and Rs 1,000), and Rawalpindi adds development, scholarship and postal charges. Multan prints a separate private table.

Is the fee structure the same for all nine Punjab Boards?

No. The dates are common, but each Board prints its own fee rates and charges, and the single-fee totals above differ between Lahore, Gujranwala, Rawalpindi, Sahiwal and Multan.

What is the admission fee for Science and Arts groups?

Lahore charges the same exam fee (Rs 900 per class) for Science and Arts. Gujranwala regular Arts is Rs 850 against Rs 900 for Science. Rawalpindi regular Arts is Rs 750 against Rs 800 for Science or Arts with practical. Sahiwal Arts without practical is Rs 650 against Rs 700. Multan charges less for Arts without practical (Rs 50 to Rs 300 depending on category and fee phase).

Are government-school students exempt from examination fees?

Partly. Lahore, Gujranwala and Multan allow public-sector regular candidates to submit forms without the examination fee during the single-fee window, but processing, certificate and similar charges still apply. Sahiwal and Rawalpindi limit any Board-approved exemption to the single-fee period. Check your Board’s notice for the exact list.

What additional charges are included in the total fee?

Depending on the Board: processing, certificate, scholarship, development, sports, registration, postal, CCTV and similar charges. For example Lahore lists processing 870, certificate 1,000, scholarship 80, development 350 and sports 280; Sahiwal lists processing 800, certificate 1,000 and development 350.

Can students submit admission forms after 11 January 2027?

It depends on the Board. Lahore says no form is accepted after 11 January 2027. Rawalpindi accepts triple fee plus Rs 200 per day until 28 February 2027, Sahiwal triple fee plus Rs 500 per day until 28 February 2027, and Multan triple fee plus Rs 500 per day until 6 March 2027. For other Boards no later window is verified.

Where can students download their Board’s official admission notification?

Use the links in the Board cards and tables on this page. We link the Board’s own PDF or notification page where available, plus saved backup copies for Lahore, Gujranwala, Rawalpindi, Sahiwal and Multan.

How can private candidates submit admission forms online?

Through the Board’s private admission portal or website: for example BISE Multan (student.bisemultan.edu.pk), BISE Faisalabad (admission.bisefsd.edu.pk), BISE Gujranwala (challan from bisegrw.edu.pk), BISE Rawalpindi (biserawalpindi.edu.pk) and BISE Sahiwal (online form, challan paid at UBL). Pay through the Board-approved channel.

What is the Punjab Boards SSC admission schedule for 2027?

Single fee 10 November to 11 December 2026, double fee 12 to 28 December 2026 and triple fee 29 December 2026 to 11 January 2027, per PBCC notification SO(Boards)Meetings/PBCC/2026 dated 10 November 2026.
