Home > Blog > Punjab Boards SSC Admission Schedule 2026–27

On 10 November 2026 the Punjab Boards Committee of Chairpersons (PBCC) issued notification SO(Boards)Meetings/PBCC/2026 approving the online admission schedule for the SSC 1st Annual Examination 2027, as proposed by BISE Lahore. Admission runs in three phases: single fee until 11 December 2026, double fee until 28 December 2026 and triple fee until 11 January 2027. The same notification gives 16 March 2027 as the commencement of the SSC Part-II (1st Annual) Examination and 20 July 2027 as the result date.

This guide explains what the notification says, what it does not say, and what to confirm with your own Board and institution. For paper dates and results, see the Punjab Boards 2027 examination schedule and 10th class result date.

Original PBCC Notification – SSC 1st Annual Examination 2027

  • Notification number: SO(Boards)Meetings/PBCC/2026
  • Notification date: 10 November 2026
  • Issuing authority: Punjab Boards Committee of Chairpersons, camped at the Higher Education Department
  • Proposed by: BISE Lahore
  • Signed by: Rizwan Nazir, Secretary PBCC

The Chairperson, in anticipation of PBCC approval, approved the online admission schedule for the SSC 1st Annual Examination 2027 as proposed by BISE Lahore.

The three admission phases

Phase 1: Single Fee

10 November 2026 to 11 December 2026

Students should complete their online admission formalities during the single-fee period.

Phase 2: Double Fee

12 December 2026 to 28 December 2026

This is the double-fee admission period according to the PBCC schedule.

Phase 3: Triple Fee

29 December 2026 to 11 January 2027

This is the final admission period specified in the notification.

The notification identifies the fee categories but does not state the amounts. Confirm them from the applicable official Board fee schedule. No further phase or extension date is given in the notification, and we have not added one.

What does this notification cover?

The notification provides the online admission schedule for the SSC 1st Annual Examination 2027. The text we have does not set out separate eligibility rules or separate deadlines for regular and private candidates, so none are given here.

For candidate-specific admission procedures, consult your respective Board and your institution.

What should students do?

The notification itself gives no student-level procedure. The steps below are general practical advice, not an official Board procedure.

  1. Contact your school administration early.
  2. Confirm your class and registration details.
  3. Check your name, father's name, date of birth and group.
  4. Ask which documents the school needs.
  5. Follow the school's instructions for online admission.
  6. Confirm the applicable charges before paying.
  7. Complete everything within the prescribed phase dates.
  8. Get confirmation that your admission was submitted.

Important instructions for students and parents

  • Do not wait until the last day.
  • Verify your personal information.
  • Confirm that your school has submitted your admission.
  • Keep any receipt or confirmation.
  • Ask the school about applicable charges.
  • Do not trust unofficial admission links or payment requests.
  • Follow the latest instructions from your own Board and institution.

What happens after admission?

Once admission is submitted, check that it is confirmed and that your registration or examination record is accurate, and follow later Board instructions. The notification gives 16 March 2027 as the SSC Part-II commencement date and 20 July 2027 as the result date (see the exam schedule page). It gives no date sheet, roll number slip date, practical date or SSC Part-I (Class 9) exam date, so none is given here. Keep preparing with your syllabus in the meantime.

Registration and examination admission: the difference

Registration or enrolment records a student with the Board, while examination admission enters the student for an exam. Where applicable they are separate administrative processes, but not every student must complete two. What you need depends on your existing registration status and your Board's instructions, so ask your school. Class 9 registration should not be confused with the SSC examination admission covered here.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Punjab Boards SSC admission schedule for 2026–27?

According to the PBCC notification, online admission for the SSC 1st Annual Examination 2027 runs in three phases: single fee from 10 November to 11 December 2026, double fee from 12 to 28 December 2026 and triple fee from 29 December 2026 to 11 January 2027.

What is the last date for admission with single fee?

The single-fee period closes on 11 December 2026.

What is the double-fee admission deadline?

The double-fee period closes on 28 December 2026.

What is the triple-fee admission deadline?

The triple-fee period closes on 11 January 2027.

When does SSC admission with double fee start?

Admission with double fee starts on 12 December 2026.

When does SSC admission with triple fee start?

Admission with triple fee starts on 29 December 2026.

What is the last date for SSC 2027 admission?

The last date given in the notification is 11 January 2027, the end of the triple-fee period. No later extension is stated.

When will the 10th class exams start in 2027?

The notification gives 16 March 2027 as the commencement of the SSC Part-II (1st Annual) Examination 2027.

When will the 10th class result be announced?

The notification gives 20 July 2027 as the declaration date of the SSC (10th Class) 1st Annual Examination 2027 result.

Which authority issued the SSC 2027 admission schedule?

The Punjab Boards Committee of Chairpersons (PBCC), camped at the Higher Education Department. The schedule was proposed by BISE Lahore.

What is the PBCC notification number?

The notification number is SO(Boards)Meetings/PBCC/2026, dated 10 November 2026.

Where can students check their Board’s admission instructions?
