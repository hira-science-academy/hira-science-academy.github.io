Punjab Boards Class 9 & 10 Admission Schedule 2026–27

Official PBCC dates, late-fee details and practical guidance for regular Class 9 and Class 10 students.

The Punjab Boards Committee of Chairpersons (PBCC) has approved the Punjab Boards admission schedule 2026–27 for regular Class 9 and Class 10 students. Admission is open from 2 November 2026, with a window without late fee and a later window with a Rs. 600 late fee.

Missing the first window costs extra, so students, parents and school heads should read the notification carefully and plan early. This guide explains what the notification says, what it does not say, and what to confirm with your own Board and school.

Original PBCC notification

The notification (reference SO(Boards)Meetings/PBCC/2026, camped at the Higher Education Department, Lahore) states that the PBCC approved the online admission schedule as proposed by BISE Multan and BISE Lahore.

The two admission phases

Phase 1: Without late fee

2 November – 23 November 2026

No additional late fee under this schedule. Regular students should complete admission formalities through their institutions within this period.

Phase 2: With late fee

24 November – 7 December 2026

Rs. 600 per student, payable to the Board. This is additional to other applicable charges.

The notification gives no closing time for either date, and no separate higher-fee period. We have not added any.

Who does this notification apply to?

Regular students are those enrolled in an affiliated school or college who appear in the exam through that institution. The notification covers regular Class 9 and Class 10 students, and the head of the institution normally handles the admission process on their behalf. Private candidates appear without institutional enrolment.

The supplied PBCC notification specifies regular students. Private candidates must check the separate admission or registration notification issued by their respective Board.

What should regular students do?

The notification itself gives no student-level procedure. The steps below are general practical advice, not an official Board procedure.

  1. Contact your school administration early.
  2. Confirm your class and registration details.
  3. Check your name, father's name, date of birth and group.
  4. Ask which documents the school needs.
  5. Follow the school's instructions for online admission.
  6. Confirm the applicable charges before paying.
  7. Complete everything within the prescribed dates.
  8. Get confirmation that your admission was submitted.

What does Rs. 600 mean?

Rs. 600 is the late fee in the notification. It is payable per student to the Board and applies to the late window (24 November–7 December 2026). It is not necessarily the full admission fee.

Important instructions for students and parents

  • Do not wait until the last day.
  • Verify your personal information.
  • Confirm that your school has submitted your admission.
  • Keep any receipt or confirmation.
  • Ask the school about applicable charges.
  • Do not trust unofficial admission links or payment requests.
  • Follow the latest instructions from your own Board and institution.

What happens after admission?

Once admission is submitted, check that it is confirmed and that your registration or examination record is accurate, and follow later Board instructions. No date sheet, roll number slip date, exam date or result date is covered by this notification, so none is given here. Keep preparing with your syllabus in the meantime.

Registration and examination admission: the difference

Registration or enrolment records a student with the Board, while examination admission enters the student for an exam. Where applicable they are separate administrative processes, but not every student must complete two. What you need depends on your existing registration status and your Board's instructions, so ask your school. Class 9 registration should not be confused with the Class 9 and 10 admission covered here.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Punjab Boards Class 9 and 10 admission schedule for 2026–27?

According to the PBCC notification, regular students can be admitted without late fee from 2 to 23 November 2026, and with a Rs. 600 late fee from 24 November to 7 December 2026.

When does Class 9 admission start in Punjab Boards?

For the schedule in this notification, admission opens on 2 November 2026 for regular Class 9 and Class 10 students.

What is the last date for 9th class admission in 2026?

Without late fee the last date is 23 November 2026. With the late fee, the notification allows admission up to 7 December 2026.

What is the last date for 10th class admission in 2026?

The same dates apply: 23 November 2026 without late fee and 7 December 2026 with late fee, as the notification covers both Class IX and X regular students.

When will admission without a late fee close?

On 23 November 2026. The notification does not state a cut-off time, so submit well before the day ends and confirm any time limit with your school.

What is the late admission date for Punjab Boards?

The late-fee window runs from 24 November to 7 December 2026.

How much is the late admission fee?

Rs. 600 per student, payable to the Board.

Is the Rs. 600 late fee per student?

Yes. The notification specifies Rs. 600 per student, payable to the Board.

Does the schedule apply to private candidates?

No. The supplied notification covers regular students. Private candidates should check the separate notification of their own Board.

Does this notification apply to all nine Punjab Boards?

It was issued by the PBCC, the coordinating body of the Punjab Boards, on a proposal by BISE Multan and BISE Lahore. We have not independently verified a separate implementation notice from each Board, so confirm with your Board and school.

How do regular students submit their admission forms?

Regular students are admitted through their institutions, which handle the online admission process. Ask your school what it needs from you and how it will submit your details.

Can students submit admission through their school?

Regular students should go through their school or college. This article does not suggest individual students file private forms.

Is Rs. 600 the complete admission fee?

No. It is the additional late fee. Standard admission charges are not stated in the notification, so confirm them with your school or Board.

Where can I find the original PBCC notification?

The image of the notification is reproduced in this article. For the latest official copy, check the PBCC or your Board's website and notice board.

How can students confirm their admission submission?

Ask your school for written or printed confirmation and keep any receipt or challan. Do not rely on verbal assurance near the deadline.
