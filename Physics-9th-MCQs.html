9th Class Physics MCQs 2026 – According to PECTAA Smart Syllabus

📌 Updated for the PECTAA Smart Syllabus 2026-27. MCQs related to the topics removed under the Smart Syllabus have been taken out, and the remaining MCQs are arranged chapter by chapter.

This page offers 9th Class Physics MCQs 2026 arranged according to the Smart Syllabus / Accelerated Learning Program (ALP) announced by the Punjab Education, Curriculum, Training and Assessment Authority (PECTAA) for the 2026-27 session. If you are searching for Physics 9th Class MCQs according to Smart Syllabus, these Class 9 Physics MCQs for Punjab Board have been organised so that you can prepare effectively for your BISE examinations without spending time on topics that have been deleted.

The PECTAA Smart Syllabus Physics MCQs below cover all nine chapters of the Class 9 Physics textbook used across the nine BISE boards of Punjab. Each chapter is split into Exercise MCQs (the textbook-exercise multiple-choice questions that remain in the Smart Syllabus) and Additional MCQs (extra practice questions that reinforce the same concepts). Students can revise 9th Physics MCQs chapter wise by choosing Exercise only, Additional only, or All MCQs for any chapter, or attempt the full test in one sitting.

As stated in the PECTAA notification, all types of questions for the BISE examination paper are prepared from the entire textbook content other than the topics and questions that have been excluded or deleted in the Smart Syllabus. Practising the revised set helps you focus on what can actually appear in your paper.

These are 9th Class Physics MCQs with answers: once you submit a test, you see your score, timing and marked-for-review questions, and you can review every question you answered incorrectly with the correct option and a short explanation. Use it as a genuine self-testing and revision tool for class tests, monthly tests, and the final Punjab Board Physics exam.

This resource was prepared by Muhammad Tayyab (Subject Specialist Education, SSE) at Govt Christian High School Daska, as part of the free study material offered on Hira Science Academy for Class 9 and Class 10 students across Punjab.

9th Physics MCQs Chapter Wise – PECTAA Smart Syllabus

All nine chapters of the PECTAA Smart Syllabus 2026-27 Physics course are listed below. Each chapter shows its Exercise and Additional MCQ counts exactly as offered in the interactive test. Tap a chapter to jump straight to it.

1. Physical Quantities

Exercise 7 + Additional 17 = 24 MCQs

Covers physical quantities, SI units and prefixes, length and volume measuring instruments (Vernier Callipers, micrometer screw gauge, measuring cylinder), significant figures, and scientific notation.

Exercise 5 + Additional 17 = 22 MCQs

Covers rest and motion, distance and displacement, speed, velocity, acceleration, equations of motion, and the area under a speed–time graph.

3. Dynamics

Exercise 6 + Additional 22 = 28 MCQs

Covers force, contact and non-contact forces, Newton's laws of motion, weight and mass, friction, momentum, and the law of conservation of momentum.

4. Turning Effect of Forces

Exercise 6 + Additional 13 = 19 MCQs

Covers torque and couples, equilibrium and its conditions, components of a force, and centripetal force.

Tap to practise this chapter →

5. Work & Energy

Exercise 6 + Additional 12 = 18 MCQs

Covers work, power, kinetic energy, potential energy, and the SI units of work, energy and power.

6. Mechanical Properties

Exercise 5 + Additional 14 = 19 MCQs

Covers elasticity and Hooke's law, density, pressure in solids and fluids, atmospheric pressure, and Pascal's law.

Covers heat and temperature, the Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin scales, and thermometric liquids.

Why Practice Class 9 Physics MCQs?

Multiple-choice practice is one of the fastest ways to revise a large syllabus like Class 9 Physics. Working through MCQs chapter by chapter helps students:

  • Revise concepts and definitions quickly
  • Identify weak chapters that need more study time
  • Improve speed and accuracy when answering objective questions
  • Get familiar with the style of board-exam MCQs
  • Practise chapter-wise instead of the whole book at once
  • Review mistakes immediately, with explanations
  • Build confidence before class tests and the final exam

Exam Preparation Tips for Class 9 Physics

A short, teacher-recommended approach for using this MCQ resource alongside your regular study:

  1. Study the textbook concepts for a chapter before attempting its MCQs.
  2. Make sure you understand the formulas and units used in that chapter.
  3. Revise key definitions, since many MCQs are definition-based.
  4. Practise the chapter-wise MCQs on this page, starting with Exercise MCQs.
  5. Review every incorrect answer and read its explanation carefully.
  6. Repeat the Additional MCQs for any chapter where your score was low.
  7. Closer to your exam, attempt the All MCQs test under timed conditions to build speed.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are Class 9 Physics MCQs?

They are multiple-choice questions covering all nine chapters of the Class 9 Physics course, offered here for chapter-wise or full-book practice with instant results, arranged according to the PECTAA Smart Syllabus.

Which syllabus do these MCQs follow?

These MCQs follow the PECTAA Smart Syllabus (Accelerated Learning Program) for Physics-9, session 2026-27, which applies to all nine BISE boards in Punjab. MCQs related to the deleted topics have been removed.

Can I practise Physics MCQs chapter-wise?

Yes, you can select any of the nine chapters from the dropdown menu and practise that chapter on its own.

Can I choose Exercise or Additional MCQs?

Yes, each chapter has separate Exercise and Additional MCQs, and you can choose to attempt only Exercise, only Additional, or All MCQs.

How can I review wrong answers?

After submitting a test, use the "Review Wrong Answers" button on the results page to see the correct option and explanation for every question you missed.

Are these MCQs useful for Punjab Board preparation?

Yes, they are designed for students preparing for class tests, monthly tests, and the Class 9 Physics Punjab Board exam under the PECTAA Smart Syllabus 2026-27.

What is the PECTAA Smart Syllabus for 9th Class Physics?

It is the Smart Syllabus / Accelerated Learning Program (ALP) for Physics-9, session 2026-27, announced by PECTAA. It reduces selected topics so the course can be completed in time. BISE papers are prepared from the textbook content other than the excluded topics and questions.

Do I need to enter my email to use the Physics MCQs?

Yes, a valid email address is requested once before you start practising. It is used only to send updates about new MCQs and study resources. Your name is optional.
