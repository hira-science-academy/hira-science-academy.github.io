PECTA 2026 SMART SYLLABUS

Grade 9 Mathematics - Reduced Syllabus

In order to facilitate students and enhance focused learning, the content of the Mathematics Grade 9 compulsory textbook has been rationalized. This initiative is designed to help students concentrate on the core Student Learning Outcomes (SLOs) essential for conceptual understanding and effective examination preparation.

The following unit-wise breakdown specifies the exact content, exercises, and examples that have been excluded from the syllabus for the annual examination 2026. Teachers and students are strongly advised to adhere to this Smart Syllabus for all teaching, learning, and assessment activities.

Important Notice

This syllabus applies to the Annual Examination 2026. Students should focus only on the included content as specified in this document. The excluded content will not be part of the final examination.
