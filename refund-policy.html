Refund Policy

Transparent and fair refund and cancellation guidelines for Hira Science Academy

Last updated: June 8, 2026

Our Refund Policy

At Hira Science Academy, our goal is to provide free, high-quality educational resources that support students and teachers in their learning journey. Since our materials are available digitally and free of charge, refund requests typically do not apply. However, in special cases such as voluntary donations or future paid services, we aim to maintain transparency and fairness.

This policy explains our practices regarding refunds, cancellations, and donations. We encourage you to read this policy carefully to understand your rights and our obligations.

1. Free Educational Resources

All downloadable materials, including notes, MCQs, numerical problems, and solved exercises, are provided completely free of cost.

✅ No financial transaction involved — refund policies do not apply

Since there is no purchase or financial transaction involved, refund policies are not applicable for these resources. All content is provided as-is for educational purposes.

  • Notes: Chapter-wise notes for all subjects (Class 9 & 10)
  • MCQs: Multiple-choice questions with answers
  • Numericals: Solved numerical problems for Physics and Mathematics
  • Solved Exercises: Step-by-step solutions to textbook exercises
  • Pairing Schemes: 2026 exam pairing schemes

2. Donations

We gratefully accept donations from supporters who wish to help us expand our educational mission and reach more students across Punjab.

📌 Donations are voluntary and non-refundable

If a donation was made in error or without authorization, please contact us within 7 days of the transaction. We will review your request carefully and issue a refund if it meets our eligibility criteria.

  • Donation Refund Eligibility: Unauthorized transactions, duplicate payments, or technical errors
  • Refund Timeline: 7-10 business days after approval
  • Processing Fee: Any applicable processing fees may be deducted from the refund amount

3. Paid Courses or Services (Future)

In the future, if Hira Science Academy introduces paid courses, premium content, or subscription-based services, detailed refund and cancellation policies will be clearly displayed on the respective course or product pages before purchase.

🔜 Paid services not yet available — policy will be updated when launched

Any future paid services will include:

  • Clear Refund Terms: Specific refund windows and conditions
  • Cancellation Policy: How and when to cancel subscriptions
  • Pro-rated Refunds: For unused portions of subscriptions
  • Contact Information: Dedicated support for refund requests

4. Cancellation Policy

Since our current services are 100% free, there are no paid subscriptions or services to cancel. However, if you have made a donation and wish to cancel or request a refund, please follow the process outlined in Section 2.

For any future paid services, cancellation policies will be clearly communicated at the time of purchase.

5. Dispute Resolution

In the event of a dispute regarding a refund or donation, we encourage you to contact us first to resolve the issue amicably.

  • Step 1: Contact us via email or phone to explain the issue
  • Step 2: We will investigate and respond within 5 business days
  • Step 3: If unresolved, you may escalate the matter to the appropriate consumer protection authority in Pakistan

We are committed to resolving all disputes fairly and in good faith.

6. Policy Updates

This Refund Policy may be revised periodically to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The latest version will always be available on this page with the date of revision mentioned above.

We encourage you to review this policy regularly to stay informed about our refund and cancellation practices.

7. Contact Us

Frequently Asked Questions About Refund Policy

Are all resources on Hira Science Academy free?

Yes, all educational resources including notes, MCQs, numerical problems, and solved exercises are completely free of charge. We believe in making quality education accessible to all students.

Can I get a refund for a donation I made?

Donations are voluntary and generally non-refundable. However, if a donation was made in error or without authorization, please contact us within 7 days. We will review your request on a case-by-case basis.

Do you offer paid courses or services?

Currently, all our services are free. If we introduce paid courses in the future, refund and cancellation policies will be clearly displayed on the respective course pages.

How do I request a refund for a donation?

To request a donation refund, please contact us via email at tayyabgchs@gmail.com with details of the transaction. We will review your request and respond within 5 business days.

How long does it take to process a refund?

If your refund request is approved, we will process the refund within 7-10 business days. The exact timeline may vary depending on your payment method and financial institution.

Who can I contact for refund-related questions?

You can contact us via email at tayyabgchs@gmail.com or by phone at +92 333 8114798. We aim to respond to all inquiries within 2-3 business days.
