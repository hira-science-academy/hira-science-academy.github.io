✅ Multiple Choice Questions & Answer Key (PECTAA 2026)

8.1 Which one of the following is not a magnetic material?
✅ Correct Answer: (C) Aluminium
Aluminium is not a magnetic material. Iron, cobalt, and nickel are ferromagnetic.
8.2 Magnetic lines of force:
✅ Correct Answer: (D) enter into the south pole
Magnetic field lines appear to originate from the north pole and end on the south pole.
8.3 Permanent magnets cannot be made by:
✅ Correct Answer: (A) soft iron
Soft iron is used for temporary magnets, not permanent magnets.
8.4 Permanent magnets are used in:
✅ Correct Answer: (B) loudspeaker
Permanent magnets are used in moving coil loudspeakers.
8.5 A common method used to magnetise a material is:
✅ Correct Answer: (A) stroking
Stroking is a common method used to magnetise a material.
8.6 A magnetic compass is placed around a bar magnet at four points as shown in the figure below. Which diagram would indicate the correct directions of the field?
✅ Correct Answer: (C)
Field lines leave the N-pole and curve around to enter the S-pole. So directly outside the N end the field points away from the magnet, directly outside the S end it points back in toward the magnet, while at the top and bottom (midway along the magnet) the lines curve from the N side across to the S side — exactly the pattern shown in option (C).
8.7 A steel rod is magnetized by the double touch stroking method. Which one would be the correct polarity of the AB magnet?
✅ Correct Answer: (A)
Since the two stroking magnets meet at the centre with unlike poles (S from the left, N from the right), each end of the bar becomes opposite to the pole that stroked onto it — so A (stroked by S) becomes N, and B (stroked by N) becomes S, giving option (A).
8.8 The best material to protect a device from external magnetic field is:
✅ Correct Answer: (D) soft iron
Soft iron has high magnetic permeability and provides magnetic shielding.

📐 Key Concepts – Magnetism (MCQs)

Magnetic Materials: Iron, Cobalt, Nickel (not Aluminium)
Magnetic Lines: Leave N-pole, enter S-pole
Permanent Magnets: Made from steel, neodymium, alnico (not soft iron)
Permanent Magnet Use: Loudspeaker
Magnetization Method: Stroking
Magnetic Shielding: Soft iron (high permeability)
