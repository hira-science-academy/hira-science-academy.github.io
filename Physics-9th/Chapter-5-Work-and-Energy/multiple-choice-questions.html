📐 Multiple Choice Questions & Answers (PECTAA 2026)

5.1 Work done is maximum when the angle between the force \(F\) and the displacement \(d\) is:

✅ Correct Answer: (A) \(0^\circ\)
Work done is maximum when force and displacement are in the same direction (\(\cos 0^\circ = 1\)).

5.2 A joule can also be written as:

✅ Correct Answer: (D) \(\text{kg m}^2\text{s}^{-2}\)
\(1\text{ J} = 1\text{ N} \times 1\text{ m} = \text{kg m}^2\text{s}^{-2}\).

5.3 The SI unit of power is:

✅ Correct Answer: (C) watt
The SI unit of power is watt (W).

5.4 The power of a water pump is \(2\,\text{kW}\). The amount of water it can raise in one minute to a height of \(5\,\text{m}\) is:

✅ Correct Answer: (D) 2400 litres
\(P = \frac{mgh}{t} \Rightarrow m = \frac{Pt}{gh} = \frac{2000 \times 60}{10 \times 5} = 2400\,\text{kg}\) (2400 litres).

5.5 A bullet of mass \(0.05\,\text{kg}\) has a speed of \(300\,\text{ms}^{-1}\). Its kinetic energy will be:

✅ Correct Answer: (A) 2250 J
\(E_k = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 = \frac{1}{2}(0.05)(300)^2 = 2250\,\text{J}\).

5.6 If a car doubles its speed, its kinetic energy will be:

✅ Correct Answer: (D) increased to four times
\(E_k \propto v^2\), so doubling speed increases kinetic energy by \(2^2 = 4\) times.

5.7 The energy possessed by a body by virtue of its position is:

✅ Correct Answer: (B) potential energy
Potential energy is the energy possessed by a body by virtue of its position.

5.8 The magnitude of momentum of an object is doubled, the kinetic energy of the object will:

✅ Correct Answer: (B) increase to four times
\(p = mv\), if \(p\) doubles \(\Rightarrow v\) doubles \(\Rightarrow E_k\) increases to four times.

5.9 Which of the following is not a renewable energy source?

✅ Correct Answer: (B) Fossil fuels
Fossil fuels are non-renewable; hydroelectric, wind, and solar are renewable.

📐 Key Concepts – Work and Energy (MCQs)

Work: \(W = F \times S\) or \(W = FS\cos\theta\)
Kinetic Energy: \(E_k = \frac{1}{2} mv^2\)
Potential Energy: \(E_p = mgh\)
Power: \(P = \frac{W}{t}\)
Efficiency: \(\eta = \frac{\text{Useful output}}{\text{Total input}} \times 100\)
Momentum: \(p = mv\)

💡 Exam Tip:

For MCQs, read each question carefully and eliminate obviously wrong options. Pay attention to key words like "maximum," "minimum," "not," and "always."
