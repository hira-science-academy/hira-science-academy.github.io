✅ Multiple Choice Questions & Answer Key (PECTAA 2026)

9.1 Physics is a branch of:
✅ Correct Answer: (C) Physical science
Physics is an important and basic part of physical sciences.
9.2 Which branch of science plays vital role in technology and engineering?
✅ Correct Answer: (D) Physics
Physics plays a vital role being the core of each invention based on physical laws and principles.
9.3 Automobile technology is based on:
✅ Correct Answer: (D) thermodynamics
Automobile technology is based on the principles of thermodynamics.
9.4 A user friendly software application of smart phone use:
✅ Correct Answer: (B) information technology
A software programme of information technology.
9.5 The working of refrigeration and air conditioning involves:
✅ Correct Answer: (D) thermodynamics
Refrigeration and air conditioning involve thermal energy transfer.
9.6 What is the ultimate truth of a scientific method?
✅ Correct Answer: (D) Law
When a theory has been tested many times and generally accepted as true, it is called a law.
9.7 The statement "If I do not study for this test, then I will not get good grade" is an example of:
✅ Correct Answer: (C) prediction
After the careful analysis of a theory, we can make predictions about certain unknown aspects of nature.
9.8 Which of the following are methods of investigation?
✅ Correct Answer: (D) All of these
Observation, experimentation, and research are all methods of investigation.
9.9 A hypothesis:
✅ Correct Answer: (D) all of these
It must be testable and is tested through experiments to collect evidence.
9.10 A graph of an organized data is an example of:
✅ Correct Answer: (D) analyzing data
Record, organize and analyze gathered data, plotting and interpreting graphs.
9.11 The colour of a door is brown. It is an example of:
✅ Correct Answer: (A) observation
The first step in scientific method is to make observations.
