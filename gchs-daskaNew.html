Historical Legacy of GCHS Daska

Established 1882 | An Anchor of Education in Punjab

Established in 1882, Govt. Christian High School Daska is one of the oldest educational institutions in Pakistan. Founded as Daska Mission High School under the Church of Scotland, it has served as a beacon of education for over 140 years. The historic foundation stone was laid on 26 March 1915 by E.R. Abbott, Deputy Commissioner.
