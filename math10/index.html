Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions about this permanent Math 10 resource library.

What is this Class 10 Mathematics resource library?▾

This is a permanent, year-independent Class 10 Mathematics resource library. Each URL remains stable across syllabus updates, so links to chapter hubs and exercises stay valid as long as the content remains relevant.

How many chapters are covered in this library?▾

All 12 chapters of Class 10 Mathematics are covered — from Complex Numbers through Probability — each with its own dedicated hub page linking to every exercise, review exercise, and related resource.

Is this library different from the Class 10 Maths Notes page?▾

Yes. This library provides permanent, year-independent URLs and resources. The Class 10 Maths Notes hub is the year-specific landing page covering the current PECTAA syllabus, MCQs practice test, and year-wise exam information.

Which BISE boards does this library cover?▾

The content follows the unified Punjab Board syllabus and is suitable for all 10 BISE boards in Punjab, including Lahore, Gujranwala, Multan, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Sargodha, DG Khan, Bahawalpur, and Sahiwal.

Are these resources free to access?▾

Yes, all chapter hubs, solved exercises, review exercises, and related resources in this library are completely free to access online. No login or registration is required.

Will the URLs in this library change?▾

No. This library was specifically designed with permanent, stable URLs. Once a chapter or exercise is published at a URL, that URL stays consistent across syllabus revisions.
