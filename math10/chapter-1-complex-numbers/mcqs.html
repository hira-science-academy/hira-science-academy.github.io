1. \( \iota^2 + \iota^4 = \) ?

✅ Correct Answer: (B) 0
\( \iota^2 = -1 \) and \( \iota^4 = (\iota^2)^2 = (-1)^2 = 1 \). Therefore, \( \iota^2 + \iota^4 = -1 + 1 = 0 \).

2. Real part of \( (2 - 3\iota)(2 + 3\iota) \) is:

✅ Correct Answer: (D) 13
\( (2-3\iota)(2+3\iota) = (2)^2 - (3\iota)^2 = 4 - 9\iota^2 = 4 - 9(-1) = 4 + 9 = 13 \). Real part is 13.

3. Imaginary part of \( (2 - \iota)(2 + \iota) \) is:

✅ Correct Answer: (A) 0
\( (2-\iota)(2+\iota) = (2)^2 - (\iota)^2 = 4 - (-1) = 5 = 5 + 0\iota \). Imaginary part is 0.

4. \( x + \iota y \) will be a pure imaginary number, when:

✅ Correct Answer: (B) \(x = 0\)
A pure imaginary number has real part equal to zero, so \( x = 0 \).

5. What is the additive inverse of \( 5 - 2\iota \) ?

✅ Correct Answer: (D) \(-5 + 2\iota\)
Additive inverse of \( a + b\iota \) is \( -a - b\iota \). So additive inverse of \( 5 - 2\iota \) is \( -5 + 2\iota \).

6. What is the multiplicative inverse of \( z = 1 + \iota \) ?

✅ Correct Answer: (D) \(\frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{2}\iota\)
\( z^{-1} = \frac{1}{1+\iota} \times \frac{1-\iota}{1-\iota} = \frac{1-\iota}{1-\iota^2} = \frac{1-\iota}{1+1} = \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{2}\iota \).

7. If \( z = 4 - 3\iota \), then \( z\overline{z} = \) ?

✅ Correct Answer: (D) 25
\( z\overline{z} = |z|^2 = (4)^2 + (-3)^2 = 16 + 9 = 25 \).

8. The conjugate of \( 9 - 4\iota \) is:

✅ Correct Answer: (B) \(9 + 4\iota\)
Conjugate of \( a + b\iota \) is \( a - b\iota \). So conjugate of \( 9 - 4\iota \) is \( 9 + 4\iota \).

9. If \( z = 4 + 4\iota \), then \( z + \overline{z} = \) ?

✅ Correct Answer: (A) 8
\( z + \overline{z} = (4+4\iota) + (4-4\iota) = 8 \).

10. If \( z = 5 + 4\iota \), then \( |z| = \) ?

✅ Correct Answer: (D) \(\sqrt{41}\)
\( |z| = \sqrt{5^2 + 4^2} = \sqrt{25 + 16} = \sqrt{41} \).

11. \( \iota^{37} = \) ?

✅ Correct Answer: (C) \(\iota\)
\( \iota^{37} = \iota^{36}\cdot\iota = (\iota^2)^{18}\cdot\iota = (-1)^{18}\iota = \iota \).

12. \( \iota^{13} \times \iota^{11} = \) ?

✅ Correct Answer: (D) 1
\( \iota^{13} \times \iota^{11} = \iota^{13+11} = \iota^{24} = (\iota^2)^{12} = (-1)^{12} = 1 \).

13. For complex numbers \( z_1 = a + b\iota \) and \( z_2 = c + d\iota \), \( z_1 = z_2 \) if and only if:

✅ Correct Answer: (A) \(a = c\) and \(b = d\)
Two complex numbers are equal if and only if their real parts are equal and their imaginary parts are equal.

14. \( (-\iota)^{-9} = \) ?

✅ Correct Answer: (B) \(\iota\)
\( (-\iota)^{-9} = \frac{1}{(-\iota)^9} = \frac{1}{(-1)^9 \iota^9} = \frac{1}{-\iota^8 \cdot \iota} = \frac{1}{-\iota} = \frac{\iota}{-\iota^2} = \frac{\iota}{1} = \iota \).

15. \( (3 - 4\iota)(5 - 6\iota) = \) ?

✅ Correct Answer: (C) \(-9 - 38\iota\)
\( (3-4\iota)(5-6\iota) = 15 - 18\iota - 20\iota + 24\iota^2 = 15 - 38\iota - 24 = -9 - 38\iota \).

16. \( (3+4\iota) \div (5-7\iota) = \) ?

✅ Correct Answer: (A) \(-\dfrac{13}{74} + \dfrac{41}{74}\iota\)
Multiply by conjugate \(5+7\iota\): \( \dfrac{(3+4\iota)(5+7\iota)}{5^2+7^2} = \dfrac{15+21\iota+20\iota+28\iota^2}{74} = \dfrac{15+41\iota-28}{74} = \dfrac{-13+41\iota}{74} \).

17. The multiplicative inverse of \( z = 8 + 9\iota \) is:

✅ Correct Answer: (B) \(\dfrac{8-9\iota}{145}\)
\( z^{-1} = \dfrac{1}{8+9\iota} \times \dfrac{8-9\iota}{8-9\iota} = \dfrac{8-9\iota}{64-81\iota^2} = \dfrac{8-9\iota}{64+81} = \dfrac{8-9\iota}{145} \).

18. If \( z_1 = 3 + 4\iota \) and \( z_2 = 2 + 3\iota \), then \( \overline{z_1 + z_2} = \) ?

✅ Correct Answer: (C) \(5 - 7\iota\)
\( z_1 + z_2 = 5 + 7\iota \). Conjugate is \( 5 - 7\iota \).

19. If \( z = \dfrac{1}{2+7\iota} \), then \( \text{Re}(z) \) and \( \text{Im}(z) \) are:

✅ Correct Answer: (A)
\( \dfrac{1}{2+7\iota} \times \dfrac{2-7\iota}{2-7\iota} = \dfrac{2-7\iota}{4-49\iota^2} = \dfrac{2-7\iota}{4+49} = \dfrac{2-7\iota}{53} \). So \( \text{Re} = \frac{2}{53} \) and \( \text{Im} = -\frac{7}{53} \).

20. If \( (3-4\iota)(a+b\iota) = 1 \), then \( a \) and \( b \) are:

✅ Correct Answer: (B) \(a = \dfrac{3}{25}, b = \dfrac{4}{25}\)
\( a + b\iota = \dfrac{1}{3-4\iota} = \dfrac{3+4\iota}{9+16} = \dfrac{3+4\iota}{25} = \dfrac{3}{25} + \dfrac{4}{25}\iota \).
