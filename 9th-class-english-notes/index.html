What Is in These Class 9 English Notes?

These Class 9 English notes are made for Punjab Board students preparing for the BISE annual exam. One PDF is split into eight sections so you can open only the part you need: summaries, letters, questions and answers for Units 1–11, word sentences, change of voice, comprehension, correct use of verbs, and tenses.

Every section opens on its own page with an online viewer and a free download button, so you can read on a phone or save the PDF for offline study. Start with the summaries to recall each unit, work through the questions and answers, then practise grammar and writing using the remaining sections.

The booklet was prepared by Waqar Sharif (SST) in simplified learning versions and clear step-by-step formats, so average students can understand and memorise the content easily. It includes poem summaries, 15 exam-format letters, questions and answers for Units 1–11, vocabulary tables with Urdu meanings, 33 active and passive voice pairs, 25 comprehension passages with answer hints, 20 verb formulas and Urdu-to-English tense translations. It comes from the school notes of Waqar Sharif, SST, Government Christian High School Daska.

These notes are written to help average students prepare efficiently and aim for the best marks they can in the annual exam, under any of the nine BISE Punjab boards: Lahore, Gujranwala, Faisalabad, Rawalpindi, Multan, Sargodha, Bahawalpur, DG Khan and Sahiwal.

For exam planning, use these notes together with the Class 9 English pairing scheme, the smart syllabus and the model papers. These notes are study support, not a replacement for your textbook.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I download Class 9 English Notes 2026 PDF?+
In the Free PDF Download section on this page, or on any of the eight section pages. Each has a Download PDF button, and reading online is free.
Are these notes suitable for Punjab Board students?+
They are prepared for Class 9 Punjab Board students and cover the textbook units, letters and grammar that are asked in BISE exams. Check them against your own textbook and your teacher’s guidance.
Do the notes include English-to-Urdu translation?+
Partly. The Word Sentences tables (pages 27–31) give an Urdu meaning for every word, and the Grammar Tenses pages (50–51) pair Urdu sentences with their English translations. The booklet is not a full Urdu translation of the textbook.
Are Class 9 English questions and answers included?+
Yes. Pages 09–26 give more than 120 questions and answers for Units 1–11, including box questions and Reading and Critical Thinking questions.
Can I download Class 9 English Guide PDF for free?+
Yes. The notes are free to read and download, with no account needed.
Are grammar and tenses included?+
Yes. Change of voice (33 sentence pairs) is on pages 32–33, correct use of verbs (20 formulas with MCQs) on 47–49, and grammar tenses on 50–51.
Are these notes useful for BISE examinations?+
They are exam-preparation material: exam-format letters, answers to textbook questions, comprehension passages and grammar rules. Use them with the pairing scheme and model papers. They do not guarantee which questions appear.
Do these notes cover all eleven English units?+
Yes. The questions and answers cover Units 1–11, and the vocabulary tables cover all 11 lessons.
Is there an answer key for the verb MCQs?+
Yes. The Correct Use of Verbs PDF ends with an answer key for all 20 example MCQs.
Is there a test or practice booklet with these notes?+
Yes. The Class 9 English Assessment & Practice Book (Units 1–11) has a word list, 124 questions, a 33-sentence passive voice test and 25 comprehension passages. It has questions only, and the answers are in the notes.
Who prepared these notes?+
They were prepared by Waqar Sharif, SST, Government Christian High School Daska, and published by Hira Science Academy.
