About these notes

Pages 34–46 give solved reading passages followed by comprehension questions. The passages are classic moral and historical pieces, and the booklet adds italicised teacher’s marks and hints that show how to frame an answer and where to find it in the text.

Read the passage, try the questions yourself, then compare with the solved answer and the hint. Make sure each answer can be traced to a line in the passage.

Comprehension is written for average students who want to score as many marks as they can in this part of the paper, under any of the nine BISE Punjab boards: Lahore, Gujranwala, Faisalabad, Rawalpindi, Multan, Sargodha, Bahawalpur, DG Khan and Sahiwal.

How to study this section

  • Read once for the gist, then read the questions.
  • Underline the lines that answer each question.
  • Answer in complete sentences from the passage.
  • Use the teacher’s hints to see how marks are earned.

What Is Inside This PDF

From the Class 9 English Complete Study Booklet prepared by Waqar Sharif (SST), pages 34–46:

  • Solved passages, including The Hungry Wolf, The Thirsty Fox, King Robert Bruce, Boycott of Banu Hashim, Hazrat Khalid Bin Waleed, Allama Iqbal, Early Rising and Social Ants.
  • Comprehension questions with solved answers.
  • Teacher’s marks and hints on how to frame answers and locate the target information.
