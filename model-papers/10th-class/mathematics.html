Objective Type

Max. Marks: 15 | Time allowed: 20 Min.

Note: Encircle the correct answer. Each question carries one mark. Cutting or overwriting in an MCQ cancels that answer. (1 × 15 = 15)

  1. Imaginary part of (2 − i)(2 + i) is:
    (A) 0    (B) 1    (C) 7    (D) 9
  2. Sum of the roots of 3x2 + 5x − 12 = 0 is:
    (A) 53    (B) −53    (C) 123    (D) 35
  3. If A = 313202, then order of At is:
    (A) 3-by-2    (B) 2-by-3    (C) 3-by-3    (D) 2-by-2
  4. If f(x) = x2 − 4, g(x) = x + 2, x ≠ −2, then f(x)g(x) =
    (A) 1x − 2    (B) 1x + 2    (C) x + 2    (D) x − 2
  5. Lowest form of 18a2bc212ac is:
    (A) 9abc6    (B) 3abc2    (C) 18b12    (D) 32
  6. What is the value of |3i + 4j| ?
    (A) 3    (B) 4    (C) 5    (D) 7
  7. cosec θ is positive in:
    (A) I & III quadrants    (B) II & IV quadrants    (C) I & II quadrants    (D) I & IV quadrants
  8. A chord passing through the centre of the circle is called:
    (A) radius    (B) diameter    (C) secant    (D) circumference
  9. In a circle, radius and tangent are:
    (A) parallel    (B) equal    (C) perpendicular    (D) zero
  10. What is the measure of an angle inscribed in a semi-circle?
    (A) 45°    (B) 60°    (C) 90°    (D) 120°
  11. Tangents drawn from a single external point to a circle are:
    (A) unequal in length    (B) perpendicular to each other    (C) equal in length    (D) inside the circle
  12. Difference between the highest and the lowest values is called:
    (A) mean    (B) variance    (C) range    (D) standard deviation
  13. Variance of the data 8, 8, 8, 8, 8, 8 is:
    (A) 0    (B) 16    (C) 8    (D) 48
  14. If one coin is tossed and one dice is rolled, then the number of sample points are:
    (A) 3    (B) 6    (C) 2    (D) 12
  15. If P(E) = 0.07, then the probability of ‘not E’ is:
    (A) 0.95    (B) 0.89    (C) 0.93    (D) 0.90

Subjective Type (Part I)

Max. Marks: 60 | Time allowed: 2:10 Hrs.

Q.2: Attempt any SIX of the following: (Units 1, 2, 3 & 4) (2 × 6 = 12)

  1. Simplify: (i8i5)−5
  2. Find the values of a and b, if (a + bi)(1 + 3i) = −8 + 11i.
  3. Solve the quadratic equation by factorization method: 6x2 + 13x − 5 = 0
  4. Form a quadratic equation whose roots are 3 + √5, 3 − √5.
  5. If the quadratic equation 16x2 + 7px + 49 = 0 has equal roots, then find the values of p.
  6. If X = 1−1−22 and Z = 300−2, then find 4X − Z.
  7. Find the value of x when A = x6515 is a singular matrix.
  8. Given that f(x) = 3x + 2 and g(x) = 2x + 3. Find f(g(−2)).
  9. Solve and express the solution on number line: |7 − 2x| = 1

Q.3: Attempt any SIX of the following: (Units 5, 6, 7 & 8) (6 × 2 = 12)

  1. Reduce to lowest form: 3x2 + 6xx2 + 4x + 4
  2. Simplify: 2x + 2y − x − 6yx2 − 4y2
  3. Write the vector AB in the form xi + yj, where A(1, −7) and B(−2, 4).
  4. Find a unit vector in the direction of the vector a = 6i + 8j.
  5. If a = i + 3j, c = 2i + j and a + 2b = c, then find |b|.
  6. Without using calculator, find the exact value of cot 120°.
  7. Calculate area of triangle ABC, in which a = 7 cm, b = 8 cm, γ = 38°.
  8. A chord 8 cm long is at a distance of 3 cm from the centre. Calculate the radius of the circle.
  9. In a circle with centre at O, the perpendicular distance of each chord PQ and RS from the centre is 6 cm. If the length of chord PQ is 18 cm, find the length of the other chord.

Q.4: Attempt any SIX of the following: (Units 9, 10, 11 & 12) (6 × 2 = 12)

  1. In a historical monument, a circular fountain with a radius of 3 m is built. A flagpole is erected 7 m away from the centre of the fountain. Two ropes from the pole are tied to the edge of the fountain, just touching it. Find the length of each rope.
  2. Find ℓ and area of sector, when r = 1.7 cm, θ = 0.25 radian.
  3. Construct a circle with the help of given radius r = 1.7 cm.
  4. Take any three non-collinear points P, Q, R and construct a circle passing through these points.
  5. Draw the box-and-whisker plot, if min. value = 12, max. value = 26, median = 19, Q1 = 14 and Q3 = 24.
  6. Find the range of the data set: 63, 89, 98, 125, 79, 108, 117, 60
  7. A librarian recorded the number of visitors during 5 days of a week: 120, 135, 130, 125, 140. Calculate the variance and standard deviation of visitors.
  8. Find the probability of getting a sum of 5 or 7 in a throw of two dice.
  9. If A and B are two mutually exclusive events of a random experiment and P(not A) = 0.45, P(A ∪ B) = 0.65, then find P(B).

Subjective Type (Part II)

Note: Attempt any TWO questions from Q.5 to Q.7. Each part carries 4 marks. (2 × 8 = 16)

Q.5:

  • (a) Find the real and imaginary parts of the complex number (2 − i2 + i)−2. (4)
  • (b) Use quadratic formula to solve the equation: 3x − 3x + 1 = 2x − 1x − 1, x ≠ −1, 1. (4)

Q.6:

  • (a) Use Cramer’s rule to solve the following pair of linear equations: x + 2y = 7, 3x − 2y = −3. (4)
  • (b) The functions f and g are defined such that f(x) = 4x + 2 and g(x) = 6x − 18. (i) Find f−1(x) and g−1(x). (ii) Find x if f−1(x) = g−1(x). (4)

Q.7:

  • (a) Solve the equation: 4x4 − 27x2 + 18 = 0. (4)
  • (b) Solve the triangle ABC, in which b = 4.8 cm, a = 4 cm, β = 71°. (4)

Subjective Type (Part III)

Note: Attempt any ONE question from Q.8 and Q.9. Each part carries 4 marks. (1 × 8 = 8)

Q.8:

  • (a) If a = 3i − j, b = −2i + 4j and c = i + 2j, then find unit vector parallel to 3a − 2c + 4b. (4)
  • (b) Machine A Output (units): 98, 100, 102, 101, 99. Machine B Output (units): 95, 100, 105, 90, 110. (i) Which machine has better performance? (ii) Which machine is more consistent? (4)

Q.9:

  • (a) Draw a circle of radius 1.5 cm and draw a tangent at point P, when P is at a distance of 8 cm from its centre. (4)
  • (b) A dice is rolled and coin is tossed together. (i) Find sample space by drawing possibility diagram. (ii) Find sample space by sketching tree diagram. (iii) What is a probability of getting a tail and an even number? (4)
