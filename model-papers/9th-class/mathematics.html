📘 How to Attempt the Class 9 Math Model Paper 2027

This model paper is designed to simulate the real Punjab Board Mathematics exam 2027. It strictly follows the PECTAA blueprint, ensuring you practice the correct distribution of MCQs, short questions, and long questions. Use it as a serious self-assessment tool to identify which chapters are exam-ready.

Attempt the paper in one continuous sitting with a timer set to 2 hours 30 minutes. Do not consult your textbook. Mark yourself strictly afterwards. For long questions, remember that Part (a) is 4 marks and Part (b) is 4 marks — show all your working, formulas, and steps to secure partial marks.

For best results, attempt this paper at least once before your final revision. Pair it with the official Mathematics pairing scheme and smart syllabus status to ensure you are practicing only what is examinable.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Math model paper aligned with the 2027 syllabus? ▼
Yes. This model paper is prepared strictly according to the PECTAA 2027 curriculum and the latest pairing scheme for Annual Examination 2027.
Does the paper follow the official Punjab Board pattern? ▼
Yes. It follows the exact pattern: 75 marks total, 15 MCQs, 18 short questions, and 3 long questions, with a total duration of 2 hours 30 minutes.
Can I download this Math model paper as a PDF? ▼
Yes. Use the Download PDF button above to get the full paper as a PDF. You can also print it directly from the page.
Are the long questions divided into parts? ▼
Yes, Q.5, Q.6, Q.7 have Part (a) = 4 marks and Part (b) = 4 marks. Q.8 and Q.9 have Part (a) = 4 marks and Part (b) = 4 marks. You must attempt any 2 of Q.5-Q.7 and any 1 of Q.8-Q.9.
