What this length converter does

This tool converts between nine common units of length: millimeters, centimeters, meters (metres), kilometers, inches, feet, yards, miles and nautical miles. Enter a value, choose the units, and the answer appears as you type. The United States mostly uses inches, feet, yards and miles. The UK uses miles on its roads but meters and centimeters in schools and trades. Canada uses kilometers on road signs, while many people still give height in feet and inches. This page covers all of these, with exact conversion factors, a height converter, charts and everyday examples.

How the conversion works

General length conversion
result = value × meters per from-unitmeters per to-unit

Every unit has a fixed length in meters. To convert, the calculator turns your value into meters and then into the unit you want. Going through the meter keeps every pair of units consistent, so converting meters to feet and back returns the starting number.

Since the 1959 international yard and pound agreement, the inch, foot, yard and mile have been defined exactly in terms of the meter. The older US survey foot (1200/3937 m) was retired in the US at the end of 2022, and this converter uses the international foot. Shortcut factors such as 3.28084 (feet per meter) and 0.621371 (miles per kilometer) are rounded, so the calculator uses the exact definitions above.

Worked examples

Example 1: 10 meters to feet

  1. Convert to meters: 10 m.
  2. Divide by the foot: 10 ÷ 0.3048 = 32.8084.
  3. 10 m ≈ 32.81 ft.

Example 2: 100 kilometers to miles

  1. 100 km = 100,000 m.
  2. 100,000 ÷ 1,609.344 = 62.1371.
  3. 100 km ≈ 62.14 mi. Canadian road signs use kilometers, while US and UK signs use miles.

Example 3: 30 centimeters to inches

  1. 30 cm = 0.30 m.
  2. 0.30 ÷ 0.0254 = 11.811.
  3. 30 cm ≈ 11.81 in, just under a foot (a school ruler).

Example 4: Height of 5 ft 10 in in centimeters

  1. Total inches = 5 × 12 + 10 = 70.
  2. 70 × 2.54 = 177.8.
  3. 5 ft 10 in = 177.8 cm = 1.778 m.

Example 5: 1 mile to feet

  1. 1 mile = 1,609.344 m and 1 ft = 0.3048 m.
  2. 1,609.344 ÷ 0.3048 = 5,280.
  3. 1 mi = 5,280 ft exactly.

Example 6: A 5K race in miles

  1. 5 km = 5,000 m.
  2. 5,000 ÷ 1,609.344 = 3.10686.
  3. 5 km ≈ 3.11 mi.

Example 7: A 100-yard football field in meters

  1. 100 yd × 0.9144 m per yard.
  2. 100 yd = 91.44 m exactly.

Example 8: A marathon in miles

  1. 42.195 km = 42,195 m.
  2. 42,195 ÷ 1,609.344 = 26.2188.
  3. A marathon is about 26.22 miles (commonly rounded to 26.2).

Three values are worth memorizing: 5,280 feet in a mile, 12 inches in a foot and 3 feet in a yard. Together with 1 inch = 2.54 cm, they cover most everyday conversions.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Mixing up statute and nautical miles. A statute mile is 1,609.344 m and a nautical mile is 1,852 m, about 15% longer. Aviation and marine problems use nautical miles.
  • Reading 5 ft 10 in as 5.10 feet. It is 5 + 10/12 = 5.833 feet, or 70 inches, which is 177.8 cm.
  • Forgetting that area and volume do not convert linearly. 1 m² is 10,000 cm², not 100 cm², and 1 m³ is 1,000,000 cm³. Use the area and volume converters for those.
  • Converting in the wrong direction. An inch is longer than a centimeter, so 30 cm is about 11.8 in, a smaller number. If centimeters to inches gives a bigger number, you divided the wrong way.
  • Rounding too early. Keep full precision and round only the final answer.
  • Treating shortcuts as exact. 1 km ≈ 0.6 mi is fine for a rough estimate, but the precise factor is 0.621371.

FAQ

How do I convert meters to feet?▾

Divide the length in meters by 0.3048, or multiply by about 3.28084. For example, 10 m = 10 ÷ 0.3048 ≈ 32.81 ft. One foot is defined as exactly 0.3048 m.

How do I convert kilometers to miles?▾

Multiply kilometers by about 0.621371, or divide by 1.609344. For example, 5 km ≈ 3.11 miles. The statute mile is defined as exactly 1,609.344 m.

How do I convert centimeters to inches?▾

Divide centimeters by 2.54, or multiply by about 0.393701. For example, 30 cm ≈ 11.81 in. One inch is exactly 2.54 cm.

How do I convert feet and inches to centimeters?▾

Convert the height to total inches (feet × 12 + inches) and multiply by 2.54. For example, 5 ft 10 in = 70 in × 2.54 = 177.8 cm. The height converter on this page does this automatically.

How many feet are in a mile?▾

There are exactly 5,280 feet in a statute mile, because 1,609.344 m ÷ 0.3048 m = 5,280. A mile also equals 1,760 yards.

What is the difference between a statute mile and a nautical mile?▾

A statute mile is 1,609.344 m and is used for road distances in the US and UK. A nautical mile is exactly 1,852 m (about 15% longer) and is used in aviation and marine navigation.

Are these conversion factors exact?▾

The inch (2.54 cm), foot (0.3048 m), yard (0.9144 m) and statute mile (1,609.344 m) are exact definitions that have been in place since the 1959 international yard and pound agreement. Shortcut factors such as 3.28084 or 0.621371 are rounded, so the calculator uses the exact definitions.

Which countries use miles, feet and meters?▾

The US uses miles, feet and inches in everyday life. The UK uses miles on roads and meters in many other settings. Canada uses kilometers on road signs, although many Canadians still give height in feet and inches.

What is the US survey foot?▾

The US survey foot (1200/3937 m) was a slightly different foot used in land surveying. It was retired in the US at the end of 2022, and everyday conversions use the international foot of exactly 0.3048 m, as this converter does.
