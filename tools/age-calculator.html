What this age calculator does

This tool works out your exact age from your date of birth to today, or to any other date you choose. It shows your age in years, months and days, plus your total age in months, weeks, days and hours. It also shows the day of the week you were born on and how many days remain until your next birthday. Leap years and the different lengths of months are handled for you.

How to calculate age from a date of birth

Age is counted in calendar units, not by dividing a number of days by 365. The calculator follows these steps:

Steps
  1. Subtract the birth year from the current year to get a first estimate of the years.
  2. Subtract the birth month from the current month. If the current day of the month is earlier than the birth day, borrow one month.
  3. Subtract the days, adding the length of the previous month when you borrowed a month. If months go below zero, borrow one year.
  4. For the totals, count the exact days between the two dates using UTC, so daylight saving time cannot shift the result.

Worked examples

Example 1: Born August 15, 2005

Calculated on October 9, 2026

  1. August 15, 2005 to August 15, 2026 is exactly 21 years.
  2. August 15 to September 15, 2026 is 1 month. September 15 to October 9 is 24 days.
  3. Result: 21 years, 1 month, 24 days, which is 7,725 days in total.

Example 2: A baby born June 20, 2026

Calculated on October 11, 2026

  1. June 20 to September 20 is 3 months.
  2. September 20 to October 11 is 21 days.
  3. Result: 0 years, 3 months, 21 days (113 days, or 16 weeks and 1 day). Parents and pediatric forms often use age in months, so the total months figure is handy.

Example 3: Born February 29, 2004

Calculated on February 28, 2025

  1. February 29, 2004 to January 29, 2025 is 20 years and 11 months.
  2. January 29 to February 28, 2025 is 30 days.
  3. Result: 20 years, 11 months, 30 days (7,670 days). In non-leap years, the day a leap-day birthday officially counts (February 28 or March 1) can differ by state or organization, so check the rule that applies to you.

Common uses

  • Checking whether you or your child meets an age requirement on a deadline, such as a kindergarten cutoff date, a sports league or a program start date.
  • Filling out forms that ask for your exact age on a specific date, such as applications, insurance and benefits paperwork.
  • Finding a baby's age in months and weeks.
  • Working out how old someone was or will be on a historical or future date.
  • Counting down to your next birthday or planning a milestone.

FAQ

How do I calculate my exact age from my date of birth?▾

Enter your date of birth and leave the second date as today. The calculator subtracts the dates in calendar units: full years first, then the remaining months, then the remaining days, adjusting for the different lengths of months and for leap years.

How old am I in months and days?▾

The result shows your age as years, months and days, and also gives your total age in months, weeks, days and hours. For example, a baby born on June 20, 2026 is 3 months and 21 days old on October 11, 2026, which is 113 days.

Can I calculate my age on a specific date in the past or future?▾

Yes. Change the "Calculate age as of" date to any day. This is useful for checking whether you meet an age requirement on a deadline, such as a school enrollment cutoff, a tournament date or a program start date.

How does the calculator handle leap years and February 29 birthdays?▾

It counts exact days using UTC dates, so leap years are handled correctly. For people born on February 29, this calculator follows strict calendar arithmetic. Which day counts as the birthday in non-leap years can vary by state or organization, so check the rule that applies to you for official purposes.

Why might another age calculator show a different result?▾

Some calculators count the day of birth as day one, use the local time zone, or treat month lengths differently. This tool uses strict calendar arithmetic on dates only, with no time zone, which matches how age is normally counted on forms and documents.

What is the difference between age in years and total days?▾

Age in years, months and days is the calendar age you would write on a form, such as 21 years, 1 month, 24 days. Total days is the exact number of days between the two dates, which is useful for milestones or record keeping.

Is my date of birth saved or sent anywhere?▾

No. The calculation runs in your web browser and the date you enter is not stored or tracked.
