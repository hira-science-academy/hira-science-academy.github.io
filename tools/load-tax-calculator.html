Mobile Balance Calculator for Pakistan

If you recharge Rs 100 and receive less than Rs 100 in your account, the difference is tax. This calculator shows you exactly how much withholding tax is deducted from your mobile load and what balance is credited to your account. It works for all major Pakistani prepaid networks: Jazz, Telenor, Zong and Ufone.

Enter your recharge amount, click calculate, and the tool will show the tax deducted and the balance you receive after the first deduction. The result reflects what actually lands in your account immediately after loading.

How Mobile Recharge Tax Is Calculated in Pakistan

Mobile recharge in Pakistan involves deductions that happen at different stages:

  1. Advance Withholding Tax: This is deducted immediately when you recharge. On Rs 100, Rs 13.04 is deducted, leaving Rs 86.96 credited to your account. This tax is collected under the Income Tax Ordinance and applies equally to all networks and to both filers and non-filers.
  2. Sales Tax on Usage: After the withholding tax is deducted, the remaining balance is subject to a 19.5% sales tax on telecom services. For example, if you have Rs 86.96 credited, a further Rs 14.19 is deducted upon usage, leaving about Rs 72.77 as the final usable balance.
Important: Your network will show the balance as Rs 86.96 immediately after recharge on a Rs 100 load. The calculator on this page shows the balance credited after the first deduction, which is the amount you see in your account immediately after loading. The sales tax is applied when you actually make calls or use data.

How to Calculate the Recharge Needed for a Mobile Package

If you want to activate a package that costs Rs 450 but your current balance is only Rs 25, you need to load enough so that the balance after tax covers the package cost. Use the Package Calculator tab above. Enter the package cost and your current balance. The calculator works out the gross recharge required so that the amount remaining after the initial withholding tax is sufficient to cover the package.

Network Compatibility

The withholding tax applies uniformly across all Pakistani telecom operators. Whether you use Jazz, Telenor, Zong, or Ufone, the deduction logic is the same. This calculator therefore produces the same result regardless of which network you use.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much balance do I get on a Rs 100 mobile load?▾

You receive Rs 86.96 after the withholding tax (Rs 13.04) is deducted.

What is the current mobile load tax rate in Pakistan?▾

The withholding tax deduction on mobile load calculates exactly to Rs 13.04 deducted per Rs 100 recharged, leaving Rs 86.96 credited to your account.

Does the tax rate differ for Jazz, Telenor, Zong and Ufone?▾

No. The standard withholding tax applies equally to all prepaid networks in Pakistan. The deduction is identical regardless of your operator.

Why did I receive Rs 86.96 on a Rs 100 load?▾

Rs 100 minus the withholding tax deduction of Rs 13.04 equals Rs 86.96. This is the amount credited immediately after the load.

How do I calculate the recharge needed for a package?▾

Use the Package Calculator tab. Enter the package cost and current balance. The calculator determines the gross recharge required so that the balance after tax covers the package cost.
