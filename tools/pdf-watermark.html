Add a watermark to a PDF online, with a live preview

A watermark is text or a picture placed on every page, or on chosen pages, of a document. Teachers use it to mark worksheets, designers use it to protect sample layouts, and offices stamp DRAFT or CONFIDENTIAL before a file leaves the building. This free PDF watermark tool lets you do that in your browser. The preview shows the page you are working on, so you see where the mark will fall before you create anything.

The watermark is written into the pages of the new PDF. It is not a layer that exists only on your screen, so it is still there when you email, print or upload the file. Your original PDF is never changed; you download a new copy.

Text or image

  • Text watermark. Type one or several lines, choose Helvetica, Times or Courier, then set size, colour, bold, italic and alignment. Quick buttons fill in common words like DRAFT, SAMPLE and COPYRIGHT.
  • Image watermark. Use a PNG or JPG logo. Transparent PNG files keep their see-through areas. You control size, opacity and rotation just as with text.

How to watermark a PDF in five steps

  1. Choose your PDF. The preview of page 1 appears next to the settings, with a pager to look at other pages.
  2. Pick Text or Image or logo and enter your text or choose your file.
  3. Adjust opacity, rotation and position. Choose a single watermark or a repeated pattern, and decide whether it sits above or below the page content.
  4. Choose the pages: all, odd, even, a range, specific pages or all except some.
  5. Name the new file, press Add watermark, then Download watermarked PDF.

Settings that make a watermark look professional

Opacity and colour

A watermark should be seen without hiding the page. Grey at 25–40% opacity works on most documents. Use a stronger colour only when the mark must be obvious, such as a red DRAFT on an unfinished report.

Rotation and position

A diagonal mark across the centre is hard to crop out and reads clearly. A small, horizontal logo in a corner is subtler and suits letterheads and certificates. The nine-point grid sets both horizontal and vertical alignment, and the edge-distance slider keeps the mark clear of the page border.

Single or tiled

A tiled watermark repeats the text or logo across the page in a staggered pattern. It is harder to avoid by cropping and is common on previews and samples. Increase the spacing if the pattern feels crowded.

Above or below the content

With Above the content the watermark is drawn last, so it is always visible. With Below the content it is drawn first, and the page’s own text sits on top of it. Below works well for light backgrounds, but wherever a page has a solid fill, such as a scanned page that is one big image, the watermark is hidden. If you cannot see the mark in the preview, switch to Above.

Choosing the pages

  • All pages for a consistent mark through the whole file.
  • Odd or even pages for double-sided layouts, where a mark sits on right-hand pages only.
  • A range such as pages 3 to 10, or specific pages such as 1, 4, 9.
  • All except to skip a cover page, a title page or an appendix.

What it supports, and what it does not

  • Adds only. The tool adds watermarks. It does not remove or edit watermarks that are already in a PDF, and it should not be used to strip someone’s copyright or confidentiality marks.
  • Fonts. Text uses the standard PDF fonts, which cover Latin letters, numbers and common symbols. If you type characters they cannot hold, such as Urdu, Arabic or Chinese, the tool says so and adds the text as a picture instead. It looks the same but is not selectable.
  • Protected PDFs. Password-protected or encrypted files cannot be opened here.
  • Size. Files up to 300 MB are accepted, though your device’s memory is the practical limit.
  • Not security. A watermark is a visible mark, not encryption. Someone with PDF editing software may be able to remove it, so do not rely on it to keep confidential content safe.
  • Page features. The original page content, size and rotation are kept. Pages with cropping or rotation settings are handled so the mark appears where you see it in the preview.

Privacy: what happens to your PDF

The watermark is added inside your browser tab with the open-source pdf-lib library, and the preview is drawn with Mozilla’s pdf.js. Both are downloaded from a public code library when you choose a file. Your document and logo are not part of those requests, and this tool has no upload code.

Analytics receives only the name of an action, such as “download”. File names, text, logos and page counts are never sent. Like the rest of the site, the page loads Google Analytics and Google advertising scripts; the Privacy Policy explains how they are used.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add a watermark to a PDF for free?▾

Choose your PDF, type the watermark text or choose a logo image, adjust the opacity, rotation and position while watching the preview, and press Add watermark. Then download the new PDF. It is free, with no account and no extra mark added by this site.

Can I add text watermarks to PDF files?▾

Yes. Type any text, such as CONFIDENTIAL or DRAFT, or use one of the quick buttons. You can choose Helvetica, Times or Courier, set the size and colour, and make it bold or italic. Text in the standard fonts stays selectable in the saved PDF.

Can I add my logo as a PDF watermark?▾

Yes. Choose a PNG or JPG file. Transparent PNG logos keep their transparency, and you can set the logo size as a percentage of the page width, its opacity, rotation and position.

Can I change watermark transparency?▾

Yes. The opacity slider runs from 5% to 100%. A low value such as 20–40% keeps the page readable underneath, and the preview shows the effect straight away.

Can I rotate a PDF watermark?▾

Yes. Set any angle from −180° to 180° with the slider, or use the 0°, 45°, −45° and 90° buttons. The popular diagonal look is 45°.

Can I watermark selected pages?▾

Yes. Choose All pages, Odd pages, Even pages, a range such as pages 3 to 8, specific pages such as 1, 4, 9, or All pages except the ones you list. The preview tells you whether the page you are looking at will be marked.

Can I add watermarks to all PDF pages?▾

Yes. All pages is the default. The same watermark is placed on every page, scaled to that page’s own size when you use an image, and the original page sizes are kept.

Can I add repeated watermarks?▾

Yes. Switch the layout to Repeated (tiled) and set the horizontal and vertical spacing. The tiles cover the whole page in a staggered pattern. For very dense settings the tool limits a page to about 600 tiles so the file stays manageable.

Can I use this PDF watermark tool offline?▾

Not completely. Your PDF is processed on your own device and is never uploaded, but the page needs an internet connection the first time to download the open-source PDF libraries it uses. Once loaded, the processing itself does not use the network.

Can I edit or remove an existing watermark?▾

No. This tool only adds watermarks. It cannot edit or remove one that is already in a PDF, and you should not remove ownership, copyright or confidentiality marks without permission. To change a watermark you added, start again from your original, unwatermarked file and add a new one.

Is a watermark enough to protect a PDF?▾

No. A watermark discourages casual copying and makes the source of a document clear, but anyone with PDF editing software may be able to remove it. It is not encryption or access control, so do not rely on it to keep confidential information secret.
