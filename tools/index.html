About These 53 Calculators

Every calculator on this page is free to use and runs entirely in your browser — no signup, no data collection, no server-side processing. Each tool shows the formula it uses, validates your input, and displays step-by-step working so you can verify the result and learn the method.

The collection spans five categories: everyday mathematics (scientific calculator, percentage, fractions, decimals, ratios, proportions, averages, LCM/GCF, exponents, roots), algebra and geometry (quadratic equations, linear equations, simultaneous systems, slopes, distances, midpoints, triangles, areas, circles, Pythagorean theorem), academic tools (GPA, CGPA, final grade, weighted grades, exam percentage, required score, grade conversion, study planning, attendance, semester planning, age calculator, BISE form picture resizer, and load tax & package calculator), physics and science (force, speed, acceleration, density, pressure, work, power, kinetic energy, Ohm's law, and a multi-formula physics calculator), and conversions and statistics (units, length, weight, temperature, area, volume, speed, time, mean/median/mode, standard deviation).

Recent additions include the Age Calculator to help verify exact eligibility dates, the Picture Resizer for BISE Forms which compresses photos to 130×130 px JPG under 20 KB for BISE Gujranwala and Punjab board registrations, and the Load Tax & Package Calculator to compute 13.04% recharge tax deductions and exact balance reloads for Jazz, Telenor, Zong, and Ufone packages.

These tools are designed for students, teachers, parents, and professionals — especially those preparing for BISE Punjab board exams, Federal Board exams, or international curriculum programs. They pair well with Hira Science Academy's free Class 9 notes and Class 10 notes.
