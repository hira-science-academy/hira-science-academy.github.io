Free Online Calculators with Step-by-Step Solutions

Every calculator on this page is free to use and runs in your browser. There is no signup, and the numbers you type are not stored or tracked. Each tool shows the formula it uses, checks your input for mistakes, and displays the working so you can verify the result and learn the method.

What you can calculate

The collection covers everyday maths (scientific calculator, percentage, fractions, decimals, ratios, LCM and GCF, exponents and roots), algebra and geometry (including the quadratic equation solver with steps, linear and simultaneous equations, slope, distance, midpoint, triangles, circles and the Pythagorean theorem), GPA and grade tools (GPA, CGPA, final grade, weighted grade, required exam score and attendance), physics formulas (force, speed, acceleration, density, pressure, work, power, kinetic energy and Ohm's law) and unit converters and statistics (length, weight, temperature, area, volume, speed, mean, median, mode and standard deviation).

Who these calculators are for

These tools suit students, teachers, parents and professionals. Use them to check homework in algebra or physics, plan your GPA for a US college semester, convert between metric, imperial and US customary units, or revise for GCSE, A-level, AP, SAT and BISE Punjab board exams. Because every answer comes with its steps, they work as a learning aid and not just an answer machine.

Made by teachers

New to the collection? Start with the Age Calculator, which gives your exact age in years, months and days. Students in Pakistan can also use the BISE form picture resizer and the mobile load tax calculator.

Frequently asked questions

Are these online calculators really free?

Yes. Every calculator is free to use with no signup, no account and no download. They run in your web browser on a phone, tablet or computer.

Do the calculators show step-by-step working?

Yes. The maths, algebra, geometry and physics calculators show the formula and the steps used, so you can check the method and learn from it instead of just copying an answer.

Is the data I enter stored or sent anywhere?

No. Calculations run in your browser and the values you type are not stored or tracked. Like most websites, we use standard analytics to count page visits.

Can I use the GPA calculator for a 4.0 scale?

Yes. The GPA Calculator lets you set the grading scale, grades and credit hours, so you can use the 4.0 scale common in US colleges or adapt it to another grading system. The CGPA Calculator then combines previous credits with new results.

What is the difference between mean, median and mode?

The mean is the average of all values, the median is the middle value when the data is sorted, and the mode is the value that appears most often. The Mean, Median & Mode Calculator finds all three for any list of numbers.

Which physics formulas are covered?

The physics calculators cover force (F = ma), speed, distance and time, acceleration, density, pressure, work, power, kinetic energy and Ohm's law, plus a multi-formula calculator for mechanics, energy and electricity.
