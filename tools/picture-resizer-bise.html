📋 Common BISE Photo Requirements

  • Format: JPG / JPEG
  • Dimensions: 130 × 130 pixels is commonly requested for many Punjab Board forms
  • File size: 20 KB or less is common for standard registration; correction portals may allow more

How to Resize a Photo for BISE Registration

  1. Choose your photo. Click the upload box or drag and drop a clear, front-facing photo with a plain white or light background.
  2. Select 130×130 or enter custom dimensions. Use the BISE preset, or type the exact width and height from your board portal.
  3. Set the target file size. For a 20 KB limit, leave the default value or enter the exact number from your portal.
  4. Adjust JPEG quality if necessary. The tool will lower quality automatically to meet the target, but you can start lower if you prefer.
  5. Process the photo. Click Process & Download. The tool resizes and compresses the image in your browser.
  6. Download the JPG. Check the output dimensions and file size shown in the result panel.
  7. Check your board’s current requirements before uploading. Requirements can change. Confirm the exact dimensions, format, and file size on your portal.

Common BISE Photo Requirements (and Why They Vary)

Many BISE registration portals commonly ask for a 130×130 pixel JPG under 20 KB. This is a widely observed requirement, not a universal rule. Some boards or correction portals may allow a larger file, a different dimension, or a different background. FBISE requirements can differ as well.

The tool supports custom dimensions and file-size targets, so it can prepare a photo for BISE Lahore, Gujranwala, Multan, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Sargodha, DG Khan, Bahawalpur, Sahiwal, or another form once you know the exact requirement. Do not guess. Check the portal.

Why a Photo May Be Rejected

  • Wrong file size: The portal may have a maximum, and sometimes a minimum.
  • Wrong dimensions: A 130×130 requirement means exactly that, not 129×131.
  • Wrong format: JPG is commonly required; PNG may be rejected.
  • Background not accepted: Some portals require white or light blue.
  • Face not recognizable: Over-compression can make the photo too blurry.
  • Photo not square: A stretched or cropped face may fail verification.

Troubleshooting Common Problems

My image is still over 20 KB.

Confirm the target file size is set to 20 KB. Lower the JPEG quality slider. If the tool stops at 6 KB and the file is still over 20 KB, the original image may be too large or detailed for that target without becoming illegible. Crop closer to the face and try again.

The downloaded image is blurry.

Start with a sharper original. Do not compress below the 6 KB floor. If the portal allows a slightly larger file, increase the target size and keep more quality.

My photo looks stretched.

The tool scales the entire image to the dimensions you enter. If the original is not square, crop it to a square before uploading.

The board portal rejects my image.

Read the exact error message. It may be about dimensions, format, background, or file size. This tool can fix dimensions and file size. Background may need to be edited elsewhere.

My PNG isn’t accepted.

The tool outputs JPG. Upload your PNG here and process it to get a JPG for the portal.

The tool does not work on my phone.

Ensure the photo is under 20 MB and your browser is up to date. Try Chrome or Safari. The tool runs entirely in the browser and does not require an app.

Privacy: Your Photo Stays in Your Browser

The tool uses the HTML5 Canvas API to resize and compress the image on your device. The JavaScript does not send image data to any server. Your photo is not uploaded to Hira Science Academy or any third party. This is a browser feature, not a guarantee of absolute anonymity; your device and browser still handle the file.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I resize a photo for BISE registration?▾

Upload your photo, select the BISE 130×130 preset or enter the exact dimensions from your board portal, set a target file size if needed, and click Process & Download. Always check your board’s current portal for the exact requirement before submitting.

What size should a BISE registration photo be?▾

Many BISE registration forms commonly ask for a 130×130 pixel JPG under 20 KB. However, requirements can vary by board and registration cycle. Always follow the exact dimensions and file size shown on your own board’s portal.

How do I reduce a photo below 20 KB?▾

Set the target file size to 20 KB. The tool will automatically lower JPEG quality in steps until the file is under 20 KB or reaches the 6 KB floor, whichever comes first. The 6 KB floor prevents the face from becoming unrecognizable.

Is my photo uploaded to a server?▾

No. The tool uses the HTML5 Canvas API to process the image in your browser. Image data is not sent to Hira Science Academy or any third party. Your device and browser still handle the file, so normal browser privacy practices apply.

Can I use this tool on Android or iPhone?▾

Yes. The tool runs in a web browser and works on modern Android and iPhone devices. On iPhone, the download may open the image in a new tab; you can long-press to save it. Ensure your photo is under 20 MB and your browser is up to date.

What should I do if my photo is still over 20 KB?▾

Lower the JPEG quality slider or confirm the target file size is set to 20 KB. The tool will reduce quality in steps. If it stops at 6 KB and the file is still over 20 KB, the original image may be too large or detailed for that target without becoming illegible. Try cropping closer to the face before uploading.

What happens if my original photo is not square?▾

The tool scales the entire image to the dimensions you enter. If the original is not square, the result may be stretched. For best results, crop your photo to a square before uploading.

Can I use this tool for FBISE or other boards?▾

The tool supports custom dimensions and file-size targets, so you can use it for other boards and online forms if you know the exact requirement. FBISE and other boards may have different rules. Always verify on the official portal before submitting.
