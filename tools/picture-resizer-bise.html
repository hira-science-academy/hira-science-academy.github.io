  • Format: JPG / JPEG
  • Dimensions: Usually 130 × 130 pixels (square portrait)
  • File size: Generally 20 KB or less for standard registrations, up to 500 KB for correction portals

What this tool does

This tool resizes and compresses your photograph entirely inside your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API. Your image is never uploaded to any server — it never leaves your device. The output is a JPEG file with your chosen pixel dimensions and file size, ready to upload to BISE Gujranwala's registration portal or any other Punjab board's online form.

Why BISE forms have strict photo requirements

Board registration portals receive hundreds of thousands of photos each cycle. To keep their servers fast and reliable, they enforce strict limits on dimensions and file size. A photo that is too large will be rejected; one that is too small or too low-quality may fail verification. The standard for BISE Gujranwala Class 9 registration is 130×130 pixels JPG under 20 KB.

How to use this tool — step by step

  1. Click the upload box and choose your photo (or drag-and-drop it).
  2. Pick a preset, or enter custom width and height.
  3. Adjust the JPEG quality slider if needed.
  4. (Optional) Set a target file size in KB — the tool will automatically lower quality to meet it, but never below 6 KB.
  5. Click Process & Download to generate and save the resized JPG.

Tips for the best result

  • Use a well-lit, front-facing photo with a plain white or blue background.
  • Crop to the face — head and shoulders, centered, before uploading. The tool scales the whole image; it does not auto-crop.
  • Start with a JPG rather than a screenshot or a PNG — JPG compresses better.
  • Don't over-compress. If the file is under 20 KB but the face is blurry, the board may reject it. Aim for the largest quality that still fits under the size limit. The tool enforces a 6 KB floor for this reason.
  • Verify on your board's portal — some boards accept up to 50 KB or require 130×130, while correction portals may allow 500 KB.

FAQ

Is my photo uploaded to a server?▾

No. All processing happens inside your browser using the Canvas API. Your image never leaves your device and is never sent to Hira Science Academy or any third party.

What format should I upload?▾

JPG or JPEG is best for BISE forms because the output must be JPG. PNG and WEBP also work as input; the tool converts everything to JPG on output.

What if the result is still over 20 KB?▾

Lower the JPEG quality slider (try 0.6 or 0.5). You can also set the 'Target file size' field to 20 KB — the tool will automatically reduce quality in steps to meet the target, while never going below 6 KB to keep the photo legible.

Will resizing distort my face?▾

The tool scales the entire image to your chosen dimensions. If your original photo is not square, the result will be stretched or compressed. For best results, crop your photo to a square before uploading.

Why is there a 6 KB minimum?▾

Below about 6 KB, a JPEG photo of a face becomes visibly blurry and blocky. Most board portals reject unrecognisable photos even if they meet the file size. The 6 KB floor prevents the tool from over-compressing your photo to meet a target that would ruin it.

Can I use this for other forms (NADRA, passport, etc.)?▾

Yes, but always verify the specific requirements of the form you are filling. Different authorities have different size and dimension rules. This tool is flexible — just enter the dimensions and target size you need.
