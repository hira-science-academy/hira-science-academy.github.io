What this unit converter does

This tool converts between common units of length, mass, area, volume, speed and time, covering metric, imperial and US customary systems. Choose a category, pick the "from" and "to" units and type a value. The answer updates as you type, and pressing Convert adds the working. The inch, foot, yard, mile, pound, ounce and US gallon are all defined exactly in terms of metric units, so conversions are precise. For temperature, use the separate temperature converter, because it needs an offset as well as a factor.

How the conversion works

General unit conversion
\[ \text{result} = \text{value} \times \frac{\text{SI size of the from-unit}}{\text{SI size of the to-unit}} \]

Each unit has a fixed size in the SI base unit of its category: meters for length, kilograms for mass, square meters for area, cubic meters for volume, meters per second for speed and seconds for time. To convert, the value is multiplied by the size of the "from" unit and divided by the size of the "to" unit. Going through the SI unit keeps every pair consistent.

Example: 10 meters to feet

  • \( 1 \text{ m} = 1 \text{ m} \), and \( 1 \text{ ft} = 0.3048 \text{ m} \) exactly.
  • \( \text{result} = 10 \times \frac{1}{0.3048} = \mathbf{32.8084 \text{ ft}} \).

Example: 72 km/h to m/s

  • \( 1 \text{ km/h} = \frac{1000 \text{ m}}{3600 \text{ s}} \approx 0.277778 \text{ m/s} \).
  • \( \text{result} = 72 \times 0.277778 = \mathbf{20 \text{ m/s}} \).

Worked examples

Length: 5 km to miles

  1. \( 1 \text{ km} = 1000 \text{ m} \) and \( 1 \text{ mile} = 1609.344 \text{ m} \).
  2. \( 5 \times \frac{1000}{1609.344} = \mathbf{3.10686 \text{ miles}} \).
  3. This is the distance of a 5K run, about 3.1 miles.

Length: 6 feet to meters

  1. \( 1 \text{ ft} = 0.3048 \text{ m} \) exactly.
  2. \( 6 \times 0.3048 = \mathbf{1.8288 \text{ m}} \).
  3. A person 6 feet tall is about 1.83 meters.

Mass: 75 kg to pounds

  1. \( 1 \text{ lb} = 0.45359237 \text{ kg} \) exactly.
  2. \( 75 \div 0.45359237 = \mathbf{165.35 \text{ lb}} \).

Mass: 500 g to ounces

  1. \( 1 \text{ oz} = 28.349523 \text{ g} \).
  2. \( 500 \div 28.349523 = \mathbf{17.637 \text{ oz}} \), a little over a pound.

Volume: 2 liters to cubic centimeters

  1. \( 1 \text{ L} = 1000 \text{ cm}^3 \).
  2. \( 2 \times 1000 = \mathbf{2000 \text{ cm}^3} \), the size of a standard 2-liter bottle.

Speed: 100 km/h to m/s

  1. \( 1 \text{ km/h} = \frac{1000 \text{ m}}{3600 \text{ s}} = 0.277778 \text{ m/s} \).
  2. \( 100 \times 0.277778 = \mathbf{27.78 \text{ m/s}} \).

Time: 3 hours to minutes and seconds

  1. \( 1 \text{ h} = 60 \text{ min} = 3600 \text{ s} \).
  2. \( 3 \times 60 = \mathbf{180 \text{ minutes}} \) and \( 3 \times 3600 = \mathbf{10,800 \text{ seconds}} \).

Area: 1 acre to square meters and hectares

  1. \( 1 \text{ acre} = 4046.86 \text{ m}^2 \).
  2. \( 4046.86 \div 10000 = \mathbf{0.4047 \text{ ha}} \).
  3. So 1 hectare is about 2.47 acres.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Confusing mass and weight. The mass units here (kg, g, lb, oz) measure mass. Weight is a force in newtons, \( W = mg \), so divide a weight in newtons by about 9.8 to get mass in kilograms.
  • Squaring and cubing the factor. Because \( 1 \text{ m} = 100 \text{ cm} \), \( 1 \text{ m}^2 = 10,000 \text{ cm}^2 \) and \( 1 \text{ m}^3 = 1,000,000 \text{ cm}^3 \), not 100 or 1,000. The converter handles this, but it is a common error by hand.
  • Mixing up liters and cubic meters. \( 1 \text{ m}^3 = 1000 \text{ L} \), and \( 1 \text{ L} = 1000 \text{ mL} = 1000 \text{ cm}^3 \).
  • Using the wrong mile. A statute mile is 1609.344 m and a nautical mile is exactly 1852 m, about 15% longer. This converter uses the statute mile for "mi" and the nautical mile for knots.
  • Assuming US and UK gallons match. A US gallon is 3.785 L and an imperial gallon is 4.546 L, almost 20% more.
  • Treating time as decimal. 1.5 hours is 90 minutes, not 1 hour 50 minutes.
  • Converting twice. If you convert meters to feet and then multiply by 0.3048 again, you end up back near meters. Keep track of the direction, or use the swap button.

FAQ

What units does this converter support?▾

Six categories: length (mm, cm, m, km, in, ft, yd, mi), mass (mg, g, kg, metric tonne, oz, lb), area (cm², m², km², ft², yd², acre, hectare), volume (mL, cm³, L, m³, US gallon, ft³), speed (m/s, km/h, mph, knot, ft/s) and time (s, min, h, d, wk). Temperature has its own converter.

How do I convert meters to feet?▾

Divide the length in meters by 0.3048, or multiply by about 3.28084. For example, \( 10 \text{ m} = 10 \div 0.3048 \approx 32.81 \text{ ft} \). One foot is defined as exactly 0.3048 m.

How do I convert kilometers to miles?▾

Multiply kilometers by about 0.621371, or divide by 1.609344. For example, \( 5 \text{ km} \approx 3.11 \text{ miles} \). One statute mile is exactly 1,609.344 m.

How do I convert km/h to m/s?▾

Divide km/h by 3.6, or multiply by 5/18. For example, \( 72 \text{ km/h} = 72 \div 3.6 = 20 \text{ m/s} \). To go the other way, multiply m/s by 3.6.

How do I convert kilograms to pounds?▾

Divide kilograms by 0.45359237, or multiply by about 2.20462. For example, \( 75 \text{ kg} \approx 165.35 \text{ lb} \). One pound is defined as exactly 0.45359237 kg.

What is the difference between mass and weight?▾

Mass is the amount of matter in an object, measured in kilograms. Weight is the gravitational force on that mass, measured in newtons (\( W = mg \), with \( g \approx 9.8 \text{ m/s}^2 \) on Earth). The mass converter converts mass units only.

Is the gallon here a US gallon or a UK gallon?▾

It is the US gallon, which is 3.785 L. The UK imperial gallon is 4.546 L, about 20% larger, so fuel-economy and recipe conversions can differ between the US and UK.

Does the converter work for compound units?▾

It handles one category at a time. Compound units such as kg/m³ (density) or N·m (torque) combine categories, so convert each part separately or use a dedicated tool such as the density calculator.
