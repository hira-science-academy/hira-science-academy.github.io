Preparation Strategy & Tips

Effective strategies to maximize your score in the Physics examination

Focus Areas

  • Prioritize chapters with higher marks weightage (Ch 3, 4, 6)
  • Practice MCQs from all chapters, especially from entire textbook content
  • Master short answer questions from textbook exercises
  • Prepare detailed answers with clear diagrams where needed

Time Management

  • Allocate 15 minutes for MCQs (Part I)
  • Spend ~20 minutes on each short answer section (Part II)
  • Reserve ~25 minutes for each detailed question (Part III)
  • Aim to finish the paper with 5–10 minutes left for review
