Grade 9 Physics Pairing Scheme 2026

This official pairing scheme outlines the complete structure of the Grade 9 Physics paper for the Annual Examination 2026, including chapter-wise distribution of questions, marks allocation, and paper timing details.

Note: This pairing scheme applies only to the Annual Examination 2026. All questions will be asked from the textbook exercises as per the SMART syllabus.

Each detailed question will consist of two parts:

Preparation Strategy & Tips

Focus Areas

  • • Prioritize chapters with higher marks weightage
  • • Practice MCQs from all chapters, especially Ch 3, 4, 6
  • • Master short answer questions from textbook exercises
  • • Prepare detailed answers with clear diagrams where needed

Time Management

  • • Allocate 15 minutes for MCQs (Part I)
  • • Spend ~20 minutes on each short answer section (Part II)
  • • Reserve ~25 minutes for each detailed question (Part III)
  • • Keep 10 minutes for revision at the end
