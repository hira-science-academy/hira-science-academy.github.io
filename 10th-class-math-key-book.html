What is a Class 10 math key book?

A key book is a complete solution guide built around your textbook. It walks through every exercise question with full working, instead of only summarising theory. Notes tell you what a method is; a key book shows you how it is applied to the exact questions your teacher will set.

This Class 10 math key book follows the new 2026 edition based on the Revised National Curriculum 2023. It was prepared by Muhammad Tayyab, Subject Specialist Mathematics, Govt Christian High School Daska, and pairs solved exercises with real-world applications, so one page serves both daily practice and exam revision.

What you get: all 12 chapters, 43 solved exercises, a review exercise for every chapter and chapter-wise MCQs, free to read online with no sign-up.

How to use the key book to score better

  1. Attempt the exercise first.Try each question on paper without looking. Even a wrong attempt shows you which step you are unsure about.
  2. Compare every step, not just the answer.Open the matching exercise and check your method line by line. Board examiners award marks for working.
  3. Redo the questions you missed.Close the key book and solve those questions again from scratch until the method feels natural.
  4. Test yourself.Finish the chapter with its review exercise and its Class 10 maths MCQs to confirm you can answer without help.

Punjab Board Class 10 maths syllabus: what to know

  • The key book follows the new PECTAA syllabus for Class 10 Mathematics, based on the Revised National Curriculum 2023.
  • It has 12 units, from Complex Numbers to Probability. Complex Numbers and Vectors are among the newer topics.
  • All nine BISE boards in Punjab (Lahore, Gujranwala, Multan, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Sargodha, DG Khan, Bahawalpur and Sahiwal) follow the same syllabus.
  • See the Class 10 maths smart syllabus and the pairing scheme to plan your revision, and read the full unit-by-unit overview in Class 10 Mathematics Notes 2026.

Class 10 math key book: frequently asked questions

What is a math key book?

A key book is a complete solution guide that solves every textbook exercise step by step, so students can verify their own working against a trusted answer.

Does this key book cover all 12 chapters?

Yes, it covers all 12 chapters of the new 2026 syllabus, from Complex Numbers through Probability.

Is this key book based on PECTAA 2026?

Yes, it follows the updated PECTAA 2026 syllabus, based on the Revised National Curriculum 2023.

How is a key book different from regular notes?

Notes summarize concepts and theory, while a key book focuses on solving every exercise question with full working.

Which BISE boards can use this key book?

All 9 BISE boards in Punjab follow PECTAA 2026, so this key book applies equally to every board.

Is the key book free?

Yes, the complete key book with solved exercises for all 12 chapters is free to access online.

Can I use it without a tutor?

Yes, the step-by-step format is written to be followed independently, making it well-suited for self-study.

Is there a dedicated page for each chapter too?

Yes, each chapter also has its own dedicated page with a full set of resources, starting with Chapter 1 – Complex Numbers.

Which book is best for 10th maths?

For Punjab Board students the best base is the official Class 10 Mathematics textbook for the new PECTAA syllabus, because board papers are set from it. A key book is a companion, not a replacement: attempt each exercise yourself, then check your working against the solution.

Where can I find notes for Class 10 math?

Chapter-wise notes, definitions, formulas and MCQs are collected on the Class 10 Maths Notes page. This page is focused on solved exercises and textbook answers.

Is class 10 maths difficult?

Most students manage it with regular practice, but the new syllabus adds units such as Complex Numbers and Vectors that feel unfamiliar at first. Work through one exercise a day, compare with the key book, and revise the definitions before moving on.

Where can I find the solutions for Class 10 Maths?

Open a chapter on this page and pick the exercise you need. Each exercise page shows the full working, and every chapter also has a review exercise.

Is there a Class 10 math key book PDF?

The key book is free to read online, exercise by exercise, with no download needed. For printable chapter-wise notes, use the Class 10 maths notes PDF page.

Is this key book for Federal Board (FBISE) or NBF books?

No. It follows the Punjab Board PECTAA syllabus. Federal Board students should use solutions written for the FBISE or NBF textbook, because the syllabus and exercise numbering may differ.
