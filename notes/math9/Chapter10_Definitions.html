Chapter 10 Definitions - Graphs of Functions

This chapter focuses on understanding different types of functions and their graphical representations.

Chapter 10 introduces various types of functions and their graphical representations, including linear, quadratic, cubic, reciprocal, and exponential functions. Understanding these graphs helps visualize mathematical relationships.

Key Definitions Covered

  • Importance of graphs in mathematics
  • Definition of a function
  • Linear functions and their graphs
  • Quadratic functions and parabolas
  • Cubic functions and their characteristics
  • Reciprocal functions and asymptotes
  • Exponential functions
  • Gradient of a curve

Important Note:

Graphs help visualize mathematical relationships and make it easier to understand how variables interact with each other. Each type of function has a distinctive graph shape that reveals important properties of the function.
