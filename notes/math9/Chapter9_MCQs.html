📌 Formula Refresher

  • Similar figures: Corresponding angles are equal, corresponding sides are proportional.
  • Area ratio: \(\dfrac{A_1}{A_2} = \left(\dfrac{l_1}{l_2}\right)^2\).
  • Volume ratio: \(\dfrac{V_1}{V_2} = \left(\dfrac{l_1}{l_2}\right)^3\).
  • Exterior angle of regular n-gon: \(\dfrac{360^\circ}{n}\).
  • Interior angle of regular n-gon: \(\dfrac{180^\circ(n-2)}{n}\).
  • Number of diagonals: \(\dfrac{n(n-3)}{2}\).

📝 Multiple Choice Questions – Chapter 9

Q1 If two polygons are similar, then:

✅ Correct Answer: (A)
Similar polygons have corresponding angles equal and corresponding sides proportional.

Q2 The ratio of the areas of two similar polygons is:

✅ Correct Answer: (B)
The area ratio of similar polygons is the square of the ratio of their corresponding sides.

Q3 If the volume of two similar solids is \(125\text{ cm}^3\) and \(27\text{ cm}^3\), the ratio of their corresponding heights is:

✅ Correct Answer: (B)
\(\dfrac{V_1}{V_2} = \left(\dfrac{l_1}{l_2}\right)^3\)
\(\dfrac{125}{27} = \left(\dfrac{l_1}{l_2}\right)^3\)
\(\sqrt[3]{\dfrac{125}{27}} = \dfrac{l_1}{l_2} = \dfrac{5}{3}\)
Ratio of heights = \(5:3\)

Q4 The exterior angle of a regular pentagon is:

✅ Correct Answer: (D)
\(\text{Exterior angle} = \dfrac{360^\circ}{5} = 72^\circ\)

Q5 A parallelogram has an area of \(64\text{ cm}^2\) and a similar parallelogram has an area of \(144\text{ cm}^2\). If a side of the smaller parallelogram is \(8\text{ cm}\), the corresponding side of the larger parallelogram is:

✅ Correct Answer: (B)
\(\dfrac{A_1}{A_2} = \left(\dfrac{l_1}{l_2}\right)^2\)
\(\dfrac{64}{144} = \left(\dfrac{8}{l_2}\right)^2\)
\(\dfrac{8}{12} = \dfrac{8}{l_2} \Rightarrow l_2 = 12\text{ cm}\)

Q6 The total number of diagonals in a polygon with \(9\) sides is:

✅ Correct Answer: (D)
\(\text{Diagonals} = \dfrac{n(n-3)}{2} = \dfrac{9(9-3)}{2} = \dfrac{54}{2} = 27\)

Q7 Two spheres are similar, and their radii are in the ratio \(4:5\). If the surface area of the larger sphere is \(500\pi\text{ cm}^2\), what is the surface area of the smaller sphere?

✅ Correct Answer: (B)
\(\dfrac{A_1}{A_2} = \left(\dfrac{r_1}{r_2}\right)^2\)
\(\dfrac{A_1}{500\pi} = \left(\dfrac{4}{5}\right)^2 = \dfrac{16}{25}\)
\(A_1 = \dfrac{16 \times 500\pi}{25} = 320\pi\text{ cm}^2\)

Q8 A regular polygon has an exterior angle of \(30^\circ\). How many diagonals does the polygon have?

✅ Correct Answer: (A)
\(\text{Exterior angle} = \dfrac{360^\circ}{n} \Rightarrow 30^\circ = \dfrac{360^\circ}{n} \Rightarrow n = 12\)
\(\text{Diagonals} = \dfrac{12(12-3)}{2} = \dfrac{108}{2} = 54\)

Q9 In a regular hexagon, the ratio of the length of a diagonal to the side length is:

✅ Correct Answer: (B)
In a regular hexagon, the longest diagonal connects opposite vertices and is twice the side length, so the ratio is \(2:1\).

Q10 A regular polygon has an interior angle of \(165^\circ\). How many sides does it have?

✅ Correct Answer: (D)
\(\text{Exterior angle} = 180^\circ - 165^\circ = 15^\circ\)
\(15^\circ = \dfrac{360^\circ}{n} \Rightarrow n = \dfrac{360^\circ}{15^\circ} = 24\)

💡 Exam Tip:

In similar figures problems, identify whether you are dealing with lengths, areas, or volumes. Remember: area ratio is the square of the length ratio, and volume ratio is the cube. For polygon questions, know that the exterior angles always sum to \(360^\circ\), so the exterior angle of a regular n-gon is \(360^\circ/n\). The number of diagonals formula is \(n(n-3)/2\).
