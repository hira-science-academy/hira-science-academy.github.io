📌 Important Revision Points

  • Area ratio of similar figures: \(\dfrac{A_1}{A_2} = \left(\dfrac{l_1}{l_2}\right)^2\) where \(k = \dfrac{l_1}{l_2}\) is the scale factor.
  • Scale factor from areas: \(k = \sqrt{\dfrac{A_1}{A_2}}\).
  • Similar triangles: if \(\Delta ABC \sim \Delta DEF\) with scale factor \(k\), then \(\text{Area}(\Delta ABC) : \text{Area}(\Delta DEF) = k^2\).
  • Area of trapezium from similar triangles: \(\text{Area of trapezium} = \text{Area of larger triangle} - \text{Area of smaller triangle}\).
  • Regular polygons: all regular polygons with the same number of sides are similar.
  • Units: area ratio is dimensionless; areas are expressed in square units (cm², m²).

📐 Exercise 9.2 – Questions & Solutions

Q1 Find the ratio of the areas of similar figures if the ratio of their corresponding lengths are:
(i) \(1 : 3\)   (ii) \(3 : 4\)   (iii) \(2 : 7\)   (iv) \(8 : 9\)   (v) \(6 : 5\)
Solution

By using the formula for the area of similar figures:

\(\dfrac{A_1}{A_2} = \left(\dfrac{l_1}{l_2}\right)^2\)

(i) \(l_1 : l_2 = 1 : 3\)

\(\dfrac{A_1}{A_2} = \left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right)^2 = \dfrac{1}{9}\)
\(A_1 : A_2 = 1 : 9\)

(ii) \(l_1 : l_2 = 3 : 4\)

\(\dfrac{A_1}{A_2} = \left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^2 = \dfrac{9}{16}\)
\(A_1 : A_2 = 9 : 16\)

(iii) \(l_1 : l_2 = 2 : 7\)

\(\dfrac{A_1}{A_2} = \left(\dfrac{2}{7}\right)^2 = \dfrac{4}{49}\)
\(A_1 : A_2 = 4 : 49\)

(iv) \(l_1 : l_2 = 8 : 9\)

\(\dfrac{A_1}{A_2} = \left(\dfrac{8}{9}\right)^2 = \dfrac{64}{81}\)
\(A_1 : A_2 = 64 : 81\)

(v) \(l_1 : l_2 = 6 : 5\)

\(\dfrac{A_1}{A_2} = \left(\dfrac{6}{5}\right)^2 = \dfrac{36}{25}\)
\(A_1 : A_2 = 36 : 25\)
✅ (i) \(1:9\)    (ii) \(9:16\)    (iii) \(4:49\)    (iv) \(64:81\)    (v) \(36:25\)
Q2 Find the unknowns in the following figures (similar figures).
Solution

(a)

From figures:

\(A_1 = 240\text{ cm}^2,\quad l_1 = 10\text{ cm},\quad l_2 = 6\text{ cm},\quad A_2 = ?\)
\(\dfrac{240}{A_2} = \left(\dfrac{10}{6}\right)^2 = \dfrac{100}{36}\)
\((240)(36) = (100)(A_2)\)
\(A_2 = \dfrac{8640}{100} = 86.4\text{ cm}^2\)

(b)

From figures:

\(A_1 = 60\text{ cm}^2,\quad l_1 = 15\text{ cm},\quad l_2 = 20\text{ cm},\quad A_2 = ?\)
\(\dfrac{60}{A_2} = \left(\dfrac{15}{20}\right)^2 = \dfrac{225}{400}\)
\((60)(400) = (225)(A_2)\)
\(A_2 = \dfrac{24000}{225} = 106.67\text{ cm}^2\)

(c)

From figures:

\(A_2 = 18\text{ cm}^2,\quad l_1 = 3.6\text{ cm},\quad l_2 = 5.76\text{ cm},\quad A_1 = ?\)
\(\dfrac{A_1}{18} = \left(\dfrac{3.6}{5.76}\right)^2 = \dfrac{12.96}{33.1776}\)
\(A_1 = \dfrac{12.96}{33.1776} \times 18 = 7.03\text{ cm}^2\)

(d)

From figures:

\(A_2 = 96\text{ cm}^2,\quad l_1 = 15\text{ cm},\quad l_2 = 12\text{ cm},\quad A_1 = ?\)
\(\dfrac{A_1}{96} = \left(\dfrac{15}{12}\right)^2 = \dfrac{225}{144}\)
\(A_1 = \dfrac{225}{144} \times 96 = 150\text{ cm}^2\)

(e)

From figures:

\(A_1 = 3.57\text{ cm}^2,\quad A_2 = 63\text{ cm}^2,\quad l_1 = 3\text{ cm},\quad l_2 = l = ?\)
\(\dfrac{3.57}{63} = \left(\dfrac{3}{l}\right)^2 = \dfrac{9}{l^2}\)
\((3.57)(l^2) = (9)(63) = 567\)
\(l^2 = \dfrac{567}{3.57} = 158.82\)
\(l = \sqrt{158.82} = 12.60\text{ cm}\)
✅ (a) \(A_2 = 86.4\text{ cm}^2\)    (b) \(A_2 = 106.67\text{ cm}^2\)    (c) \(A_1 = 7.03\text{ cm}^2\)    (d) \(A_1 = 150\text{ cm}^2\)    (e) \(l = 12.60\text{ cm}\)
Q3 Given that area of \(\Delta ABC = 36\text{ cm}^2\), \(m\overline{AB} = 6\text{ cm}\), \(m\overline{BD} = 4\text{ cm}\). Find (a) the area of \(\Delta ADE\), (b) the area of trapezium \(BCED\).
Solution

From the figure:

\(\text{Area of } \Delta ABC = A_1 = 36\text{ cm}^2\)
\(m\overline{AB} = l_1 = 6\text{ cm}\)
\(m\overline{AD} = m\overline{AB} + m\overline{BD} = l_2 = 6 + 4 = 10\text{ cm}\)

(a) Area of \(\Delta ADE\)

Since \(\Delta ABC \sim \Delta ADE\):

\(\dfrac{A_1}{A_2} = \left(\dfrac{l_1}{l_2}\right)^2\)
\(\dfrac{36}{A_2} = \left(\dfrac{6}{10}\right)^2 = \dfrac{36}{100}\)
\((36)(100) = (36)(A_2)\)
\(A_2 = \dfrac{3600}{36} = 100\text{ cm}^2\)

(b) Area of trapezium BCED

\(\text{Area of trapezium} = \text{Area of } \Delta ADE - \text{Area of } \Delta ABC\)
\(= 100 - 36 = 64\text{ cm}^2\)
✅ (a) Area of \(\Delta ADE = 100\text{ cm}^2\)    (b) Area of trapezium \(= 64\text{ cm}^2\)
Q4 Given that \(\Delta ABC \sim \Delta DEF\) with scale factor \(k = 3\). If the area of \(\Delta ABC\) is \(50\text{ cm}^2\), find the area of \(\Delta DEF\).
Solution

Given:

\(k = 3,\quad A_1 = 50\text{ cm}^2,\quad A_2 = ?\)

Since \(\Delta ABC \sim \Delta DEF\):

\(\dfrac{A_1}{A_2} = k^2\)
\(\dfrac{50}{A_2} = 3^2 = 9\)
\(A_2 = \dfrac{50}{9} = 5\tfrac{5}{9}\text{ cm}^2\)
✅ Area of \(\Delta DEF = 5\tfrac{5}{9}\text{ cm}^2\)
Q5 Quadrilaterals \(ABCD\) and \(EFGH\) are similar, with scale factor \(k = \dfrac{1}{4}\). If the area of \(ABCD\) is \(64\text{ cm}^2\), find the area of \(EFGH\).
Solution

Given:

\(k = \dfrac{1}{4},\quad A_1 = 64\text{ cm}^2,\quad A_2 = ?\)

Since \(ABCD \sim EFGH\):

\(\dfrac{A_1}{A_2} = k^2\)
\(\dfrac{64}{A_2} = \left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)^2 = \dfrac{1}{16}\)
\((64)(16) = (1)(A_2)\)
\(A_2 = 1024\text{ cm}^2\)
✅ Area of \(EFGH = 1024\text{ cm}^2\)
Q6 The areas of two similar triangles are \(16\text{ cm}^2\) and \(25\text{ cm}^2\). What is the ratio of a pair of corresponding sides?
Solution

Given:

\(A_1 = 16\text{ cm}^2,\quad A_2 = 25\text{ cm}^2\)

Let \(l_1, l_2\) be the corresponding sides. Since the triangles are similar:

\(\dfrac{A_1}{A_2} = \left(\dfrac{l_1}{l_2}\right)^2\)
\(\left(\dfrac{l_1}{l_2}\right)^2 = \dfrac{16}{25}\)
\(\dfrac{l_1}{l_2} = \sqrt{\dfrac{16}{25}} = \dfrac{4}{5}\)
\(l_1 : l_2 = 4 : 5\)
✅ Ratio of corresponding sides = \(4 : 5\)
Q7 The areas of two similar triangles are \(144\text{ cm}^2\) and \(81\text{ cm}^2\). If the base of the larger triangle is \(30\text{ cm}\), find the corresponding base of the smaller triangle.
Solution

Given:

\(A_1 = 144\text{ cm}^2,\quad A_2 = 81\text{ cm}^2,\quad l_1 = 30\text{ cm},\quad l_2 = ?\)

Since the triangles are similar:

\(\dfrac{A_1}{A_2} = \left(\dfrac{l_1}{l_2}\right)^2\)
\(\dfrac{144}{81} = \left(\dfrac{30}{l_2}\right)^2\)
\(\dfrac{12}{9} = \dfrac{30}{l_2}\)
\(12 \cdot l_2 = 30 \cdot 9 = 270\)
\(l_2 = \dfrac{270}{12} = 22.5\text{ cm}\)
✅ Base of smaller triangle = \(22.5\text{ cm}\)
Q8 A regular heptagon is inscribed in a larger regular heptagon and each side of the larger heptagon is 1.7 times the side of the smaller heptagon. If the area of the smaller heptagon is \(100\text{ cm}^2\), find the area of the larger heptagon.
Solution

Given:

\(l_1 = x,\quad l_2 = 1.7x,\quad A_1 = 100\text{ cm}^2,\quad A_2 = ?\)

Since the heptagons are similar:

\(\dfrac{A_1}{A_2} = \left(\dfrac{l_1}{l_2}\right)^2\)
\(\dfrac{100}{A_2} = \left(\dfrac{x}{1.7x}\right)^2 = \dfrac{1}{2.89}\)
\((100)(2.89) = (1)(A_2)\)
\(A_2 = 289\text{ cm}^2\)
✅ Area of larger heptagon = \(289\text{ cm}^2\)

💡 Exam Tip:

When the scale factor is given directly (like \(k = 3\) or \(k = \tfrac{1}{4}\)), use \(A_1/A_2 = k^2\). When you are given the two areas and need to find a side, take the square root of the area ratio — that gives you the length ratio. For trapezium problems, remember that the trapezium's area is the difference of the larger and smaller similar triangles, not their sum. Always state the units (cm²) in your final answer.

If two figures are similar with a scale factor \(k\) (the ratio of corresponding lengths), then the ratio of their areas is \(k^2\). The formula is \(\dfrac{A_1}{A_2} = \left(\dfrac{l_1}{l_2}\right)^2\).

First find the scale factor \(k\) by dividing the larger side length by the smaller side length. Then multiply the smaller area by \(k^2\) to get the larger area.
