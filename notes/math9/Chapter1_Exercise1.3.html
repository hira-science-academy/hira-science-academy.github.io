📐 Exercise 1.3 – Questions & Solutions

Q1 The sum of three consecutive integers is forty-two, find the three integers.
Solution

Let first integer \(= x\)

Second integer \(= x + 1\)

Third integer \(= x + 2\)

According to question:

\(x + x + 1 + x + 2 = 42\)
\(3x + 3 = 42\)
\(3x = 42 - 3\)
\(3x = 39\)
\(x = \frac{39}{3}\)
\(x = 13\)

Hence,

First integer = \(x = 13\)
Second integer = \(x + 1 = 13 + 1 = 14\)
Third integer = \(x + 2 = 13 + 2 = 15\)
✅ The three consecutive integers are 13, 14, and 15
Q2 The diagram shows right angled \(\Delta ABC\) in which the length of \(\overline{AC}\) is \((\sqrt{3} + \sqrt{5})\text{ cm}\). The area of \(\Delta ABC\) is \((1 + \sqrt{15})\text{ cm}^2\). Find the length of \(\overline{AB}\) in the form of \((a\sqrt{3} + b\sqrt{5})\text{ cm}\), where \(a\) and \(b\) are integers.
Solution
\(\overline{AC} = (\sqrt{3} + \sqrt{5})\text{ cm}\)
Area of \(\Delta ABC = (1 + \sqrt{15})\text{ cm}^2\)
\(\overline{AB} = ?\)

Area of \(\Delta ABC = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height}\)

\((1 + \sqrt{15}) = \frac{1}{2} \times (\sqrt{3} + \sqrt{5}) \times AB\)
\(\frac{2(1 + \sqrt{15})}{\sqrt{3} + \sqrt{5}} = \overline{AB}\)
\(\overline{AB} = \frac{2(1 + \sqrt{15})}{\sqrt{3} + \sqrt{5}}\)
\(\overline{AB} = \frac{2 + 2\sqrt{15}}{\sqrt{3} + \sqrt{5}} \times \frac{\sqrt{3} - \sqrt{5}}{\sqrt{3} - \sqrt{5}}\)
\(\overline{AB} = \frac{2(\sqrt{3} - \sqrt{5}) + 2\sqrt{15}(\sqrt{3} - \sqrt{5})}{(\sqrt{3})^2 - (\sqrt{5})^2}\)
\(\overline{AB} = \frac{2\sqrt{3} - 2\sqrt{5} + 2\sqrt{45} - 2\sqrt{75}}{3 - 5}\)
\(\overline{AB} = \frac{2\sqrt{3} - 2\sqrt{5} + 2\sqrt{9\cdot 5} - 2\sqrt{25\cdot 3}}{-2}\)
\(\overline{AB} = \frac{2\sqrt{3} - 2\sqrt{5} + 2(3)\sqrt{5} - 2(5)\sqrt{3}}{-2}\)
\(\overline{AB} = \frac{2\sqrt{3} - 2\sqrt{5} + 6\sqrt{5} - 10\sqrt{3}}{-2}\)
\(\overline{AB} = \frac{-8\sqrt{3} + 4\sqrt{5}}{-2}\)
\(\overline{AB} = \frac{-8\sqrt{3}}{-2} + \frac{4\sqrt{5}}{-2}\)
\(\overline{AB} = 4\sqrt{3} - 2\sqrt{5}\)
✅ \(\overline{AB} = (4\sqrt{3} - 2\sqrt{5})\text{ cm}\)
Q3 A rectangle has sides of length \((2 + \sqrt{18})\text{ m}\) and \((5 - \frac{4}{\sqrt{2}})\text{ m}\). Express the area of rectangle in the form \(a + b\sqrt{2}\), where \(a\) and \(b\) are integers.
Solution
Length of rectangle = \(l = 2 + \sqrt{18}\text{ m}\)
\(l = 2 + \sqrt{9 \cdot 2}\text{ m}\)
\(l = 2 + 3\sqrt{2}\text{ m}\)
Breadth = \(b = 5 - \frac{4}{\sqrt{2}}\text{ m}\)
\(b = 5 - \frac{4}{\sqrt{2}} \times \frac{\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{2}}\text{ m}\)
\(b = 5 - \frac{4\sqrt{2}}{(\sqrt{2})^2}\text{ m}\)
\(b = 5 - \frac{4\sqrt{2}}{2}\text{ m}\)
\(b = 5 - 2\sqrt{2}\text{ m}\)
Area of rectangle = \(l \times b\)
\(= (2 + 3\sqrt{2}) \times (5 - 2\sqrt{2})\)
\(= 2(5 - 2\sqrt{2}) + 3\sqrt{2}(5 - 2\sqrt{2})\)
\(= 10 - 4\sqrt{2} + 15\sqrt{2} - 6(\sqrt{2})^2\)
\(= 10 + 11\sqrt{2} - 6(2)\)
\(= 10 + 11\sqrt{2} - 12\)
\(= -2 + 11\sqrt{2}\)
\(= (11\sqrt{2} - 2)\text{ m}^2\)
✅ Area \(= (11\sqrt{2} - 2)\text{ m}^2\)
Q4 Find two numbers whose sum is 68 and difference is 22.
Solution

Let first number = \(x\)

Second number = \(y\)

According to question:

\(x + y = 68 \quad \dots (i)\)
\(x - y = 22\)
\(x = 22 + y \quad \dots (ii)\)

Putting value of \(x\) in equation \((i)\):

\(22 + y + y = 68\)
\(2y = 68 - 22\)
\(2y = 46\)
\(y = \frac{46}{2}\)
\(y = 23\)

Putting value of \(y\) in equation \((ii)\):

\(x = 22 + 23\)
\(x = 45\)
✅ First number = 45, Second number = 23
Q5 The weather in Lahore was usually warm during the summer of 2024. The TV news reported temperature as high as \(48^\circ\text{C}\). By using the formula, \(^\circ\text{F} = \frac{9}{5}^\circ\text{C} + 32\), find the temperature as Fahrenheit scale.
Solution
\(^\circ\text{F} = \frac{9}{5}(48) + 32\)
\(^\circ\text{F} = 9 \times 9.6 + 32\)
\(^\circ\text{F} = 86.4 + 32\)
\(^\circ\text{F} = 118.4\)
✅ Temperature \(= 118.4^\circ\text{F}\)
Q6 The sum of the ages of the father and son is 72 years. Six years ago, the father's age was 2 times the age of the son. What was son's age six years ago?
Solution

Let present age of father = \(x\) years

Present age of son = \(y\) years

\(x + y = 72 \quad \dots (i)\)

Six years ago:

Father's age = \(x - 6\)
Son's age = \(y - 6\)
\(x - 6 = 2(y - 6)\)
\(x - 6 = 2y - 12\)
\(x = 2y - 12 + 6\)
\(x = 2y - 6 \quad \dots (ii)\)

Putting value of \(x\) in equation \((i)\):

\(2y - 6 + y = 72\)
\(3y = 72 + 6\)
\(3y = 78\)
\(y = 26\)

Six years ago age of son:

\(= y - 6\)
\(= 26 - 6\)
\(= 20\) years
✅ Son's age six years ago = 20 years
Q7 Mirha bought a toy for Rs.1500 and sold for Rs.1520. What was her profit percentage?
Solution
\(CP = 1500\text{ Rs}\)
\(SP = 1520\text{ Rs}\)
Profit = \(SP - CP\)
Profit = \(1520 - 1500 = 20\text{ Rs}\)
Profit % = \(\frac{\text{Profit}}{CP} \times 100\%\)
\(= \frac{20}{1500} \times 100\%\)
\(= \frac{20}{15}\%\)
\(= 1.33\%\)
✅ Profit percentage \(= 1.33\%\)
Q8 The annual income of Tayyab is Rs. 9,60,000 while the exempted amount is Rs. 1,30,000. How much tax would he have to pay at the rate of \(0.75\%\)?
Solution
Annual Income = \(9,60,000\text{ Rs}\)
Exempted Amount = \(1,30,000\text{ Rs}\)
Taxable Income = \(9,60,000 - 1,30,000\)
\(= 8,30,000\text{ Rs}\)
Tax rate = \(0.75\%\)
Tax amount = \(0.75\% \times 8,30,000\)
\(= \frac{0.75}{100} \times 8,30,000\)
\(= 6,225\text{ Rs}\)
✅ Tax amount = Rs. 6,225
Q9 Find the compound markup on Rs. 3,75,000 for one year at the rate of \(14\%\) compounded annually.
Solution
Principal Amount = \(3,75,000\text{ Rs}\)
Time = \(1\text{ year}\)
Rate = \(14\%\)
Compound Markup = ?

Since the time is 1 year, the compound markup formula is effectively the same as simple interest for the first year:

Compound Markup = \(\frac{\text{Principal Amount} \times \text{Time} \times \text{Rate}}{100}\)
\(= \frac{375000 \times 1 \times 14}{100}\)
\(= 52,500\text{ Rs}\)
✅ Compound markup = Rs. 52,500

💡 Exam Tip:

For word problems, always define your variables clearly at the start. When dealing with geometry and radicals, ensure you rationalize the denominator correctly. For financial math (profit, tax, markup), double-check the percentage formulas and ensure units (Rs., %) are correctly applied. These solutions follow the exact PECTAA / Punjab Board textbook method.
