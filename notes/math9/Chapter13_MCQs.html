📌 Formula Refresher

  • Probability of an event: \(P(A) = \dfrac{n(A)}{n(S)}\).
  • Complementary rule: \(P(A') = 1 - P(A)\).
  • Range of probability: \(0 \leq P(A) \leq 1\).
  • Relative frequency: \(\text{R.F.} = \dfrac{f}{N}\).
  • Expected frequency: \(E = N \times P(A)\).
  • Single die: \(n(S) = 6\).
  • Pair of dice: \(n(S) = 36\).
  • Four dice: \(n(S) = 6^4 = 1296\).
  • Deck of cards: \(n(S) = 52\); 4 suits of 13; 26 red and 26 black.
  • Three coins: \(n(S) = 2^3 = 8\).

📝 Multiple Choice Questions – Chapter 13

Q1 When a single fair die is rolled, the sample space has how many outcomes?

✅ Correct Answer: (B) 6
A standard die has six faces numbered 1 through 6, so \(S = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6\}\) and \(n(S) = 6\).

Q2 When a pair of fair dice is thrown, the size of the sample space \(n(S)\) is:

✅ Correct Answer: (C) 36
Each die has 6 outcomes and the two dice are independent, so \(n(S) = 6 \times 6 = 36\).

Q3 When four dice are thrown, the sample space \(n(S)\) equals:

✅ Correct Answer: (C) 1296
Each die has 6 outcomes, and four dice are independent, so \(n(S) = 6^4 = 1296\). (Note: \(6^6 = 46656\) is for six dice, not four.)

Q4 When two dice are rolled together, the probability of getting a double 2 (i.e. \(2\) on both dice) is:

✅ Correct Answer: (C) 1/36
Only one pair \((2, 2)\) in the 36 pairs gives a double 2, so \(P = 1/36\).

Q5 A card is drawn from a well-shuffled pack of 52. What is the probability of getting a Jack or a King?

✅ Correct Answer: (B) 8/52 = 2/13
There are 4 jacks and 4 kings, so \(n(A) = 8\) favorable outcomes out of 52 cards: \(P = 8/52 = 2/13\). Option C (\(44/52\)) is the probability of getting neither a jack nor a king, which is the complementary event.

Q6 The probability of getting a head when a fair coin is tossed is:

✅ Correct Answer: (B) 1/2
A fair coin has two equally likely outcomes: \(\{H, T\}\), so \(P(\text{head}) = 1/2\).

Q7 If \(P(A) = 0.65\), then \(P(A')\) is:

✅ Correct Answer: (B) 0.35
By the complementary rule, \(P(A') = 1 - P(A) = 1 - 0.65 = 0.35\).

Q8 If a fair coin is tossed three times, the number of possible outcomes in the sample space is:

✅ Correct Answer: (C) 8
Each toss has 2 outcomes and the three tosses are independent, so \(n(S) = 2^3 = 8\): \(S = \{HHH, HHT, HTH, THH, HTT, THT, TTH, TTT\}\).

Q9 The relative frequency of an event is defined as:

✅ Correct Answer: (B) frequency of event ÷ total frequency
Relative frequency = \(f/N\) where \(f\) is the frequency of the specific event and \(N\) is the total number of trials. Option A is the formula for expected frequency, and option C is the complementary probability formula.

Q10 The expected frequency of an event is calculated by:

✅ Correct Answer: (A) \(N \times P(A)\)
Expected frequency \(E = N \times P(A)\), where \(N\) is the total number of trials and \(P(A)\) is the probability of the event. Options B and D are the formulas for probability and relative frequency respectively; option C is the complementary probability formula.

💡 Exam Tip:

Before computing a probability, first state \(n(S)\): \(6\) for one die, \(36\) for two dice, \(1296\) for four dice, \(52\) for a deck of cards, \(8\) for three coin tosses. Most incorrect options in probability MCQs are distractors based on the wrong sample space (e.g. \(4/52\) instead of \(8/52\) for jacks + kings). When you see "at least" or "not", think complementary — \(P(A') = 1 - P(A)\) is almost always faster than enumerating.
