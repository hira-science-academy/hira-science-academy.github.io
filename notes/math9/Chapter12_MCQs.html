📌 Formula Refresher

  • Mean (ungrouped): \(\bar{X} = \dfrac{\sum X}{n}\).
  • Mean (grouped): \(\bar{X} = \dfrac{\sum fX}{\sum f}\).
  • Median (ungrouped, even \(n\)): average of the two middle values after ordering.
  • Median (grouped): \(\tilde{X} = \ell + \dfrac{h}{f}\left(\dfrac{n}{2} - c\right)\).
  • Mode: most frequent value (ungrouped); \(\hat{X} = \ell + \dfrac{f_m - f_1}{(f_m - f_1)+(f_m - f_2)}\times h\) (grouped).
  • Weighted mean: \(\bar{X}_w = \dfrac{\sum WX}{\sum W}\).
  • Class boundary: lower limit − 0.5 and upper limit + 0.5.

📝 Multiple Choice Questions – Chapter 12

Q1 Which of the following is an example of continuous data?

✅ Correct Answer: (C) The mass of a student measured as 28.5 kg
Continuous data can take any value within an interval, uses decimals and is obtained by measuring. The other three options are whole-number counts, which are discrete data.

Q2 The size of the class interval 20 – 24 (inclusive classes) is:

✅ Correct Answer: (C) 5
For inclusive classes, \(h = \text{upper limit} - \text{lower limit} + 1 = 24 - 20 + 1 = 5\).

Q3 The class boundaries of the class 30 – 34 are:

✅ Correct Answer: (B) 29.5 and 34.5
Subtract \(0.5\) from the lower limit and add \(0.5\) to the upper limit: \(30 - 0.5 = 29.5\), \(34 + 0.5 = 34.5\). Boundaries close the gap between consecutive classes.

Q4 The median of 110, 120, 122, 124, 125, 127, 130 is:

✅ Correct Answer: (C) 124
There are 7 values (odd). The median is the \(\left(\frac{7+1}{2}\right)^{th} = 4^{th}\) value in the ordered list: \(110, 120, 122, \mathbf{124}, 125, 127, 130\). So the median is \(124\).

Q5 The arithmetic mean of 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 is:

✅ Correct Answer: (B) 8
\(\bar{X} = \dfrac{\sum X}{n} = \dfrac{4+6+8+10+12}{5} = \dfrac{40}{5} = 8\).

Q6 The mode of the data 3, 5, 5, 7, 9, 5, 11 is:

✅ Correct Answer: (B) 5
The value \(5\) appears three times; every other value appears once. Mode = the most frequent observation = \(5\).

Q7 The class mark (midpoint) of the class 16 – 20 is:

✅ Correct Answer: (B) 18
Class mark \(= \dfrac{\text{lower limit} + \text{upper limit}}{2} = \dfrac{16+20}{2} = 18\).

Q8 When classes have unequal widths, the heights of the histogram bars must represent:

✅ Correct Answer: (C) The adjusted frequency (frequency ÷ width)
When the widths are unequal, the raw frequency cannot be used as height, because then the area of each bar would not be proportional to the frequency. Plotting the adjusted frequency (frequency ÷ width) ensures the area under each bar equals the frequency.

Q9 A frequency polygon is drawn by plotting points at (class mark, frequency) and:

✅ Correct Answer: (B) Joining them with straight line segments
A frequency polygon is a closed geometric figure — the points are joined by straight line segments, and the polygon is closed by adding an empty class at each end and extending the line down to zero.

Q10 The weighted mean of marks where \(W\) and \(X\) are \((2, 60)\), \((3, 70)\) and \((5, 80)\) is:

✅ Correct Answer: (B) 73
\(\sum WX = 2(60) + 3(70) + 5(80) = 120 + 210 + 400 = 730\). \(\sum W = 2+3+5 = 10\). \(\bar{X}_w = \dfrac{730}{10} = 73\).

💡 Exam Tip:

In MCQs, always read the question first and try to answer it before looking at the options — that avoids being distracted by plausible-looking wrong answers. Pay special attention to whether the question is about raw frequency or adjusted frequency, and whether the data is grouped or ungrouped. For the median, always order the data first; for the mode, always count repetitions carefully.
