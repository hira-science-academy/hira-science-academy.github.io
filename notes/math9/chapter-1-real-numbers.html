⚡ Key Concepts – Real Numbers

Rational Numbers
\(\frac{p}{q}\)

Where \(p, q\) are integers and \(q \neq 0\). Decimal form is terminating or recurring.

Irrational Numbers
\(\neq \frac{p}{q}\)

Cannot be written as a fraction. Decimal form is non-terminating and non-recurring (e.g., \(\pi, e, \sqrt{2}\)).

Real Numbers
\(\mathbb{R} = \mathbb{Q} \cup \mathbb{Q}'\)

The union of rational and irrational numbers forms the set of real numbers.

Laws of Radicals
\(\sqrt[n]{ab} = \sqrt[n]{a} \cdot \sqrt[n]{b}\)

Fundamental rules for multiplying, dividing, and simplifying radical expressions.

Conjugate Surd
\((\sqrt{a} + \sqrt{b})(\sqrt{a} - \sqrt{b}) = a - b\)

Multiplying conjugate surds rationalizes the expression, removing the radical.

Distributive Property
\(a(b+c) = ab + ac\)

Multiplication distributes over addition and subtraction.

❓ Chapter 1 Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between rational and irrational numbers?

Rational numbers can be written as a fraction \(p/q\) (terminating or repeating decimals), while irrational numbers cannot be written as fractions and have non-terminating, non-repeating decimals (e.g., \(\pi\), \(\sqrt{2}\)).

Is 0 a rational number?

Yes, 0 is a rational number because it can be expressed as \(0/q\) where \(q\) is any non-zero integer (e.g., \(0/1\), \(0/2\)).

What is a surd?

A surd is an irrational radical with a rational radicand. For example, \(\sqrt{5}\) is a surd, but \(\sqrt{9}\) is not because it simplifies to the rational number 3.

How do I find rational numbers between two given numbers?

To find a rational number between \(a\) and \(b\), calculate their average: \((a + b)/2\). Repeat this process with the new number and one of the originals to find more.

What is the difference between a terminating and a recurring decimal?

A terminating decimal has a finite number of digits after the decimal point (e.g., 0.375). A recurring decimal has an infinitely repeating pattern of digits (e.g., 0.333... or 0.142857...).
