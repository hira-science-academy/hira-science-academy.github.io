📐 Exercise 1.2 – Questions & Solutions

Q1 Rationalize the denominator of the following: (i) \(\frac{13}{4+\sqrt{3}}\) (ii) \(\frac{\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{5}}{\sqrt{3}}\) (iii) \(\frac{\sqrt{2}-1}{\sqrt{5}}\)
Solution

(i)

\(\frac{13}{4+\sqrt{3}} = \frac{13}{4+\sqrt{3}} \times \frac{4-\sqrt{3}}{4-\sqrt{3}}\)
\(= \frac{13(4-\sqrt{3})}{(4)^2-(\sqrt{3})^2}\)
\(= \frac{13(4-\sqrt{3})}{16-3}\)
\(= \frac{13(4-\sqrt{3})}{13}\)
\(= 4-\sqrt{3}\)

(ii)

\(\frac{\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{5}}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{5}}{\sqrt{3}} \times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{\sqrt{3}}\)
\(= \frac{\sqrt{2}\cdot\sqrt{3} + \sqrt{5}\cdot\sqrt{3}}{(\sqrt{3})^2}\)
\(= \frac{\sqrt{6} + \sqrt{15}}{3}\)

(iii)

\(\frac{\sqrt{2}-1}{\sqrt{5}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}-1}{\sqrt{5}} \times \frac{\sqrt{5}}{\sqrt{5}}\)
Q2 Simplify the following: (i) \(\left(\frac{81}{16}\right)^{-\frac{3}{4}}\) (ii) \(\left(\frac{3}{4}\right)^{-2} \div \left(\frac{4}{9}\right)^3 \times \frac{16}{27}\) (iii) \((0.027)^{-\frac{1}{3}}\) (iv) \(\sqrt[7]{\frac{x^{14} \times y^{21} \times z^{35}}{y^{14} \times z^7}}\) (v) \(\frac{5(25)^{n+1} - 25(5)^{2n}}{5(5)^{2n+3} - (25)^{n+1}}\) (vi) \(\frac{(16)^{x+1} + 20(4^{2x})}{2^{x-3} \times 8^{x+2}}\) (vii) \((64)^{-\frac{2}{3}} \div (9)^{-\frac{3}{2}}\) (viii) \(\frac{3^n \times 9^{n+1}}{3^{n-1} \times 9^{n-1}}\) (ix) \(\frac{5^{n+3} - 6 \cdot 5^{n+1}}{9 \times 5^n - 4 \times 5^n}\)
Solution

(i) \(\left(\frac{81}{16}\right)^{-\frac{3}{4}}\)

\(\left(\frac{81}{16}\right)^{-\frac{3}{4}} = \left(\frac{16}{81}\right)^{\frac{3}{4}}\)
\(= \left(\frac{2^4}{3^4}\right)^{\frac{3}{4}}\)
\(= \frac{(2^4)^{\frac{3}{4}}}{(3^4)^{\frac{3}{4}}}\)
\(= \frac{2^3}{3^3} = \frac{8}{27}\)

(ii) \(\left(\frac{3}{4}\right)^{-2} \div \left(\frac{4}{9}\right)^3 \times \frac{16}{27}\)

\(\left(\frac{3}{4}\right)^{-2} \div \left(\frac{4}{9}\right)^3 \times \frac{16}{27}\)
\(= \left(\frac{4}{3}\right)^2 \div \left(\frac{4}{3^2}\right)^3 \times \frac{4^2}{3^3}\)
\(= \frac{4^2}{3^2} \div \frac{4^3}{(3^2)^3} \times \frac{4^2}{3^3}\)
\(= \frac{4^2}{3^2} \div \frac{4^3}{3^6} \times \frac{4^2}{3^3}\)
\(= \frac{4^2}{3^2} \times \frac{3^6}{4^3} \times \frac{4^2}{3^3}\)
\(= 4^{2+2-3} \times 3^{6-2-3}\)
\(= 4^1 \times 3^1 = 4 \times 3 = 12\)

(iii) \((0.027)^{-\frac{1}{3}}\)

\((0.027)^{-\frac{1}{3}} = \left(\frac{27}{1000}\right)^{-\frac{1}{3}}\)
\(= \left(\frac{1000}{27}\right)^{\frac{1}{3}}\)
\(= \left(\frac{10^3}{3^3}\right)^{\frac{1}{3}}\)
\(= \frac{10}{3}\)

(iv) \(\sqrt[7]{\frac{x^{14} \times y^{21} \times z^{35}}{y^{14} \times z^7}}\)

\(= \left(\frac{x^{14} \times y^{21} \times z^{35}}{y^{14} \times z^7}\right)^{\frac{1}{7}}\)
\(= (x^{14} \cdot y^{21-14} \cdot z^{35-7})^{\frac{1}{7}}\)
\(= (x^{14} \cdot y^7 \cdot z^{28})^{\frac{1}{7}}\)
\(= x^2 y z^4\)

(v) \(\frac{5(25)^{n+1} - 25(5)^{2n}}{5(5)^{2n+3} - (25)^{n+1}}\)

\(= \frac{5 \cdot (5^2)^{n+1} - 5^2 \cdot (5)^{2n}}{5 \cdot 5^{2n+3} - (5^2)^{n+1}}\)
\(= \frac{5 \cdot 5^{2n+2} - 5^2 \cdot 5^{2n}}{5 \cdot 5^{2n+3} - 5^{2n+2}}\)
\(= \frac{5^{2n+3} - 5^{2n+2}}{5^{2n+4} - 5^{2n+2}}\)
\(= \frac{5^{2n+2}(5-1)}{5^{2n+2}(5^2-1)}\)
\(= \frac{5-1}{25-1} = \frac{4}{24} = \frac{1}{6}\)

(vi) \(\frac{(16)^{x+1} + 20(4^{2x})}{2^{x-3} \times 8^{x+2}}\)

\(= \frac{(2^4)^{x+1} + 20(2^2)^{2x}}{2^{x-3} \times (2^3)^{x+2}}\)
\(= \frac{2^{4x+4} + 20 \cdot 2^{4x}}{2^{x-3} \times 2^{3x+6}}\)
\(= \frac{2^{4x}(2^4 + 20)}{2^{4x+3}}\)
\(= \frac{16 + 20}{2^3} = \frac{36}{8}\)

(vii) \((64)^{-\frac{2}{3}} \div (9)^{-\frac{3}{2}}\)

\((64)^{-\frac{2}{3}} \div (9)^{-\frac{3}{2}}\)
\(= (4^3)^{-\frac{2}{3}} \div (3^2)^{-\frac{3}{2}}\)
\(= 4^{-2} \div 3^{-3}\)
\(= \frac{1}{4^2} \div \frac{1}{3^3}\)
\(= \frac{1}{16} \times 27 = \frac{27}{16}\)

(viii) \(\frac{3^n \times 9^{n+1}}{3^{n-1} \times 9^{n-1}}\)

\(\frac{3^n \times (3^2)^{n+1}}{3^{n-1} \times (3^2)^{n-1}}\)
\(= \frac{3^n \cdot 3^{2n+2}}{3^{n-1} \cdot 3^{2n-2}}\)
\(= 3^{n+2n+2 - (n-1+2n-2)}\)
\(= 3^{3n+2 - (3n-3)}\)
\(= 3^5 = 243\)

(ix) \(\frac{5^{n+3} - 6 \cdot 5^{n+1}}{9 \times 5^n - 4 \times 5^n}\)

\(= \frac{5^{n+1}(5^2 - 6)}{5^n(9 - 4)}\)
\(= \frac{5^{n+1}(25 - 6)}{5^n(5)}\)
\(= \frac{5^{n+1}(19)}{5^{n+1}} = 19\)
Q3 If \(x = 3 + \sqrt{8}\) then find the values of (i) \(x + \frac{1}{x}\) (ii) \(x - \frac{1}{x}\) (iii) \(x^2 + \frac{1}{x^2}\) (iv) \(x^2 - \frac{1}{x^2}\) (v) \(x^4 + \frac{1}{x^4}\) (vi) \(\left(x - \frac{1}{x}\right)^2\)
Solution

Given \(x = 3 + \sqrt{8}\). First, find \(\frac{1}{x}\):

\(\frac{1}{x} = \frac{1}{3+\sqrt{8}} \times \frac{3-\sqrt{8}}{3-\sqrt{8}}\)
\(= \frac{3-\sqrt{8}}{(3)^2-(\sqrt{8})^2}\)
\(= \frac{3-\sqrt{8}}{9-8} = 3-\sqrt{8}\)

(i) \(x + \frac{1}{x} = ?\)

\(x + \frac{1}{x} = (3+\sqrt{8}) + (3-\sqrt{8})\)
\(= 3 + \sqrt{8} + 3 - \sqrt{8} = 6\)

(ii) \(x - \frac{1}{x} = ?\)

\(x - \frac{1}{x} = (3+\sqrt{8}) - (3-\sqrt{8})\)
\(= 3 + \sqrt{8} - 3 + \sqrt{8} = 2\sqrt{8}\)

(iii) \(x^2 + \frac{1}{x^2} = ?\)

As \(x + \frac{1}{x} = 6\)

Taking square on both sides:

\(\left(x + \frac{1}{x}\right)^2 = (6)^2\)
\((x)^2 + \left(\frac{1}{x}\right)^2 + 2(x)\left(\frac{1}{x}\right) = 36\)
\(x^2 + \frac{1}{x^2} + 2 = 36\)
\(x^2 + \frac{1}{x^2} = 36 - 2 = 34\)

(iv) \(x^2 - \frac{1}{x^2} = ?\)

\(x^2 - \frac{1}{x^2} = (x)^2 - \left(\frac{1}{x}\right)^2\)
\(= \left(x + \frac{1}{x}\right)\left(x - \frac{1}{x}\right)\)
\(= (6)(2\sqrt{8}) = 12\sqrt{8}\)

(v) \(x^4 + \frac{1}{x^4} = ?\)

As \(x^2 + \frac{1}{x^2} = 34\)

Taking square on both sides:

\(\left(x^2 + \frac{1}{x^2}\right)^2 = (34)^2\)
\((x^2)^2 + \left(\frac{1}{x^2}\right)^2 + 2(x^2)\left(\frac{1}{x^2}\right) = 1156\)
\(x^4 + \frac{1}{x^4} + 2 = 1156\)
\(x^4 + \frac{1}{x^4} = 1156 - 2 = 1154\)

(vi) \(\left(x - \frac{1}{x}\right)^2 = ?\)

As \(x - \frac{1}{x} = 2\sqrt{8}\)

Taking square on both sides:

\(\left(x - \frac{1}{x}\right)^2 = (2\sqrt{8})^2\)
\(= (2)^2 \cdot (\sqrt{8})^2\)
\(= 4 \cdot 8 = 32\)
Q4 Find the rational numbers \(p\) and \(q\) such that \(\frac{8-3\sqrt{2}}{4+3\sqrt{2}} = p + q\sqrt{2}\)
Solution
\(\frac{8-3\sqrt{2}}{4+3\sqrt{2}} = p + q\sqrt{2}\)
\(\frac{8-3\sqrt{2}}{4+3\sqrt{2}} \times \frac{4-3\sqrt{2}}{4-3\sqrt{2}} = p + q\sqrt{2}\)
\(\frac{8(4-3\sqrt{2}) - 3\sqrt{2}(4-3\sqrt{2})}{(4)^2 - (3\sqrt{2})^2} = p + q\sqrt{2}\)
\(\frac{32 - 24\sqrt{2} - 12\sqrt{2} + 9(\sqrt{2})^2}{16 - 9(\sqrt{2})^2} = p + q\sqrt{2}\)
\(\frac{32 - 36\sqrt{2} + 9(2)}{16 - 9(2)} = p + q\sqrt{2}\)
\(\frac{32 - 36\sqrt{2} + 18}{16 - 18} = p + q\sqrt{2}\)
\(\frac{50 - 36\sqrt{2}}{-2} = p + q\sqrt{2}\)
\(\frac{50}{-2} - \frac{36\sqrt{2}}{-2} = p + q\sqrt{2}\)
\(-25 + 18\sqrt{2} = p + q\sqrt{2}\)

By comparing we get,

\(p = -25\) and \(q = 18\)
Q5 Simplify the following: (i) \(\frac{(25)^{\frac{3}{2}} \times (243)^{\frac{3}{5}}}{(16)^{\frac{5}{4}} \times (8)^{\frac{4}{3}}}\) (ii) \(\frac{54 \times \sqrt[3]{(27)^{2x}}}{9^{x+1} + 216(3^{2x-1})}\) (iii) \(\sqrt{\frac{(216)^{\frac{2}{3}} \times (25)^{\frac{1}{2}}}{(0.04)^{-\frac{3}{2}}}}\) (iv) \((a^{\frac{1}{3}} + b^{\frac{2}{3}}) \times (a^{\frac{2}{3}} - a^{\frac{1}{3}}b^{\frac{2}{3}} + b^{\frac{4}{3}})\)
Solution

(i) \(\frac{(25)^{\frac{3}{2}} \times (243)^{\frac{3}{5}}}{(16)^{\frac{5}{4}} \times (8)^{\frac{4}{3}}}\)

\(= \frac{(5^2)^{\frac{3}{2}} \times (3^5)^{\frac{3}{5}}}{(2^4)^{\frac{5}{4}} \times (2^3)^{\frac{4}{3}}}\)
\(= \frac{5^3 \times 3^3}{2^5 \times 2^4}\)
\(= \frac{125 \times 27}{32 \times 16}\)
\(= \frac{3375}{512}\)

(ii) \(\frac{54 \times \sqrt[3]{(27)^{2x}}}{9^{x+1} + 216(3^{2x-1})}\)

\(= \frac{54 \times [(3^3)^{2x}]^{\frac{1}{3}}}{(3^2)^{x+1} + 216(3^{2x-1})}\)
\(= \frac{54 \times (3)^{2x}}{3^{2x+2} + 216(3^{2x-1})}\)
\(= \frac{54 \times 3^{2x}}{3^{2x}[3^2 + 216(3^{-1})]}\)
\(= \frac{54}{3^2 + \frac{216}{3}}\)
\(= \frac{54}{9 + 72} = \frac{54}{81}\)
\(= \frac{2}{3}\)

(iii) \(\sqrt{\frac{(216)^{\frac{2}{3}} \times (25)^{\frac{1}{2}}}{(0.04)^{-\frac{3}{2}}}}\)

\(= \sqrt{\frac{(216)^{\frac{2}{3}} \times (25)^{\frac{1}{2}}}{(0.04)^{-\frac{3}{2}}}}\)
\(= \sqrt{\frac{(6^3)^{\frac{2}{3}} \times (5^2)^{\frac{1}{2}}}{\left(\frac{4}{100}\right)^{-\frac{3}{2}}}}\)
\(= \sqrt{\frac{(6)^2 \times (5)^1}{\left(\frac{1}{25}\right)^{-\frac{3}{2}}}}\)
\(= \sqrt{\frac{6^2 \times 5}{(25)^{\frac{3}{2}}}}\)
\(= \sqrt{\frac{6^2 \times 5}{(5^2)^{\frac{3}{2}}}}\)
\(= \sqrt{\frac{6^2 \times 5}{5^3}}\)
\(= \sqrt{\frac{6^2}{5^{3-1}}}\)
\(= \sqrt{\frac{6^2}{5^2}}\)
\(= \frac{6}{5}\)

(iv) \((a^{\frac{1}{3}} + b^{\frac{2}{3}}) \times (a^{\frac{2}{3}} - a^{\frac{1}{3}}b^{\frac{2}{3}} + b^{\frac{4}{3}})\)

This is of the form \((x+y)(x^2 - xy + y^2) = x^3 + y^3\) where \(x = a^{\frac{1}{3}}\) and \(y = b^{\frac{2}{3}}\).

\(= (a^{\frac{1}{3}} + b^{\frac{2}{3}})\left[(a^{\frac{1}{3}})^2 - (a^{\frac{1}{3}})(b^{\frac{2}{3}}) + (b^{\frac{2}{3}})^2\right]\)
\(= (a^{\frac{1}{3}})^3 + (b^{\frac{2}{3}})^3\)
\(= a + b^2\)

💡 Exam Tip:

When rationalizing denominators with binomial surds, always multiply by the conjugate. For expressions with exponents, apply the laws of exponents step-by-step, converting all bases to prime factors if possible. These solutions follow the exact PECTAA / Punjab Board textbook method.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions – Exercise 1.2

Exercise 1.2 of Unit 1 (Real Numbers) covers: rationalizing denominators of surds (single and binomial), simplifying expressions with fractional and negative exponents, applying the laws of exponents to complex fractions, using the algebraic identity \((x+y)(x^2 - xy + y^2) = x^3 + y^3\), and evaluating expressions of the form \(x + \dfrac{1}{x}\) when \(x\) is of the type \(a + \sqrt{b}\). Full step-by-step working is shown on this page and in the embedded PDF.

Multiply both numerator and denominator by the conjugate of the denominator, \(4 - \sqrt{3}\). Then apply the difference-of-squares identity \((a+b)(a-b) = a^2 - b^2\) in the denominator so the surd disappears:

\(\dfrac{13}{4+\sqrt{3}} \times \dfrac{4-\sqrt{3}}{4-\sqrt{3}} = \dfrac{13(4-\sqrt{3})}{4^2 - (\sqrt{3})^2} = \dfrac{13(4-\sqrt{3})}{16-3} = 4-\sqrt{3}\)

Use the law \(a^{-m/n} = \dfrac{1}{a^{m/n}}\) to remove the negative exponent, express the base as a power of prime factors, then apply \((a^m)^n = a^{mn}\):

\(\left(\dfrac{81}{16}\right)^{-\frac{3}{4}} = \left(\dfrac{16}{81}\right)^{\frac{3}{4}} = \left(\dfrac{2^4}{3^4}\right)^{\frac{3}{4}} = \dfrac{2^3}{3^3} = \dfrac{8}{27}\)

First rationalize \(\dfrac{1}{x}\) by multiplying numerator and denominator by \(3 - \sqrt{8}\). Since \((3+\sqrt{8})(3-\sqrt{8}) = 9 - 8 = 1\), we get \(\dfrac{1}{x} = 3 - \sqrt{8}\). Then:

\(x + \dfrac{1}{x} = (3+\sqrt{8}) + (3-\sqrt{8}) = 6\)
\(x - \dfrac{1}{x} = (3+\sqrt{8}) - (3-\sqrt{8}) = 2\sqrt{8}\)

Using \(\left(x + \dfrac{1}{x}\right)^2 = x^2 + \dfrac{1}{x^2} + 2\), we get \(x^2 + \dfrac{1}{x^2} = 36 - 2 = 34\). Squaring again gives \(x^4 + \dfrac{1}{x^4} = 34^2 - 2 = 1154\). The same pair of values is used for \(x^2 - \dfrac{1}{x^2} = (x+\frac{1}{x})(x-\frac{1}{x}) = 12\sqrt{8}\) and \(\left(x-\frac{1}{x}\right)^2 = (2\sqrt{8})^2 = 32\).

Rationalize the left-hand side by multiplying the numerator and denominator by the conjugate \(4 - 3\sqrt{2}\). Expand the numerator and denominator using \((a+b)(a-b) = a^2 - b^2\):

\(\dfrac{(8-3\sqrt{2})(4-3\sqrt{2})}{4^2 - (3\sqrt{2})^2} = \dfrac{32 - 24\sqrt{2} - 12\sqrt{2} + 9(2)}{16 - 18}\)
\(= \dfrac{50 - 36\sqrt{2}}{-2} = -25 + 18\sqrt{2}\)

Comparing with \(p + q\sqrt{2}\) gives \(p = -25\) and \(q = 18\), because \(p\) and \(q\) are rational while \(\sqrt{2}\) is irrational.
